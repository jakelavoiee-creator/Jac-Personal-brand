# Aura tee photoshoot bible (Higgsfield), v1, 28 Oct 2026

**North star.** Quiet Miami luxury, with the clothes as proof. Editorial and calm, never loud. Every image must look like the product a customer will actually receive, because it is a product photo first and a mood second.

## 1. The garment: locked (paste this block into every prompt)
Sources: Tapstitch close-ups (fabric, hem, collar) and Tapstitch's on-body photos (fit).

> **Aura French Terry Raw Edge T-shirt.** Heavyweight cotton French terry: a smooth, matte flat-knit face with very fine vertical knit ribs visible up close, and a looped terry back that shows only where the hems curl. **Raw-cut hems** at the sleeves and the bottom, with no stitching line, rolling slightly outward to reveal the looped inner side. **Ribbed crew neck**, a dense 1×1 rib about 2.5 cm tall, sitting close around the base of the neck. **Boxy, cropped, oversized fit**: a wide square body that stands away from the torso, the hem landing at the top of the trouser waistband, **dropped shoulder seams** sitting well down the upper arm, and wide short sleeves ending just above the elbow. The fabric is dense and holds its shape: soft structured folds, never clingy, never shiny.

**Colour locks** (the tee comes in two colours only; the beige in the close-ups is just Tapstitch's sample colour):
- **Black:** deep matte jet black that absorbs light. Folds read as charcoal shadows, never shiny.
- **White:** clean optic white with neutral shadows, never cream or yellow.

## 4. The shot list: 4 images per colour per design (4:5, 1600×2000)
| # | Shot | Composition | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Back hero**, the PDP's first image | Model's back to camera, head turned over the shoulder; the print fills the **centre third**; framed from crown to mid-thigh; neutral warm-grey studio wall, soft key light from 45° | Sells the design (the back is the product) |
| 2 | **Front fit** | Full body facing camera, arms relaxed, feet shoulder-width; grey studio; the crop line at the waistband and the dropped shoulders clearly visible | Answers "how does it fit" |
| 3 | **Miami lifestyle** | Golden hour on a rooftop, a marble terrace, a dark luxury garage or a palm-lined driveway; the model fills the left two-thirds and the scene the right third; the print or logo is readable | Brand world; also the ad-ready image |
| 4 | **Detail** (its own shoot, Jac 28 Oct: never a crop or edit of the hero image) | Macro close-up: a raw-edge sleeve curling to show the terry loops, plus the print texture, at shallow depth of field | Proves quality and fabric |

**Lighting:** studio light for images 1–2. Golden hour (Ava) or harsh-sun / flash (Jack) for 3, natural daylight for 4. These map to lighting sheets 6, 2, 4, 7 and 1.

## 6. Pinterest references
Use them for **pose, framing and light only**. Don't recreate a specific photographer's image shot for shot, and never keep another person's face or another brand's clothing. That is a copyright risk and off-brand.

## 7. Honest-use rules (AI models)
- Jack and Ava are **brand faces / models**, which is fine.
- **They must never appear as customers.** No reviews, "customer photos" or testimonials using them. That is FTC deception and it's on our never-do list.
- **Fit and fabric must be truthful:** the same crop, weight and colour as the real tee. Misleading product images cause returns and chargebacks.
- **Ads:** when an ad uses photorealistic AI people, apply Meta's AI disclosure where Ads Manager asks for it.
- **Keep one real photo per product** (the Tapstitch on-body photo or a real try-on) to anchor trust.

## 8. QA checklist (every image, before upload)
- [ ] The print text is letter-perfect and placed as in §2
- [ ] Raw hems visible: no stitched hem line
- [ ] The crop ends at the waistband; drop shoulders; sleeves above the elbow
- [ ] The colour is true: jet black or optic white, not cream
- [ ] Same face and accessories as the character sheet; hands and fingers correct
- [ ] No third-party logos; nothing in the top 14% / bottom 35% if it's also used as a 9:16 ad
- [ ] Exported as a 4:5 JPG at 1600×2000, named `aura-miami-<design>-tee-<colour>-<shot>.jpg` (alt text is written at upload)

**28 Oct, PRINT PLACEMENT LOCK, Nine To Five back (measured on the white flat-lay mockup).**
- **Measurements:**
  - The top of "TOO CREATIVE" sits **45% of the way down** from collar to hem, just below the armpit line.
  - "(MEMBERS ONLY)" ends **66% down**, level with the raw sleeve edges.
  - Below the print to the hem is about a third of the shirt length.
  - The text block is about 70% of the body width, between the armpits.
- **Why:** the first white back render put it at 17–43%, too high. On a body the model drifts the print upward, so always state the armpit and sleeve-edge anchors.

**28 Oct, HAND RULE (Tristan, Created To Create white back).**
- **Problem:** "thumb hooked, fingers over the pocket" is vague, and the model merged the fingers into the pocket.
- **Write pocket poses precisely:**
  - The four fingers are hidden inside the pocket up to the knuckles.
  - Only the back of the hand (knuckles to wrist) shows above the pocket edge.
  - The thumb rests outside along the pocket edge.
  - The wrist is straight; the elbow is bent about 100° and points out, with a gap between arm and torso.
  - The pocket edge stays intact, with a slight bulge from the hand.
- **Fix:** an edit pass on the hand only, so a good print is never regenerated.
- **Fallback:** the arm hangs loose at the side.
