# Founder ad: "Too Creative For Nine To Five" (Q4 evergreen, swappable offer)

**North star:** a real owner, talking straight, about why the line exists. Quiet confidence, no hype. The offer is a single swappable line, so the same footage runs for Black Friday, Christmas and Boxing Day.

**Format:** 9:16, phone camera, ~40–45 s, word-by-word captions (`scripts/ad-edit/aedit.py`).
- **Safe zones:** keep face, product and captions out of the top 14% and bottom 35% of the frame.
- **Names:** `Founder-Video-<HookName>-<Date>`, e.g. `Founder-Video-TooCreative-20261126`.

## Locked rules for this ad
1. **Show the real product.** Hold the **final Too Creative For Nine To Five tee** (the store version). Keep the prototype "Owners Club" hoodie off camera: the ad has to show what people receive.
2. **No membership claims.** "(MEMBERS ONLY)" can be seen on the print, but never say "join the club / become a member". "Welcome to the family" is fine.
3. **Don't shame people with jobs.** Most buyers have a 9-to-5. The message is "you're meant for more", never "your job is a waste".
4. **No income promises.** Say "freedom", not "financial freedom".
5. **Say the offer exactly as it works at checkout,** with the real end date.

## Offer decision (Jake)
"Buy 2, third free" replaces the locked BFCM tiers (Buy 2 save 20% · Buy 3+ save 25%). Real numbers, before ads:

| Order | Offer | Customer pays | Contribution | Break-even ROAS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hoodie + tee | Save 20% (locked plan) | $112 | $43.07 | 2.60 |
| 2 hoodies | Save 20% (locked plan) | $136 | $53.20 | 2.56 |
| 2 hoodies + free tee | **Buy 2, free tee** | $170 | $60.11 | **2.83** |
| Hoodie + tee + free tee | **Buy 2, free tee** | $140 | $44.16 | **3.17** |
| 2 hoodies + free hoodie | Buy 2, any 3rd free | $170 | $46.93 | 3.62 |
| 3 tees | Buy 2, any 3rd free | $110 | $28.21 | **3.90** (too thin) |

**Recommendation:** if the hook is "free piece", make it **"Buy any 2, get a free tee"** (a $55 tee, cheapest item). It reads stronger than a percentage, and the customer gets a real third garment. "Any third piece free" is too thin on tees. In Shopify it's an automatic **Buy X Get Y** discount; the customer still adds the free tee to the cart, so say that on the product page and in the cart.
