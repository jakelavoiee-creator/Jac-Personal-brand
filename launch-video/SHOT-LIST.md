# Launch Series: "Claude + Higgsfield made this ad"

**Format:** one episode per Trybe beauty product, 31s, 9:16.
**Story:** the behind-the-scenes shows the Claude to Higgsfield MCP connection doing the work. The payoff is the finished ad, which is the same ad submitted to the brand on Trybe.
**North star:** cinematic and calm. A dark walnut desk at golden hour, warm lamp bokeh, no face on camera. Screens are shot at angles, never flat.
**Spec:** 1080×1920 master, upscaled to 2160×3840, 30fps.

## The on-screen prompt (shot 11)

```
Use Higgsfield to make a 10-second vertical 9:16 product ad for this [PRODUCT NAME] by [BRAND].
Keep the packaging exactly as in the photo. Premium beauty commercial: soft studio light, slow macro of the texture, product hero on the final frame.
No before/after results, no text in the video.
```

## Ad rules (every episode)

- The packaging and product must match the real listing exactly.
- No AI-generated skin results or before/afters tied to the product.
- Check the brand's Trybe brief and usage terms before posting the behind-the-scenes video.
- Disclose **#ad** plus the AI label on the behind-the-scenes post, and follow Trybe's disclosure rules on the ad itself.

## Recording instructions (no camera needed)

1. **Mac setup:** dark mode, dock hidden, a fresh Chrome profile with no bookmarks bar, all other tabs closed.
2. **Record:** Cmd+Shift+5 → **Record Entire Screen**. Turn **Show Mouse Clicks** on.
3. **Cursor pace:** move slowly and pause 0.5s on each target before clicking. Leave 1s of padding at both ends of every shot.
4. **Files:** one file per shot, named `shot03.mov`, `shot04.mov` and so on.
5. **Reuse:** the MCP shots (3–9) are recorded once and reused in every episode. Only shots 1, 10–13 change per product.

## Edit assembly

- Each recording is pinned onto the green screen of its plate (CapCut: Chroma key on the plate layer, then position and skew the recording beneath it).
- Warm grade on the screen layers: +8 temperature, −10 highlights, soft vignette.
- Sound: a trending sound at −14 LUFS, plus a keyboard click and a whoosh on the cuts at 3.2s and 18.3s.
- Export the 1080×1920 master, then upscale to 4K with Higgsfield `upscale_video` (Topaz, 2160p).
