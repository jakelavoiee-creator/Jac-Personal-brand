## The ad: "Wrong dish" (food-ad fake-out, 12s, 9:16)

**North star:** a hot-sauce commercial that turns into a body oil ad halfway through. Saturated reds and hot pinks, glossy, playful, golden-hour glow.
**Hook mechanic:** the viewer thinks it's a food ad. The twist at 2s makes them stay.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text (Inter Bold, white, bottom third) |
|------|------|------|
| 0.0–2.0 | **Hook:** a glossy food-commercial close-up of tacos on a red table, a hand tips the Hot Girl Hot Sauce bottle toward them | add hot sauce? 🌶️ |
| 2.0–3.5 | **Twist:** the bottle swerves away from the plate and tilts over a bare shoulder instead | wrong dish 😏 |
| 3.5–7.0 | Slow motion: golden oil drizzles onto the shoulder and runs down the arm, catching the light | *(none)* |
| 7.0–9.5 | A hand glides the oil along a forearm and leg, a glossy golden sheen in warm sunlight | hot girl glow |
| 9.5–12.0 | **Hero:** the bottle (plus the Shimmer version) on a hot-pink backdrop with fresh red chilies, slow push-in | Hot Girl Hot Sauce Body Oil |

**Rules:** the bottle matches the real product exactly. No faces. The sheen and shimmer stay realistic to what the oil really looks like on skin. No claims (no "hydrates for 24h", no "anti-aging").
