# Higgsfield × Trybe Playbook

**North star:** every episode pays twice.
1. **The behind-the-scenes reel** (Claude + Higgsfield MCP → finished ad) posts on the Higgsfield campaign accounts → Launchpoint payout.
2. **The finished ad** is pitched to the brand on Trybe → campaign access, then commission on every sale the ad drives.

One product, one recording session, two income streams.

---

## Step 1: Pick the product on Trybe (10 min)

Pick products that make a strong ad **and** convert. Score each candidate and only shoot a 4/4:

- [ ] **Visual product.** Packaging with color, texture or shine: serums, lip products, fragrance, body oils, skincare jars. Plain white tubes make weak ads.
- [ ] **Active campaign** that accepts new creators, with a commission rate you'd be happy with.
- [ ] **Clean product photo** available on the listing (front-facing, sharp, plain background).
- [ ] **Usage terms allow** creator-made content and posting the product in your own videos. Read the brief before shooting.

Keep a running shortlist of 5 so the next episode is always ready.

## Step 2: Screenshot (on camera = shot 10)

- Full-screen the product page, zoom the browser to 125% so the product fills the frame.
- Screenshot the **product image only** (Win+Shift+S / Cmd+Shift+4). Clean edges, no browser UI.
- Also download the highest-resolution product photo from the listing. That's what Claude gets; the screenshot is for the camera.

## Step 3: Prompt in Claude (on camera = shot 11)

Attach the product photo and paste:

```
Using Higgsfield, create a professional 9:16 vertical product ad for this [PRODUCT NAME] by [BRAND], ready for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Product fidelity: the packaging, label, colors and shape must match the attached photo exactly. Never redesign or relabel it.

Direction: premium beauty commercial. Soft diffused studio light, a [COLOR] backdrop pulled from the packaging, slow macro shots of the texture, one hand-held application moment, and the product hero shot on the final frame.

Pacing: 10 to 15 seconds, slow and smooth, no hard cuts mid-shot.

Rules: no before/after results, no skin transformation, no claims, no on-screen text or logos generated in the video.

Make 3 variants with different settings, then upscale the best one to 4K and give me the link.
```

## Step 4: Quality control (before anything goes out)

Reject and regenerate if any box fails:

- [ ] Packaging matches the real product: label, color, cap, shape.
- [ ] No warped text, melted hands or extra fingers.
- [ ] No implied results (glowing-skin before/after, wrinkle reduction).
- [ ] Hero shot of the product in the final 2 seconds.
- [ ] 9:16, 4K, plays cleanly on a phone at full brightness.

## Step 5: Pitch the brand on Trybe

Apply to the brand's campaign and attach the finished ad. Message:

```
Hi [BRAND] team,

I made this ad for [PRODUCT NAME] and I'd love to join your campaign.

It's a 4K, 9:16 product ad ready for Reels, TikTok and paid social. The packaging is shown exactly as it ships, with no implied results, so it's safe to run as-is.

I can make more variants for other products in your line, and I'd love to promote it through my affiliate link.

Thanks,
Jac
```

Send it the same day you post the behind-the-scenes reel, so the ad reads as fresh and the reel can be linked as proof of process.

## Step 6: Post the behind-the-scenes reel

- Edit with `launch-video/edit/` (MCP section is recorded once and reused).
- Caption template is in `SHOT-LIST.md`.
- Turn on Instagram's **AI info** label. Add **#ad** if you have any paid or affiliate relationship with the brand.

## Step 7: Track every episode

| Date | Brand | Product | Commission | Reel link | Reel views | Pitch sent | Brand reply | Sales |
|------|-------|---------|------------|-----------|------------|------------|-------------|-------|

Review weekly: which product categories get the most views, and which brands say yes. Double down on both.
