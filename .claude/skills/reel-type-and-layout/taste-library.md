## Caption styles
- Quiet Lowercase: single line, lowercase, DM Serif Display, white or #F6E7A1, centered, 200 ms fade-in, text about 3.5% of frame height, 62 to 68% down.
- Editorial Mix: sentence Inter Medium, emphasis word Instrument Serif Italic in the accent color, 2 lines max, 220 ms fade-up per phrase. Best match for the awakening series.
- Word-by-Word Pop: Montserrat Bold, white, 2 to 3 px black outline, active word scales to 108% for 120 ms, accent on 1 to 2 words per line.
- Highlight Box: rounded chip (8 px radius) behind the active word, slides in 80 ms.
- Dynamic Minimalism: words alternate white and one accent, tight tracking.
- Kinetic Poster: key phrase 1.6x, support 0.7x, left-aligned stack, hard cuts.
- Typewriter Reveal: 40 to 60 ms per character, soft key-click, 500 ms cursor blink. Good for confessions.
- Karaoke Fill, Stack Cut, Neon Glow, Luxury Lower Third, Subtitle Sentence, Hormozi Classic (parity only, consider demoting).
- Text Behind Subject: needs a subject mask.

## Look
Warm cinematic grade, grain 6 to 10%, one accent color (#9B2226 starting value), black-and-white b-roll for abstract ideas.
