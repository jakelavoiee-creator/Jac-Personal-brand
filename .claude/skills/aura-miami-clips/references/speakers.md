## Notes
- **Audio-only sources** (Neville Goddard, Napoleon Hill, Alan Watts lectures) pair well with B&W cinematic b-roll from the Aura vaults. Render with `--layout fill` over the b-roll in CapCut, or ask Claude to extend `clips.py` with a b-roll overlay mode.
- **Avoid** speakers with active controversies at posting time. Check the news before scheduling.
- **Track performance per speaker** in `aura-miami-analytics`; the roster should evolve from results, not taste.
