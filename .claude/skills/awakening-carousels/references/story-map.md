# Story map: the awakening, ages 18–24

One chapter per age. Each carousel lives in one chapter. The draft meanings are placeholders: replace them with the real moments (the user's own events, dates, places). Real details are what make people save a post.

| Age | Chapter | What it means | Image vocabulary | Darkness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | **the crack** | Life "fine" on paper, a quiet *this isn't it*. Training as escape. | an empty gym with one light, a night run, a locker-room mirror | 0.55 |
| 19 | **the search** | Consuming everything: books, podcasts, meditation, discipline as identity. | headlamp trails, open books in low light, early-morning roads | 0.55 |
| 20 | **the dark night** | Old self dissolving, friendships fall away, isolation. | a silhouette at a window, rain on glass, fog, a dark room | 0.70 |
| 21 | **the shedding** | Letting go of habits, people and the ego of the body. Grief. | a stripped-back gym, worn shoes, ash and smoke, cold water | 0.65 |
| 22 | **the rebuild** | Discipline returns, but from love instead of fear. Body and soul aligned. | chalk and iron, a sunrise sprint, breath in cold air | 0.55 |
| 23 | **integration** | Calm nervous system, presence in training and life. | still water, a stretch at dawn, a wide horizon | 0.45 |
| 24 | **the return** | Purpose and service, turning back to guide others. | a lone figure walking into light, an open road, a mountain summit | 0.40 |

Darkness steps down after the dark night, so the grid slowly gets lighter as the story moves forward.

## Recurring formats (inside any age)
- **"age NN"**: the chapter story (flagship, 7 slides)
- **"signs you're in the dark night"**: a mirror post for the viewer (6 slides)
- **"what nobody told me at NN"**: myth-busting (6 slides)
- **"the practice that got me through NN"**: one exercise for people to save (5 slides)

The last slide of every post should hand the viewer a mirror ("if you feel it too…"). The caption should end with **"comment your age 👇"**.
