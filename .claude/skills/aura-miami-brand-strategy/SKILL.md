## How to advise
Act as a creative director and operator. Give a recommendation, not a survey.
- **Anchor every answer to the north star and the numbers.** A tactic that raises sales but cheapens the brand is a no.
- **Run the numbers:**
  - Break-even ROAS = price ÷ contribution.
  - Bundles only work when contribution survives the discount.
  - **The core never goes on a flat sale.** Value comes from bundles, gifts, access and gift cards.
- **Use the 7 principles truthfully:**
  - **Reciprocity:** a personal extra in the box.
  - **Scarcity:** only real numbered or retired runs.
  - **Authority:** only third parties.
  - **Commitment:** small-yes ladders.
  - **Liking**
  - **Social proof:** the most specific *real* proof.
  - **Unity:** "one of us".
  - Counterfeit versions are out. Cialdini's line: naturally present vs counterfeit.
- **Holidays:**
  - Loss aversion with deadlines we keep.
  - Visible real competition.
  - **Fairness:** no bots, honest restocks.
  - **Gifting:** easy size exchanges, clear cutoffs, gift cards after the cutoff.
- **Extensions must pass all 5:**
  1. Same identity?
  2. Natural to an Aura customer?
  3. Same quality bar?
  4. Makes the master brand more desirable?
  5. **Sensory match:** does it feel, smell and weigh like Aura? (BRAND-PLAYBOOK §10.4)
  - **Earn the right first:** no extension before 2027 H2. Trademark classes 25, then 3 and 9, before any launch.
  - Aura for Artists: start as a **collab line**; the B2B merch service comes later.
  - Fragrance: an indie or private-label launch first, licensing only at scale.
  - Eyewear: needs a brand world, not a commodity.
- **2027 path to $1M** (~8,300 orders at a $120 AOV) needs four things by Q2:
  1. Real-feeling photos and reviews.
  2. A monthly drop ritual with a growing list.
  3. A trained ad account.
  4. Faster fulfilment or bulk runs for the hero items.

## Product tiers (BRAND-PLAYBOOK §10.1)
- **Tier 1, cult essentials:** the boxy raw-edge tee and hoodie. Permanent, at full price.
- **Tier 2, capsules:** time-boxed, then retired.
- **Tier 3, halo:** only once a real bulk or numbered run exists.
- **New-capsule filter:** would it go in a 10-year archive book?
- **Ads:** use the 3-part process reel (glimpse → real process → reveal on body). Feature the size-exchange promise on product pages and in ads.

## Scarcity and reach on print-on-demand (BRAND-PLAYBOOK §9)
- **Core designs:** always available at full price; built for reach and light buyers (Byron Sharp).
- **Capsules:** time-boxed, then **retired forever**. That is the only honest scarcity on print-on-demand. Never "only 50 left" and never stock counters.
- **Numbered runs:** only when an item moves to real bulk production.
- **Distinctive assets** (repeat them for years):
  - AURA wordmark
  - black/white/gold
  - the back-print formula
  - "Too creative for nine to five"
  - the cross necklace on the models
- **Takeoff item:** the Nine To Five tee and hoodie.
- **Ads target the moments people buy:**
  - first big paycheck
  - quitting the 9–5
  - a Miami trip or night out
  - a new chapter
  - a gift for someone ambitious
- **Drop ritual:** 48h → 24h reveal → 3h → 1h early for the list → live. The site stays open.

## Product photography (Higgsfield)
- **Model:** GPT Image 2.5, high quality, 2k, 4:5.
- **References in every shot:**
  - character sheet
  - flat-lay or mockup with the exact print
  - Pinterest image for pose/set/light only
  - **a real Tapstitch photo of the blank for colour.** Without it, black comes out washed.
- **Must hold:**
  - Raw-cut hems and sleeves (no stitched hem).
  - Boxy cropped fit: hem at the waistband, dropped shoulders, sleeves above the elbow.
  - Letter-perfect print.
  - Only the Aura logo visible.
- **Shot set:** 4 shots per colour per design (back hero → front fit → Miami lifestyle → close-up).
- **Close-ups are their own generations,** never crops of other shots.

## Never
- Fake scarcity, fake "was" prices or invented proof.
- AI models presented as customers.
- Membership claims before the program exists.
- Misleading product images.
- Copying other brands' assets.

**Persuasion yes, deception no.**
