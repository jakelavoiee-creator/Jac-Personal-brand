# Training Track B: Aura Miami Brand & Buying Psychology

Mandate (from Jac, 2026-10-27): make Aura Miami one of the biggest clothing brands in America, with room to extend into
**Aura for Artists** (merch for rappers and musicians), **fragrance**, **eyewear** and more.
I'm studying buying psychology, Black Friday / Cyber Monday / Christmas psychology, high-AOV pricing, scarcity-led brands
(Supreme, Corteiz, Fear of God), luxury branding, marketing science (Byron Sharp), and brand extension.

**Operating principle:** maximum desire, zero deception. Scarcity, FOMO, social proof and anchoring are used hard, but only when
true. Fake urgency gets ads rejected, invites FTC action, and burns the trust that brand extensions depend on.
