* **Title:** Why Retargeting is DEAD (Do This Instead for E-commerce)
* **Channel:** BitBranding (Presented by Aaron Pearson, Co-Founder & Managing Director) [00:13, 02:50]

---

## One-line thesis
Traditional retargeting ad campaigns on Meta are inefficient and self-competing due to post-iOS algorithm changes; apparel brands should allocate 100% of their ad budget to Top of Funnel (TOF) Broad Prospecting with Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) and handle retargeting through Email, SMS, and Live Selling.

---

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Stop Running Dedicated Meta Retargeting Campaigns** [03:07]
   * Stop creating custom audience retargeting ad sets (e.g., Website Visitors 30 Days, Add to Cart 14 Days) that artificially constrain Meta's machine learning algorithm and inflate CPMs.

2. **Reallocate 100% of Ad Spend to TOF Prospecting** [04:33]
   * Use Meta purely as a cold customer acquisition platform to drive new visitors to your Shopify store.

3. **Build a Single TOF CBO Prospecting Campaign in Meta Ads Manager** [05:44–11:20]
   * **Campaign Level:** Objective = Conversions [06:07]. Turn on Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) [07:20].
   * **Ad Set Level:** 
     * Conversion Location = Website [07:42].
     * Optimization Event = Purchase [08:46].
     * Dynamic Creative = Toggle ON [08:50].
     * Audience = Broad targeting based on customer research (Age, Gender, Location) without stacked interest filters [06:22, 09:50].
     * Placements = Manual Placements (Feeds, Stories, Reels, Search, Messages); uncheck "Apps and sites" [09:08].
     * Attribution = 1-day click or 1-day view (preferred) or 7-day click / 1-day view [10:10].
   * **Ad Level:** Populate Dynamic Creative with 3–5 headlines, 3–5 primary text options, and 5–6 creative assets (images/videos) [11:48].

4. **Implement Third-Party Attribution Tracking** [14:15]
   * Connect an external attribution platform (e.g., Triple Whale) to track real pixel data, first-party customer lifetime value (LTV), and blended ROAS.

5. **Capture First-Party Data On-Site for Owned Channel Retargeting** [17:42]
   * Install an on-site email/SMS pop-up designed to capture at least 10% of new website visitors [17:48].

6. **Automate Email & SMS Remarketing Flows** [18:00–19:12]
   * Set up core automated email/SMS sequences in Klaviyo to convert abandoned visitors without spending additional ad dollars.

## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:** Simplified CBO campaign structure focusing on broad prospecting rather than multi-tiered funnel campaigns [03:49, 05:44].
* **Campaign Objective:** Conversions (Purchase event) [06:07, 08:46].
* **Budgets:** CBO at the campaign level (e.g., $100/day daily budget demonstrated) [07:30].
* **Bidding Strategy:** Default Highest Volume / Lowest Cost bid strategy [07:30].
* **Targeting Settings:**
  * **Location:** United States [09:50].
  * **Age:** Broad age bracket defined by customer research (e.g., 25–60 shown in demo) [09:58].
  * **Gender:** Women (for female apparel store demo) [10:00].
  * **Detailed Targeting:** None / Broad (rely on Meta's machine learning and creative to self-target) [06:22].
* **Placements:** Manual Placements — keep Facebook/Instagram Feeds, Stories, Reels, Search, and Messages enabled. **Exclude:** "Apps and sites" (Audience Network) [09:08–09:16].
* **Testing Cadence & Ad Format:** Dynamic Creative Ad Sets (DCT). Submit 3–5 headlines, 3–5 primary text variations, and 5 to a maximum of 6 creative assets (images/videos) per ad set [11:48–11:54].
* **Scaling Rules:** Scale budget directly at the CBO campaign level or introduce new DCT ad sets with fresh creative assets into the main CBO campaign [12:12–12:18].
* **Attribution Setting:** `1-day click or view` (for accounts generating 30–50+ purchases per week). Fall back to `7-day click or 1-day view` if purchase volume is lower [10:10–10:28].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

* **Running Separate Retargeting Campaigns on Meta:** Meta’s prospecting algorithm already groups users who interacted with similar brand sites (e.g., Nordstrom, Banana Republic) into warm clusters [01:25]. Setting up separate retargeting ad sets forces you to bid against yourself, causing CPMs to spike to $50–$200+ [02:34, 05:10].
* **Over-Constraining Audience Parameters:** Narrowing audiences post-iOS 14 limits machine learning data and leads to ad fatigue [03:36].
* **Selecting "Website and Shop" as Conversion Location:** Facebook Shop native checkout presents payment payout delays, improper PayPal integration receipts, tax tracking confusion, and unwanted automated 20% discounts [07:48–08:43].
* **Overloading Assets in Dynamic Creative:** Putting 10 images into one DCT dilutes ad spend across too many combinations. Limit assets to 5–6 images/videos per DCT [11:39–11:54].
* **Trusting Meta Ads Manager Revenue Reporting:** Post-iOS 14 Meta reporting is under-reporting or misattributing revenue; relying solely on Ads Manager leads to bad optimization decisions [14:00–14:35].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Eliminate Meta retargeting ad sets:** Reallocate 100% of Meta budget to Top-of-Funnel (TOF) CBO prospecting campaigns.
2. **Leverage cross-web machine learning:** Meta automatically serves your broad prospecting ads to users who browsed competitor stores (e.g., Nordstrom, Banana Republic) without requiring custom audience setup.
3. **Streamline Dynamic Creative Ad Sets:** Use a maximum of 5–6 images/videos, 3–5 headlines, and 3–5 primary text variations per DCT ad set to avoid budget dilution.
4. **Bypass Facebook Shop native checkout:** Select "Website" as the conversion location and optimize for "Purchase" to maintain full control over payouts, taxes, and pricing on Shopify.
5. **Shift retargeting to owned channels:** Capture ~10% of site traffic via pop-ups and run 6 automated Klaviyo flows (Browse Abandonment, Abandon Cart, Abandon Checkout, Welcome, Winback, Upsell) alongside SMS and Live Selling.
