## Title & channel
*   **Title**: How To Create High Converting Ads with AI (New System)
*   **Channel**: Sam Piliero

## One-line thesis
Scale creative output and performance by using AI tools like Superscale to identify high-impression competitor ads and marketplace trends, then automatically iterate on internal "winners" to maximize the probability of hitting "home run" creatives.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1.  **Manual Competitor Research (The Baseline)**: Go to the Meta Ads Library, search for a primary competitor (e.g., Honeylove), filter for "Active ads," and sort by "Impressions: High to Low" to identify ads that have been running the longest (a proxy for profitability) [02:04].
2.  **Marketplace Trend Analysis**: Identify broader category trends beyond direct competitors (e.g., "Value Stacking" or "Moody Cinematic Lighting") using a tool like Superscale that aggregates data from nearly 1 million ads [07:05].
3.  **Define Internal Winners**: Analyze your own Meta Ads Manager. Identify "winners" as ads that drive at least 5% of total account/campaign spend and maintain a ROAS above the account average (e.g., >2.1 ROAS) [09:29].
4.  **AI-Driven Recreation**: Use AI (Claude, ChatGPT, or Superscale) to recreate identified competitor ads or marketplace trends using your own product images.
    *   **Manual path**: Copy competitor image -> Prompt AI to swap products/branding [02:30].
    *   **Automated path**: Use Superscale’s "Create this ad for me" feature to automatically map competitor layouts to your product catalog [05:03].
5.  **Iteration Loop**: Prompt an AI assistant (integrated via MCP) to scan your ad account daily for winners and generate 2–3 new variations of each based on current high-performing "bold" trends [11:29].

## Meta ads specifics
*   **Winner Identification**: Sort ads by "Amount Spent" over the last 30 days. High-spend ads that maintain target ROAS are the primary candidates for iteration [09:17].
*   **Research Metrics**: When researching competitors in the Ads Library, look for ads launched several months ago that are still active; these are confirmed "winners" [02:14].
*   **Attribution**: The example account shown used "7-day click, 1-day view" attribution [09:00].
*   **Scaling Rule**: Increasing the volume of ad creative variations increases the statistical probability of finding a "home run" ad [04:18].

## Creative specifics
*   **Ad Formats**: Focus on static image ads for rapid iteration, though video is also supported [11:39].
*   **Hooks/Frameworks**:
    *   **Features into Benefits**: Mapping specific product attributes to consumer outcomes [06:27].
    *   **Value Stacking**: Visually grouping multiple products or benefits to increase perceived value [07:08].
    *   **Moody Cinematic Lighting**: A specific visual trend for apparel [07:57].
    *   **High-Contrast Visuals**: For athleisure [07:41].
*   **Iteration Loop**: Generate at least two new versions for every "winner" identified in the ad account [11:42].
*   **Volume**: The agency maintains "Boards" of ~300 top-performing ads across categories (Lead Gen, E-com, Fashion, Supplements) to use as reference material [05:29].

## Claude / AI workflow
*   **Tools**: Claude (Opus 5 High), ChatGPT, and Superscale (formerly Nano Banana).
*   **MCP Integration**: Used to connect the AI directly to Meta Ads Manager to pull real-time performance data [10:05].
*   **Manual Skims Prompt**: "I like this image. it's from a competitor. I took it from the Facebook Ads Library, and I just want you to replace their branding and the bra that's on here with the one that I'm going to send in the second image. Also make sure all the branding is swapped out, and can you also return to me three different headlines instead of the current headline that's on this ad?" [03:00].
*   **Winning Ad Identification Prompt**: "the facebook ads MCP I want you to locate the top five percent of ads in my ad account that specifically are over my average return on ad spend. To be very clear, I’m looking for any ads that are over a two point one return on ad spend that have spent greater than five thousand dollars. All of these ads, I want delivered to me every single day on a regular schedule, and you could just send me the Facebook ad library link so that I could go ahead and reproduce this elsewhere" [10:05].
*   **Automated Iteration Prompt**: "Spent more than $5,000 over the last 30 days. I want you to find the winning ads in my ad account, only the image ads for this example, and recreate two versions of every single winner that you can find. Feel free to be as creative as you want. I’m going for a bold look in this case because I want to hop on one of the bold trends that I saw in the ad trend category" [11:29].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
*   **The "Protected" Watermark**: Large brands like Skims now use a "PROTECTED" watermark on Ads Library images, which causes standard AI tools (Claude/ChatGPT) to reject the image or fail to process it [01:25].
*   **Ad Fatigue**: Warning against recreating competitor ads that have been running too long without checking recent relevance; they may be fatigued and no longer effective [03:36].
*   **Manual Inefficiency**: Manually prompting AI to swap products results in "off-brand" content that lacks "flavor" or "love" [03:57].

## Claims vs. evidence
*   **Claim**: Using the "M3 Method" for e-commerce clients results in a 28% increase in ROAS and a 55% increase in profit in the first 90 days [00:45].
*   **Evidence**: Showed a real Meta Ads Manager dashboard with $670k spend and a 2.36 ROAS [09:00].
*   **Evidence**: Showed the "Ad Trends" interface identifying specific frameworks like "Value Stacking" based on a database of nearly 1 million ads [07:08].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Don't just copy direct competitors**: Look for rising trends in the broader "Athleisure & Activewear" category to stay ahead of the curve [07:38].
2.  **Filter by longevity**: In the Meta Ads Library, the most valuable ads to study are those that have been active for several months, as these are proven profit-drivers [02:14].
3.  **Automate iteration**: Identify your top 5% of ads by spend and use AI to generate new versions daily. If an ad represents >5% of your spend, it deserves multiple variants [09:29].
4.  **Use specific visual frameworks**: "Value Stacking" and "Features into Benefits" are highlighted as high-impact frameworks for physical products [07:08].
5.  **Bypass watermarks with specialized tools**: If standard AI rejects watermarked competitor images, use a dedicated creative tool (like Superscale) designed for media buyers [04:39].
