## One-line thesis
To scale a youth fashion/streetwear brand, pair aesthetic product content and compelling package offers with an Instagram-only Meta cold conversion campaign that split-tests manual demographic targeting against Advantage+ Audience.

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Brand Pre-requisite Audit [01:46 - 03:48]:** Ensure 5 core factors are optimized before launching ads: Aesthetic Content, Unique Product, Win-Win Offer, Brand Aesthetic, and Platform Selection (Instagram focus).
2. **Meta Business Manager Setup [03:56 - 05:40]:**
   - Log into personal Facebook account and navigate to Business Settings (`All Tools` -> `Business Settings`) [04:20].
   - Navigate to `Pages` -> `Add` -> `Create a new page` -> Choose `Brand or Product` -> Category: `Clothing Brand` -> Name page after the brand -> Click `Create Page` [04:32].
   - Under Page settings, click `Assign people` -> Select your account -> Toggle `Full control` -> Click `Assign` [04:52].
   - Navigate to `Ad accounts` -> `Add` -> `Create a new ad account` -> Name account -> Set time zone/currency -> Click `Next` & `Create` [05:03].
   - Assign full control to your personal account on the Ad Account [05:33].
3. **Instagram Account Linking [05:41 - 05:59]:**
   - Go to `Instagram accounts` -> Click `Add` -> Log in with Instagram brand credentials -> Click `Assign people` -> Toggle `Full control` -> Click `Assign` [05:42].
   - Under `Connected assets`, click `Assign assets` -> Select the Ad Account -> Click `Add` [06:31].
4. **Pixel / Dataset Setup & Asset Connection [06:00 - 06:40]:**
   - Go to `Data sources` -> `Datasets` -> Click `Add` -> Name dataset after brand -> Click `Create` [06:03].
   - Click `Assign people` -> Toggle `Full control` -> Click `Assign` [06:15].
   - Click `Connected assets` -> `Assign assets` -> Select your Ad Account -> Click `Add` [06:21].
5. **Billing Configuration [06:50 - 07:08]:**
   - Go to `Billing & payments` -> `Accounts` -> Click `Add payment method` (or `Pay now`) -> Input credit/debit card details [06:57].
6. **Shopify Integration & CAPI Pixel Link [07:09 - 07:57]:**
   - In Shopify Admin, add the `Facebook & Instagram` sales channel app [07:13].
   - Open app settings and navigate to `Share data` -> Set data sharing preference to **Maximum** (enables Meta Pixel, Advanced Matching, and Conversions API) [07:34].
   - Connect the Meta Dataset/Pixel and verify the Pixel ID in Shopify matches the Pixel ID shown in Business Settings [07:44].
7. **Campaign Creation [07:58 - 09:03]:**
   - In Ads Manager, verify correct Ad Account is selected in top dropdown [08:08].
   - Click `+ Create` -> Select objective **Sales** -> Choose **Manual sales campaign** [08:16].
   - Name campaign: `Cold - Conv` [08:31].
   - Toggle **Use a catalog** to **OFF** [08:53]. Click `Next` [09:03].
8. **Ad Set Configuration (Manual Demographic) [09:04 - 11:03]:**
   - Name Ad Set: `Cold - Conv` [09:07].
   - Conversion location: **Website** [09:13].
   - Performance goal: **Maximize number of conversions** [09:19].
   - Pixel: Select brand dataset (`CorruptedUSA 2023 Pixel (MAIN)`) [09:23].
   - Conversion event: **Purchase** [09:35].
   - Budget: Set Daily Budget to **$20.00/day** [09:39].
   - Audience: Select Age **18–28**, All Genders, Location: United States [10:43].
   - Placements: Select **Manual placements** -> Uncheck Facebook, Messenger, and Audience Network -> **Instagram Only** [10:50]. Click `Next`.
9. **Ad Creation - Concept 1 (Manual Flat Lay Carousel) [11:07 - 13:34]:**
   - Name Ad: `1` [11:10].
   - Select Facebook Page & Instagram Account under Identity [11:20].
   - Ad Setup: `Create Ad` -> Format: **Carousel** [11:43].
   - Media: Select/upload professional flat lay product images [11:56].
   - Primary Text: Write concise offer/urgency copy (e.g., `Judas Hoodie OUT NOW - Limited Time Only`) [12:47].
   - Call To Action: **Shop now** [12:25].
   - Website URL: Input specific product landing page URL for each card [13:08].
10. **Ad Creation - Concept 2 (Use Existing Organic Post) [13:35 - 14:33]:**
    - Duplicate Ad `1` -> Rename duplicate to `2` [13:36].
    - Ad Setup: Change from `Create ad` to **Use existing post** [13:43].
    - Click `Select post` -> Tab `Instagram` -> Select high-engagement organic feed/carousel post -> Click `Continue` [13:48].
    - Call To Action: **Shop now** -> Enter direct product URL [14:26].
11. **Audience Split-Test Setup [14:34 - 15:00]:**
    - In left navigation column, duplicate Ad Set `Cold - Conv` [14:40].
    - Rename duplicate Ad Set to `Cold - Conv +` [14:47].
    - In duplicate Ad Set, scroll to Audience section -> Switch to **Advantage+ Audience** [14:53].
    - Click **Publish** [14:58].

## Meta ads specifics
- **Account Structure:** Single Sales Campaign (`Cold - Conv`) containing 2 Ad Sets (`Cold - Conv` and `Cold - Conv +`) and 2 Ads per Ad Set (`1` and `2`) [08:27 - 14:55].
- **Campaign Objective:** Sales (Manual Sales Campaign) [08:22].
- **Budgets:** $20/day per ad set starting budget (Recommended test range: $10/day to $40/day) [09:39, 09:47].
- **Bidding:** Maximize number of conversions (Lowest cost volume strategy) [09:19].
- **Targeting:** 
  - Ad Set 1: Age 18–28, United States, All Genders [10:44].
  - Ad Set 2: Advantage+ Audience [14:53].
- **Placements:** Manual Placements -> **Instagram Only** (uncheck Facebook, Messenger, Audience Network) [10:50 - 11:03].
- **Testing Cadence:** Side-by-side split test between manual young demographic (18-28) vs. Advantage+ Audience [10:31, 14:36].
- **Scaling Rules:** Never raise daily ad set budgets by more than **33% per day**. Exceeding 33% resets the campaign back into the learning phase [10:07].
- **Kill Rules:** None explicitly specified in video.
- **Attribution Setting:** 7-day click or 1-day view (shown on screen in Ads Manager) [08:07, 09:04].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
- **Running Ads on Facebook for Streetwear:** Do not advertise on Facebook if your audience is young (18-28), as they do not actively scroll Facebook; focus spend strictly on Instagram [03:15, 10:54].
- **Over-budgeting Scale Increase:** Scaling daily ad set budget by >33% in a single day ruins campaign optimization by pushing it back into the learning phase [10:07].
- **Using Automated Catalogs for Cold Ads:** Avoid turning on "Use a catalog" for cold sales conversion campaigns; use manual creative selection instead [08:53].
- **Missing Asset Connections:** Ensure Business Manager Assets (Ad Accounts, Instagram Accounts, and Datasets) are explicitly connected to each other, or tracking and delivery will fail [04:52, 06:21, 06:31].
- **Generic Traffic Destinations:** Do not link ads to generic homepages when showcasing a specific garment; direct traffic straight to that product page [13:08].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Restrict Placements to Instagram:** Uncheck Facebook and Messenger for streetwear brands targeting 18–28 year-olds to prevent wasted spend [03:15, 10:50].
2. **Leverage "Use Existing Post" Ads:** Run ads using organic Instagram posts to retain and accumulate likes and social proof directly on your brand profile grid [14:04].
3. **Set Shopify Data Sharing to Maximum:** Always select "Maximum" data sharing in Shopify's Meta app to activate Conversions API and Advanced Matching [07:34].
4. **Cap Budget Increases at 33% Daily:** Scale profitable ad sets in increments of 33% per day or less to avoid resetting the Meta learning phase [10:07].
5. **Pack In-Box Perceived Value:** Incorporate free unannounced items (stickers, keychains, bracelets) or offers into your bundle strategy to boost conversion rates [02:35].
