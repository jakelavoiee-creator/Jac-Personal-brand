## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Source Creators Inbound**: Use platforms like Insense (`insense.pro`) for product seeding (gifting product in exchange for content) and paid campaigns. Inbound creator applications provide stronger negotiating leverage on rates [01:43 - 02:02].
2. **Pitch 30-Day Complimentary Partnership Access**: When onboarding new or inbound creators, pitch a 30-day complimentary access trial: *"Grant us 30-day complimentary partnership access to test your ad. If your content scales, you get onboarded to a monthly retainer + performance bonuses"* [05:44 - 06:31]. (~90% acceptance rate) [07:03].
3. **Request Access via Leadsie**: Generate a custom access request link using Leadsie (`leadsie.com`) selecting Facebook Page and Instagram Account permissions [04:29 - 04:44]. The creator opens the link and completes 3 steps: 1. Connect Meta, 2. Select Accounts, 3. Finish [04:46 - 04:52].
4. **Set Up Dual-Identity Partnership Ads**: In Meta Ads Manager, toggle **Partnership ad** to **ON**. Assign Identity 1 as your brand's FB Page/IG Account and Identity 2 as the creator's FB Page/IG Account. Set header display to **Dynamic identity** [09:10 - 09:37].
5. **Test Variations in CBO**: Cut the creator's raw video into 3 distinct hooks/angles. Launch 3 ads inside a dedicated adset within your main testing CBO (`EAM//Acquisition//US//CBO`) or a dedicated raw content CBO (`EAM//Raw-Content//US//CBO`) [08:01 - 08:48].
6. **Validate & Scale Before Signing Retainers**: Do not sign retainers upfront. Validate that Meta’s algorithm actually spends budget ($100–$1,000+/day) and achieves profitable CPA/ROAS on the ad [02:16 - 02:53].
7. **Onboard Winners to Retainers + Bonuses**: Once validated, sign the creator to a monthly retainer ($500–$1,500/month for 1 video/week) plus a performance bonus tied to generated revenue/spend [03:21 - 03:33].
8. **Maintain Creator Feedback Loops**: Use ad analytics tools (e.g., Atria `atria.ai`) to generate performance reports for creators. Share CPA/ROAS data so creators understand which hooks convert and can duplicate success [12:35 - 12:51].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Paying Upfront Retainers / High Whitelisting Fees to Unvalidated Creators**: Avoid paying `$1,000+` upfront access fees to micro-creators (e.g., 2,000–4,000 followers) before testing if their ad can scale in Ads Manager [02:16, 11:32 - 11:40]. Unproven micro-creators should not be paid more than `$100–$200` for initial access [11:53 - 11:57].
2. **Not Utilizing Meta Partnership Ads Toggle**: Running traditional whitelisted ads (creator profile only) misses out on Meta’s algorithmic preference and spend distribution for dual-identity Partnership Ads [00:33 - 00:50, 09:46 - 09:50].
3. **Failing to Provide Performance Feedback Loops**: Not sharing performance data (CPA, ROAS, hook rate) with creators leads to repetitive low-converting content [12:30 - 12:57].
4. **Undercompensating Scaled Creators**: Trying to keep high-performing creators on low flat fees when their ad is generating tens of thousands in revenue damages long-term relationships [12:15 - 12:28].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Default All UGC to Meta Partnership Ads**: Always enable the *Partnership ad* toggle and pair your clothing brand profile with the creator profile using **Dynamic Identity** to leverage Meta's algorithmic distribution push [09:10 - 09:37].
2. **Standardize the 30-Day Complimentary Access Pitch**: Pitch creators on granting 30-day complimentary access via Leadsie links during product seeding/gifting. Reserve paid monthly retainers exclusively for creators whose ads pass your spend and CPA thresholds [05:44 - 06:31].
3. **Use Leadsie to Eliminate Onboarding Friction**: Stop asking creators for manual Meta Business Manager access or ad IDs. Send a single Leadsie link (`leadsie.com`) to get FB/IG partner access in under 60 seconds [04:29 - 04:52].
4. **Structure Creator Retainers with Performance Incentives**: Pay validated creators `$500–$1,500/month` for 1 video/week, and add performance bonuses linked to revenue/spend generated by their ads to align incentives [03:21 - 03:33].
5. **Always Test 3 Cut Variations per Creator Asset**: Never run a single raw video. Slice raw creator footage into 3 distinct hooks/angles and test them inside a single adset (`UGC Video #1`) within your CBO campaign [08:35 - 08:48].
