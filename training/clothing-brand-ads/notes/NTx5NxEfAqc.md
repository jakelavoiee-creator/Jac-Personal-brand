## One-line thesis
Meta’s Advantage+ Sales Campaign (ASC) works best when structured deliberately with isolated winning creatives and strict custom audience exclusions, rather than blindly relying on Meta’s recommendation of stacking 10–20 ads into a single automated campaign without separate retargeting [13:03, 15:13, 17:16].

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Pixel Training via Manual Setup (For New Stores):**  
   If operating a new e-commerce store with no pixel data, start with a Manual Sales Campaign setup using interest/defined audience targeting to feed initial pixel conversion signals before transitioning to Advantage+ Sales Campaigns [16:30–17:15].

2. **Create Advantage+ Sales Campaign (ASC):**  
   In Ads Manager, select the **Sales** objective and opt for Advantage+ Sales Campaign setup [00:12, 01:04].

3. **Configure Campaign Budget & Bidding:**  
   * Enable Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO / Advantage Campaign Budget) [01:01, 06:21].
   * Set **Campaign Bid Strategy** to **Highest Volume** (default). Avoid ROAS Goal / Cost Cap unless historical account ROAS is stable [01:33–02:58].

4. **Set Ad Set Conversion Location & Goals:**  
   * Set **Conversion Location** strictly to **Website** (drive sales to a single conversion location) [04:05–04:12].
   * Set **Performance Goal** to **Maximize number of conversions** [05:01–05:06].
   * Keep **Attribution Setting** on the default (7-day click, 1-day view, 1-day engaged view) [05:10–05:33].

5. **Apply Ad Set Spending Limits for Multi-Ad Set Control:**  
   Because ASC enforces CBO, use **Ad Set Spending Limits** (Daily Minimum / Daily Maximum) to prevent CBO from allocating budget disproportionately across ad sets [06:40–07:05].

6. **Implement Audience Exclusions (Isolate TOF vs. Retargeting):**  
   * Set minimum age (restricted to 18+ or 25+ in ASC) [07:42–07:50].
   * In **Custom Audience Exclusion**, explicitly exclude existing buyers (e.g., Purchase 180 days) and engaged users (e.g., FB/IG Engaged 14d/180d, Website Visitors) to ensure the ASC functions strictly as a Top of Funnel (TOF) prospecting engine [08:03–08:28, 18:30–19:00].

7. **Isolate Creatives (Single-Ad Structure):**  
   Instead of uploading 10–20 creatives into one ASC ad set, place individual winning creatives into their own isolated ad sets/campaigns to guarantee dedicated budget delivery per creative [15:13–15:45].

8. **Build a Dedicated Retargeting Campaign:**  
   Run a separate manual/custom retargeting campaign targeting 7-day to 14-day engaged non-buyers at a high frequency (5–8 impressions per week) [18:08–18:22, 18:30–19:11].

9. **Build Long-Term Retention/Restock Retargeting (For Repeat Purchase Products):**  
   Target past buyers in specific post-purchase windows (e.g., last 60 days excluding last 45 days, or last 90 days excluding last 75 days) using reminder/restock copy [20:28–20:58].

## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:** 
  * Separate **TOF Prospecting ASC** (with exclusions) and **Manual Retargeting** into separate campaigns [08:40, 18:30, 19:12].
  * Single-ad campaign/ad set structure for proven winning creatives [15:13–15:35].
* **Campaign Objective:** Sales [01:04].
* **Budgets & Allocation:** 
  * CBO / Advantage Campaign Budget set at campaign level [01:01, 06:21].
  * Example budget shown on screen: **RM75.00/day** [01:28].
  * Funnel Spend Split: ~80% Top of Funnel (TOF) / ~20% Retargeting (or <5% budget allocated for long-term replenishment retargeting) [19:28, 20:56–21:30].
  * Ad Set Spending Limits (Daily Maximum, e.g., RM10.00/day per ad set) used to enforce equal spend distribution under CBO [06:40–06:53].
* **Bidding Strategy:** 
  * Default: **Highest Volume** [01:33, 02:29].
  * Alternative Options Shown in Menu: Cost per result goal, Aim for a certain return on ad spend (ROAS goal), Bid cap [01:33–01:46].
  * Warning: ROAS goal requires pre-existing, highly stable account ROAS data to function effectively; otherwise, ads stop delivering [02:30–02:58].
* **Targeting:**
  * Location: Country level (e.g., Malaysia) or State/Region level (e.g., Penang) [07:23–07:35].
  * Minimum Age: ASC restricts age selection to minimum thresholds (18 or 25 and above); specific age brackets (e.g., 30–40) cannot be locked in ASC [07:42–08:01].
  * Exclusions: Custom Audience Exclusions used for existing buyers (Website Purchase 180 days) and engaged audiences (FB Engaged 14d/180d, IG Engaged 14d/180d, Website Visitors 14d/180d, FB Messaged 14d/180d) [08:03–08:28].
* **Placements & Devices:**
  * Placements: Advantage+ placements (default) or Placement Controls (Feeds, Stories & Reels, In-stream, Search results, Messages, Apps and sites) [07:40, 10:50–10:56].
  * Devices & OS: Option to filter by All Devices, Mobile Devices Only, Android Devices Only, or iOS Devices Only [10:36–10:48]. (Example cited: Mobile iOS-only targeting for iPhone case retailers [10:43–10:48]).
* **Attribution Setting:** Default 1-day engaged view, 7-day click, 1-day view. Recommended NOT to alter attribution settings manually as it disrupts Ads Manager conversion reporting [05:10–05:33].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Blindly Stacking 10–20 Ads in One ASC Campaign:** Meta claims ASC needs 10–20 ads, but doing so often causes algorithm budget drift, unpredictable delivery, and ruined performance on winning ads [13:03–14:55].
2. **Selecting "Website and Shop" as Conversion Location:** Can lead to Meta applying uncontrolled discounts on Meta Shop platforms or skewing buyer destination tracking [04:15–04:58].
3. **Tampering with Default Attribution Settings:** Changing attribution settings (e.g., away from 7-day click / 1-day view) frequently hides true conversion data in Ads Manager and ruins attribution reporting [05:12–05:33].
4. **Setting ROAS Bidding Goals on New/Unstable Accounts:** Setting a target ROAS without months of consistent pixel revenue history will stall budget delivery entirely [02:30–02:58].
5. **Ignoring Custom Audience Exclusions in ASC:** Failing to exclude recent buyers and engaged traffic from ASC turns your "prospecting" campaign into an expensive retargeting loop [08:03–08:28, 18:30–19:00].
6. **Relying Solely on ASC for Retargeting:** ASC does not deliver the required high frequency (5–8x per week) needed to convert warm site visitors [17:16–18:22].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Isolate Your Winning Apparel Creatives:** When a specific dress, jacket, or UGC video creative emerges as a winner, move it into its own single-ad campaign/ad set with dedicated budget rather than leaving it diluted inside a multi-ad ASC stack [15:13–15:35].
2. **Protect Prospecting with Strict Exclusions:** Always exclude 180-day purchasers and 14-to-180-day social/website engaged users from your TOF Advantage+ Sales Campaigns so top-of-funnel dollars go exclusively to acquiring new cold buyers [08:03–08:28, 18:30–19:00].
3. **Build Category-Specific Prospecting Angles:** For multi-category clothing stores, run distinct TOF campaigns focused on specific categories (e.g., Dresses, Accessories, Jewelry) to identify which product line acts as the strongest cold-traffic entry point [19:20–19:40].
4. **Maintain a Separate High-Frequency Retargeting Engine:** Do not rely on ASC for retargeting. Build dedicated manual retargeting ad sets targeting 7-day cart abandoners and site visitors with a high frequency goal of 5–8 impressions per week [18:08–18:22].
5. **Set Up Automated Restock/Replenishment Loops:** For repeat-purchase products or seasonal wardrobe updates, create long-term retargeting ad sets targeting past buyers at specific intervals (e.g., Day 45 to Day 60 post-purchase) with restock incentives [20:28–20:58].
