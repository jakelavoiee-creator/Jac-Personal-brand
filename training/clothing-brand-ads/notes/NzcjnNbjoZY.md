## Title & channel
3 Ways To Use Claude For Your Clothing Brand (ChatGPT is dead?) - BitBranding

## One-line thesis
Clothing brands should transition from generic AI prompting to a systematic "onboarding" of Claude AI using deep psychographic data, competitive gap analysis, and historical performance numbers to create a brand-specific growth engine.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1.  **Migrate Memory:** Import historical data and voice preferences from ChatGPT to Claude to avoid starting from zero [02:10].
2.  **Build a System Prompt:** Create a master "Onboarding" document for Claude that defines the brand's role, context, persona, and output rules [19:30].
3.  **Customer Persona Synthesis:** Feed Claude raw customer data (reviews, DMs, comments, returns) to generate a "Psychological Customer Profile" focusing on hidden fears and purchase motivations [08:13].
4.  **Competitive Gap Analysis:** Input competitor homepage copy and ad headlines to identify the "One Sentence" positioning that no one else can claim [11:52].
5.  **Iterative Ad Copy Generation:** Use a four-input structure (Customer, Product Outcome, Competition, Constraints) to generate three distinct hooks (Identity, Problem, Proof) and then merge the best elements [03:41].
6.  **Weekly "CFO Read":** Upload 30-day performance snapshots (revenue, spend, MER, inventory, returns) to get strategic risk and opportunity assessments [14:03].

## Meta ads specifics (account structure, campaign objective, budgets, bidding, targeting, placements, testing cadence, scaling rules, kill rules, attribution)
*   **Ad Formats:** Focus on Facebook Feed ads and 50-word Instagram Stories variants [05:40].
*   **Budgets:** One example uses an ad spend of $800 per day ($24,000/month) [15:47].
*   **Placements:** Specific mention of tailoring copy for Instagram Stories [06:47].
*   **Targeting:** The strategy focuses on psychographics (why they buy/internal dialogue) over traditional demographics (age/income) [03:49].
*   **Research:** Use the Meta Ads Library to find and copy headlines from the top 3 competitors for gap analysis [11:41].

## Creative specifics (formats, hooks, angles, UGC/static/video, copy formulas, volume of creatives, iteration loop)
*   **Copy Formula (The 3 Variants):**
    1.  **Identity Hook:** Who the buyer is/how they see themselves (e.g., "For the mom who refuses to dress her boy like everyone else") [05:13].
    2.  **Problem Hook:** What they are frustrated by (e.g., "Why are boys' graphic tees either ugly or falling apart?") [05:17].
    3.  **Proof Hook:** Results/Outcomes (e.g., "124,000+ tees sold. Here's why moms keep coming back") [05:22].
*   **The "Combine" Move:** Don't choose one variant; ask Claude to take the hook from Variant 1, the body from Variant 2, and the CTA from Variant 3 to create a "Master Ad" under 100 words [05:39].

## Claude / AI workflow (skills, prompts, connectors, data inputs, outputs — quote prompts verbatim where shown)

### Connectors & Memory
*   **ChatGPT Migration:** Settings -> Capabilities -> Memory -> Import memory from other AI providers [02:11].
*   **Claude Connectors:** Connect Gmail and Google Docs to Claude to allow it to "read" the last 50 sent emails and 10 recent docs to build a "Voice Skill" [31:00].
*   **Voice Input:** Use "Whisper AI" to transcribe 10-12 pages of the founder "talking" about the brand to provide better context than typing [20:14].

### Prompts Verbatim
*   **Customer Persona Prompt [09:10]:** "Based on this, build a psychological customer profile with: 1. Core identity statement — how this customer sees themselves. 2. Purchase motivation — what they're really buying (not the product, the feeling or signal it gives them). 3. The hidden fear — what they worry about that they'd never say out loud. 4. Their exact language — specific words and phrases from the data. 5. The competitor they almost bought from — and why they didn't. Be specific. Use the exact voice from the data. No marketing language."
*   **CFO Analysis Prompt [14:51]:** "You are an experienced e-commerce CFO specializing in direct-to-consumer clothing brands. Here is our 30-day business snapshot... Based on this data: 1. What is working that we should double down on immediately? 2. What is the biggest risk hiding in these numbers right now? 3. What is the one thing you would fix first if this were your business? 4. What single metric best predicts whether next month will be better or worse? Be direct. Plain language. Prioritize ruthlessly. No hedging."
*   **System Prompt Structure [19:42]:**
    *   **ROLE:** Direct-response brand strategist and copywriter for [Brand Name].
    *   **BRAND CONTEXT:** [Brand Name] is a [Category] clothing brand for [Who they are]. Positioning: [The one sentence no competitor can say]. Price point: $[X]-[X].
    *   **CUSTOMER PROFILE:** (Paste the results from the Persona Synthesis).
    *   **TONE AND VOICE:** Include what to say and what *never* to say.
    *   **VOICE EXAMPLES:** Paste 2-3 lines from top-performing ads.
    *   **OUTPUT RULES:** 1. Always provide 3 variants. 2. Never use corporate language. 3. Always include a CTA. 4. Keep ads under 125 words.

## Mistakes / warnings called out
*   **Generic Prompting:** Writing "Write me a Facebook ad for my hoodie" results in robot-like output that doesn't convert [03:12].
*   **Demographic Focus:** Only looking at age/income/location instead of psychographics (why people buy) [03:49].
*   **Treating AI as a Routine, not a System:** Manually re-explaining the brand every time a new chat is opened burns time and causes inconsistent output [18:41].
*   **Exclamation Marks:** Specifically warns to never use exclamation marks in ad copy [21:11].
*   **Replacing Judgment:** Warning that AI cannot "feel" the energy of a product drop or replace the founder's taste [17:46].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Stop Prompting, Start Onboarding:** Treat Claude like a new employee by creating a permanent "System Prompt" PDF that stays in its memory [19:18].
2.  **Mine the "Hidden Fear":** Effective copy for clothing brands must address the psychological anxiety or "signal" the clothing provides, not just the quality of the fabric [09:11].
3.  **The "3-to-1" Copy Strategy:** Always generate three distinct hook types (Identity, Problem, Proof) and combine the winning elements into a final "Master" version [05:30].
4.  **Automate Voice Matching:** Use Claude's "Connectors" to scan your actual sent emails and Google Docs so the AI writes exactly like you, rather than a generic marketer [21:00].
5.  **Use AI as a CFO:** Perform weekly data uploads to find "hidden risks" (like rising CAC or poor 45-day repurchase rates) that are buried in the Shopify dashboard [14:03].
