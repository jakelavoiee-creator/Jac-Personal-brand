## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Dial in Brand Prerequisites (Proof of Concept):** Before launching Meta ads, establish a winning product, an optimized website with clean branding, a strong offer, and high-quality creative assets [00:32–01:31].
2. **Shift Mindset from Media Buying to Creative Production:** Stop relying on complex manual targeting or audience hacks. Accept that Meta’s AI manages targeting via creative signals [01:45–02:08].
3. **Audit Ad Creatives Against Quality Benchmarks:** Ensure all creative assets feature clean backdrops, wrinkle-free flat-lays, high contrast, proper CTA spacing, and clear product details [03:34–05:54]. Eliminate bad creative practices like dark shadows, background color blending, and AI-generated image "slop" [07:52–09:28].
4. **Set Up Campaign Structure:**
   * Create an **ABO (Adset Budget Optimization)** campaign for testing or **CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization)** for scaling/mass testing [09:54–10:04].
5. **Configure Ad Set Parameters:**
   * **Targeting:** Broad (no interest targeting, no lookalikes) [10:20].
   * **Demographics:** Age and Gender open [10:30].
   * **Placements:** Advantage+ Placements enabled [10:37].
   * **Budget:** Set daily budget to **$30–$50/day** per ad set [11:23].
6. **Set Attribution Window:** Select **7-Day Click, 1-Day View** (avoid "1-Day Engaged View") [11:41–11:44].
7. **Deploy Ad Creative & Offer:** Link ads directly to the specific product page and pair them with an incentive offer (e.g., Free Shipping + Discount / Gift) [12:28–12:43].
8. **Evaluate & Iterate Based on Meta's Ranking Signals:** Monitor watch time, CTR, and engagement metrics to feed high-performing assets to the algorithm while replacing burned-out creatives [09:34–09:43].

## Meta ads specifics

### Campaign Structure & Objectives
* **Campaign Level:**
  * **ABO (Adset Budget Optimization):** Recommended for standard creative testing [09:56].
  * **CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization):** Recommended for scaling or mass testing [10:00].
* **Objective:** Sales / Conversions (implicit for ecom) [00:20, 11:48].

### Budget & Bidding
* **Ad Set Daily Budget:** Sweet spot is **$30 to $50 per day** (adjusted depending on the total number of creatives being tested) [11:23].
* **Bidding Strategy:** Automatic bidding managed by Meta’s AI algorithm [13:23].

### Targeting
* **Audience Targeting:** **Broad only.** Do NOT use interest targeting or Lookalike Audiences (LALs) during creative testing [10:20].
* **Age & Gender:** **Open** [10:30].
* **Targeting Logic:** Creatives are the new targeting; +90% of algorithm learning signals derive from creative engagement, so the ad itself filters the audience [03:17–03:25].

### Placements
* **Selection:** **Advantage+ Placements** [10:37].
* **Reasoning:** Meta’s system operates on bidding and engagement dynamics. Instagram often carries higher CPMs. When Instagram CPMs spike, Meta temporarily shifts spend to Facebook to buy cheaper engagement/clicks, lowering overall campaign CPMs. Once system metrics stabilize, Meta re-allocates delivery back to Instagram, securing buyers in your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) without paying inflated Instagram CPM rates [10:40–11:16].

### Attribution Setting
* **Selected Window:** **7-Day Click, 1-Day View** [11:41].
* **Rejected Window:** Do NOT use 7-Day Click + 1-Day Engaged View [11:44].
* **Justification:**
  * Delivers the most accurate purchase data for e-commerce [11:48].
  * Captures higher Average Order Value (AOV) purchasers who take time to consider [11:53–11:57].
  * View-through data supports top-of-funnel cold traffic campaigns [11:59].
  * Engaged-view windows inflate performance metrics and skew data reporting [12:02].
  * Results in more stable scaling and less wasted ad spend [12:10–12:15].

## Creative specifics

### Core Philosophy: The "Andromeda Update" [02:10]
* Meta’s algorithm aggressively ranks and rotates creative assets [02:14].
* Bad creatives burn out fast; high-performing creatives capture **80% to 90%** of total campaign spend [02:18–02:22].
* Creative quality dictates **80%+** of overall performance, compared to manual audience setup [02:29].

### Performance Signals for Creative Ranking [09:34]
* **High Watch Time** → Meta Boost [09:36, 03:04]
* **High Engagement Rate** → Meta Boost [09:37]
* **High Click-Through Rate (CTR)** → Meta Boost [09:39]
* **Low Feedback Score / Low CTR / Low Engagement** → Meta Penalty (increased CPMs, spend throttled to zero) [02:46–02:55, 09:38].

### Effective Creative Formats & Examples

1. **High-Quality Product Flat-Lays (e.g., Ditch, Flowar.la):**
   * Clean, consistent backdrops across all product lines [03:54].
   * Sharp lighting without dark shadows or fabric wrinkles [03:59].
   * Full composition showing clear product details [03:59].
   * Frame layout designed to leave dedicated negative space at the bottom so CTA buttons ("Shop Now") do not overlap product imagery [05:51].

2. **Collection Overview / Grid Mockups (e.g., YoungLA, Flowar.la):**
   * A full grid showcase displaying all colorways or items in a drop/collection [05:16, 05:47].

3. **Lifestyle & On-Body Shots (e.g., YoungLA):**
   * Detailed close-up fabric shots combined with full-body fit shots on athletes/models [04:48].
   * Contextual lifestyle imagery (e.g., models wearing full brand outfit at a racetrack) [04:58–05:12].

4. **Skit / Short-Form Organic Videos (e.g., YoungLA):**
   * Personality-driven video skits used at top-of-funnel to build brand voice, community, and engagement [04:35–04:46].

5. **Behind-the-Scenes / Craftsmanship Footage (e.g., Valvetronic Designs):**
   * Hero media showing real employees fabricating/welding products to establish trust and authenticity [06:03–06:18].

### E-Commerce Website & Product Page CRO Checklist

2. **Prominent Discount Banners:** Highlight seasonal or site-wide sales at header level (e.g., "BLACK FRIDAY UP TO 60% OFF") [03:50].
3. **List-Building Incentives:** Include persistent bottom-corner popups/buttons (e.g., "GET $5 OFF") to capture Email/SMS leads for future product drops and restocks [04:05–04:14].
4. **Vehicle / Model Selectors (for catalog-heavy technical brands):** Use search filter dropdowns ("SEARCH BY YEAR, MAKE AND MODEL") right on the hero section to reduce friction and eliminate search time [06:27–06:36].
5. **Detailed Product Photos:** Clean images displaying build quality and individual components [06:48].
6. **Cross-Sell / Bundle Offers:** Implement "Frequently Bought Together" modules directly under the primary purchase action [06:58].
7. **Delivery Transparency:** Display precise, real-time estimated shipping times on the product page [07:01].
8. **In-Depth Long-Form Content for High-Ticket Products:** Embed full YouTube video walkthroughs and written breakdown articles covering installation, sound, and specs directly below the product page buy box (essential for high-ticket items priced at $4k–$5k) [07:10–07:30].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Wrinkled & Poorly Positioned Flat-Lays:** Using imagery with fabric wrinkles, awkward sideways positioning, visible clothing hangers, or heavy shadows degrades brand credibility [08:00–08:18].
2. **AI-Generated Design "Slop":** Avoid cheap AI-generated artwork on apparel T-shirts [08:24–08:30].
3. **Text Placement Overlap:** Positioning primary graphic text at the absolute bottom of an ad image causes it to be covered by Meta’s native UI and "Shop Now" CTA buttons [08:34–08:39].
4. **Poor Color Contrast (Black-on-Black):** Displaying a black garment on a dark or black fabric background hides the product outline and drops engagement [08:41–08:52].
5. **Cluttered Ad Graphics:** Overloading promotional images with random graphics, multiple competing callouts, and messy financing text weakens the core offer [08:54–09:25].
6. **Relying on Manual Optimizations / Audience Hacking:** Trying to outsmart Meta with hyper-niched interest groups or complex manual bidding in 2026 is dead and leads to wasted spend [01:52, 02:03].
7. **Using "1-Day Engaged View" Attribution:** Inflates ad metrics with low-intent clickers and skews performance data [11:44, 12:02].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Creative is Your Only Real Targeting Leverage [03:17]:** Because Meta's Andromeda system automates delivery based on user engagement signals (+90% derived from creative performance), run your ad sets with broad targeting and rely on the visual content of the ad to filter your audience [03:19–03:25, 10:20].
2. **Standardize Clean Flat-Lay Production [03:54, 08:00]:** Eliminate wrinkles, hangers, uneven lighting, and dark shadows. Use high-contrast backdrops and keep composition consistent across your entire catalog [03:59].
3. **Always Leave Negative Space for Meta's UI [05:51]:** Design vertical ad creatives with clear space at the bottom edge so Meta’s native overlay elements and "Shop Now" CTA buttons never obscure key product details or typography [08:34–08:39].
4. **Use Advantage+ Placements to Arbitrage CPMs [10:37]:** Enable Advantage+ placements to let Meta automatically siphon traffic to Facebook when Instagram CPMs spike, lowering your overall account CPM before routing converting traffic back to Instagram [10:40–11:16].
5. **Lock In a 7-Day Click, 1-Day View Attribution Window [11:41]:** Avoid 1-day engaged-view settings that inflate metrics. Use 7-day click, 1-day view to capture full AOV customer behavior and stabilize campaign scaling [11:48–12:15].
