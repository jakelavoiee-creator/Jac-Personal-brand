## ## One-line thesis
Meta’s AI ad delivery and targeting systems have advanced to the point where separating audiences and funnel stages creates auction overlap and restricts optimization; maximum performance is now achieved by consolidating into single-campaign, single-ad-set structures containing 20+ diverse creatives.

---

## ## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Eliminate Audience Separation [00:22]:** Stop splitting cold (interest/lookalike) and warm (retargeting/custom) audiences into separate ad sets or campaigns.
2. **Consolidate Account Architecture [05:14]:** Reduce campaigns and ad sets down to the fewest elements possible—ideally **1 Campaign and 1 Ad Set** per product/service category [05:23].
3. **Set Up Broad/Advantage+ Targeting [01:03]:** Input custom audiences or interest themes only into the *"Suggest an audience"* section rather than using hard constraints [01:06].
4. **Define Audience Segments for Reporting [02:52]:** Navigate to *Advertising Settings -> Audience Segments* in Ads Manager and define *"Engaged Audience"* and *"Existing Customers"* to analyze breakdown data without splitting ad sets [03:00].
5. **Load High Creative Volume & Diversity [10:44]:** Upload **20+ ads** into the single ad set spanning distinct formats (images, long/short video, UGC, founder-led, testimonials, product demos) [11:10].
6. **Consolidate Ad Copy Testing [12:41]:** Test copy variations using Meta’s built-in multiple text options (up to 5 Primary Texts, 5 Headlines, 5 Descriptions per ad unit) instead of creating duplicate ad units [12:43].
7. **Build Full-Funnel Content into One Ad Set [16:30]:** Include Top-of-Funnel (TOF), Middle-of-Funnel (MOF), and Bottom-of-Funnel (BOF) ad creative within the exact same ad set and allow Meta to sequence delivery automatically [17:16].
8. **Separate Only by Product Range, Location, or Offer Type [18:58]:** Create separate campaigns only when selling distinctly different product categories (e.g., Hats vs. Shoes), targeting distinct geographic hard boundaries (under Controls), or selling distinct service levels (e.g., Agency vs. Mentorship) [19:09, 20:38].

## ## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:** Consolidated framework. Default is **1 Campaign -> 1 Ad Set -> 20+ Ads** [05:23, 10:44].
* **Campaign Objective:** Sales / Conversions (or Leads) [00:57].
* **Budgets:** Consolidated budget at the campaign or ad set level to gather maximum conversion volume per element [05:30].
* **Bidding:** Standard platform optimization based on selected conversion events [01:00].
* **Targeting:** 
  * **Controls (Hard Boundaries) [01:05, 09:28]:** Location settings only [09:28].
  * **Suggest an Audience (Advantage+) [01:07]:** Custom audiences (website visitors, email lists, social engagers) and interest suggestions are placed here [01:13]. Meta expands targeting beyond these inputs whenever it predicts higher conversion likelihood [01:55].
  * **Audience Testing [07:52]:** Testing Interests vs. Lookalikes vs. Open Targeting in separate ad sets is explicitly marked as outdated/discontinued (red X) [08:17].
* **Placements:** Automatic / Advantage+ Placements.
* **Testing Cadence:** Add new creative variations continuously into the main ad set or via dedicated creative testing campaigns if existing winning ads starve new creatives of spend [12:12, 22:38].
* **Scaling Rules:** Scale budget on consolidated campaigns as Meta optimizes across the full user pool [05:48].
* **Kill Rules:** **Do NOT turn off high-CPA Top-of-Funnel (TOF) ads** [18:24]. High-CPA TOF ads feed top-of-funnel awareness that drives cheap conversions on Bottom-of-Funnel (BOF) ads within the same ad set [18:28].
* **Attribution:** Standard 7-day click / 1-day view shown in screenshots [03:44]. Third-party server-side tracking (e.g., Hyros) is recommended to capture recurring or delayed multi-touch revenue missed by Meta [14:05].

## ## Creative specifics

* **Formats:** Combination of Static Images, Image Carousels, Short-Form Videos, and Long-Form Videos within a single ad set [11:12, 16:30].
* **Hooks & Angles:** 
  * **TOF Angles:** Founder-led story, origin story, broad problem awareness, educational/value ads [15:14, 16:30].
  * **MOF Angles:** Product demonstrations, feature walk-throughs, long/short-form case studies, interviews [15:18, 16:30].
  * **BOF Angles:** Customer testimonials, before-and-after reviews, direct urgency/call-to-action [15:20, 16:30].
* **Volume of Creatives:** **20+ active ads per ad set** [10:44] (historically capped at 5–6 ads [10:20]).
* **Copy Formulas:** Leverage Meta’s dynamic text variations inside each ad unit [12:41]:
  * Up to **5 Primary Text options** [12:43].
  * Up to **5 Headline options** (e.g., *"Trusted by 763+ Members"*, *"We'll Mentor You Daily"*, *"Limited Spots Available"*) [12:50, 13:17].
  * Up to **5 Description options** [12:52].
* **Iteration Loop:** Use individual ad units to test distinct visual creatives; use internal text options to test copy variations [12:55, 13:08].

## ## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Splitting Retargeting & Cold Traffic [00:08, 02:20]:** Running separate warm and cold campaigns leads to massive audience overlap and duplicated spend because Meta's targeting algorithms expand beyond custom audience inputs anyway [02:26].
2. **Fragmenting Conversion Data [05:41]:** Spreading ad spend across numerous campaigns/ad sets prevents individual ad sets from getting enough conversion events to pass the learning phase.
3. **Limiting Ad Volume to 5–6 Ads Per Ad Set [10:20]:** Modern Meta algorithm updates (such as Andromeda) require large creative volume (20+ ads) to personalize ad delivery per user segment [10:35, 11:30].
4. **Creating Separate Ads for Copy/Headline Testing [12:20]:** Creating duplicate ads just to test minor text changes clutter the ad set; use the multi-text variation fields inside a single ad unit instead [13:25].
5. **Killing High-CPA Top-of-Funnel Ads [18:24]:** Turning off a TOF ad because its standalone CPA looks high will collapse the performance of low-CPA BOF retargeting ads that depend on the TOF ad for initial prospect awareness [18:31].
6. **Blindsided by Meta Recommendations/Warnings [22:04]:** Blindly following Meta's automated warnings can ruin custom strategies (like Omnipresent Nurture campaigns); deviate intentionally when required by your business model [22:07].

## ## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Combine Prospects and Past Buyers in One Sales Campaign:** Stop creating separate retargeting ad sets for website visitors or Instagram engagers. Combine all traffic into **1 Advantage+ / Broad Sales Campaign** and add your customer lists under *"Suggest an audience"* [01:07, 05:23].
2. **Build a 20+ Creative Stack Per Ad Set:** Upload at least 20 different ad variations into your main ad set [10:44]. Mix high-contrast studio statics, UGC try-on hauls, lifestyle reels, fabric close-ups, customer review carousels, and founder stories [11:12].
3. **Separate Campaigns Only for Distinct Garment Categories:** Keep product lines with distinct buyer intents in separate campaigns (e.g., **Campaign 1: Outerwear/Jacket Range** vs. **Campaign 2: Footwear Range**) [19:09, 20:05]. Do not split variations like Red Dress vs. Blue Dress into separate campaigns [19:06].
4. **Use Text Options for Copy Variations:** When testing hooks, captions, or promos (e.g., *"Free Shipping"* vs. *"15% Off"*), input them into the 5 Primary Text and 5 Headline slots within the same ad rather than publishing duplicate ad units [12:43, 13:17].
5. **Do Not Pause High-CPA Lifestyle / Brand Ads:** A visually striking lifestyle video may show a high direct CPA (e.g., £98 vs £20 on a catalog ad), but it introduces new shoppers to the brand [18:08]. If you turn it off, overall campaign revenue and catalog ad conversions will drop [18:28].
