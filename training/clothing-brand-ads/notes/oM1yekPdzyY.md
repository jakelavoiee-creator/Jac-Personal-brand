## One-line thesis
Shopify is evolving into an "agentic" commerce infrastructure where AI handles discovery, real-time pricing AB testing, and cross-channel campaign management, shifting the brand owner's priority to clean product data and high-quality photography.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1.  **Enable Custom Storefronts via Catalog API + UCP:** Transition from traditional theme constraints to AI-driven discovery where agents pull live data (pricing, availability) directly into customer conversations [01:43].
2.  **Integrate Sidekick with Third-Party Apps:** Use the AI assistant to query data across the stack (Klaviyo, Judge.me, Loop Returns, Smile.io) to monitor email performance and loyalty metrics without switching dashboards [04:33].
3.  **Deploy AI-Powered Shopify Inbox:** Activate the free live chat tool to allow AI to handle immediate sizing queries and product recommendations based on backend product descriptions [06:32].
4.  **Install Shopify Smart Pricing App:** Use the native app to run price-point AB tests and receive AI suggestions based on inventory levels, costs, and seasonality [08:19].
5.  **Join the Campaign Autopilot Waitlist:** Shift toward goal-based advertising by setting budgets and ROAS guardrails for the AI to manage across Meta, Google, and Shop campaigns [10:09].
6.  **Refine Backend Data for AI Discoverability:** Clean up product categories and descriptions to ensure AI agents can accurately surface products in natural language searches [17:40].

## Meta ads specifics

*   **Campaign Setup:** Users set a budget and "guardrails" (target ROAS and specific products to promote) [10:40].

*   **The Showroom:** A highly stylized, interactive digital fitting room experience built via the Catalog API that personalizes the shopping environment around the customer [02:50].

## Offer, product, landing page & Shopify CRO
*   **Smart Pricing AB Testing:** Native ability to test price variants (e.g., $38 vs. $44 for a T-shirt) to determine elasticity without third-party apps [08:44].
*   **Theme/Checkout AB Testing ("Rollouts"):** Allows 50/50 traffic splits to test new collection page designs or hero banners [13:48].
*   **Variant-Level Publishing:** Control specific variants (colors/sizes) by sales channel or international market without workarounds [12:56].
*   **Discounts by Market:** Run targeted promotions (e.g., Summer sale in Europe only) without affecting US pricing [14:16].
*   **Stacking Discounts:** Apply multiple discount types (percentage off + fixed amount) to a single product in one order [14:34].
*   **Persistence:** 365-day session persistence for Shop account holders, reducing login friction for returning customers [14:55].
*   **Storefront Search:** Native search now handles typos and unusual phrasing (e.g., "BLK jogger" vs "Black Jogger") to prevent zero-result dead ends [13:17].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Shift to "Guardrail" Management:** Media buyers must transition from manual lever-pulling to setting high-level ROAS goals and product priorities for Shopify's Campaign Autopilot.
2.  **Product Data is the New Targeting:** Clean, detailed metadata and product descriptions are required for AI agents to correctly surface items in conversational and "agentic" search.
3.  **Visuals Drive Discoverability:** High-quality photography is now a technical requirement for image-based AI lookup and "Showroom" custom storefronts.
4.  **Native Price Testing:** Price elasticity is now a native optimization lever; use the Smart Pricing app to find the highest margin price point that maintains conversion.
5.  **Reduced Login Friction:** Leverage the 365-day session persistence to increase LTV and repeat purchase rates by removing the "login wall" for returning Shop app users.
