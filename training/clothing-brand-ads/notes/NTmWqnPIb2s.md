## One-line thesis
To scale Meta ads profitably under the Andromeda algorithm, advertisers must shift from high-production video and micro-interest targeting to high-volume static creatives, long-form copy targeting, strict ad-to-landing-page message matching, and business-level net cash flow optimization.

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Hack #1: Leverage Static Creatives over Video ("Statics Are Eating Video Alive")** [00:06]  
   * Meta’s *Andromeda* engine requires an extreme volume of fresh creatives to prevent ad fatigue [00:31]. Static image ads are cheaper and faster to produce than videos/VSLs [01:01], allowing advertisers to feed Meta’s algorithm higher creative volume [01:52].
2. **Hack #2: Execute the "One Keyword Hack" (Identity Triggers)** [02:45]  
   * Duplicate a top-performing generic ad and insert a specific niche/demographic/identity keyword into the headline and body copy (e.g., changing "leads" to "dental leads" or "lawyer leads") [03:38]. This signals Meta's AI to deliver the ad to that exact audience segment [04:05].
3. **Hack #3: Build the "Winning Format Clone" & "Zombie Campaigns"** [05:04]  
   * Take a proven winning ad copy and feed it into an AI LLM (Claude, ChatGPT, or Kong) [05:36]. Prompt the AI to rewrite the ad into dozens of demographic-specific variations [06:06]. Group these into CBOs [06:31]. 
   * **Zombie Campaign Execution:** For high-conviction creatives that receive $0 spend in a CBO, extract them and relaunch them together in a dedicated "Zombie Campaign" ad set to force spend and uncover hidden winners [06:54].
4. **Hack #4: Don't Make Your Ads Look Like Ads (Native Content / Burner Account Strategy)** [07:11]  
   * Users naturally ignore polished ads [07:35]. Repurpose top-performing organic video/post formats or long-form unedited content directly into paid ads [08:01]. 
   * Build a "Burner Account" on TikTok/Instagram to follow niche competitors/influencers, train the algorithm on popular content, and model those viral native formats for ads [08:33].
5. **Hack #5: Combine Broad Targeting with Hyper-Specific Creative** [09:09]  
   * Avoid interest-stacking [09:24]. Run broad location targeting (e.g., country-level) and rely on long-form copy and specific creative hooks to act as the primary targeting filter for Meta’s AI [09:51, 10:52].
6. **Hack #6: Match the Ad to the Page (Message Congruence)** [11:16]  
   * Ensure 1:1 text and hook alignment between the winning ad’s headline/copy and the landing page’s headline, subheadline, and lead-in text [12:28, 13:00].
7. **Hack #7: Retarget Non-Buyers with a Different Offer** [13:17]  
   * Do not retarget non-converters with the same initial offer [13:28]. Retarget bounced visitors using objection-handling ads, customer proof/testimonials, cross-sell/ascension products, or value-based audit/consultation offers [14:53–15:31].
8. **Hack #8: Track Business-Level Net Cash Flow over In-Platform ROAS** [15:49]  
   * Focus on blended ROAS at the business level and overall net free cash flow rather than maintaining artificially inflated in-platform ROAS on low ad budgets [16:33, 16:43].

## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:** 
  * Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) for scaling groups of winning ad creatives [06:31].
  * "Zombie Campaigns" for re-testing non-spending creatives in isolation [06:54].
* **Campaign Objectives:** Conversions / Lead Generation / Sales [03:52, 11:43].
* **Targeting:** 
  * **Broad Targeting:** Country-wide targeting (e.g., Australia, USA, United Kingdom) without interest filters [10:52–11:00].
  * **Creative-as-Targeting:** Replacing manual interest stacking with niche-specific copy keywords and long context windows in body copy [03:50, 09:51].
* **Testing Cadence:** Block 1 hour per week to generate new static/copy variations [02:35]. Maintain at least 3 active split tests on landing pages/ads at all times [12:36].
* **Scaling & Kill Rules:** 
  * Scale ad spend aggressively as long as net profit / net cash flow increases, even if in-platform ROAS declines (e.g., scaling spend from $1,000/mo to $78,786/mo while ROAS drops from 10–15 down to 5–11) [16:16, 17:01].
  * Re-allocate spend in CBOs to creatives with the lowest Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) and highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) [04:48, 06:35].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

* **Over-relying on High-Production VSLs:** Spending months producing expensive video ads while static images can be produced faster, cheaper, and often yield lower CPAs [01:01, 02:20].
* **Interest Stacking:** Combining multiple interest targeting layers (e.g., Golf + Mercedes Benz) under Andromeda restricts the algorithm's context window [09:24, 10:17].
* **Riding a Single Winner to Death:** Scaling a single winning ad without building format clones leads to rapid ad fatigue and campaign collapse [05:15–05:21].
* **Message Disconnect:** Running ads with copy/hooks that do not match the exact headline on the destination landing page, causing high bounce rates [11:31–11:37].
* **Retargeting Non-Buyers with the Same Offer:** Showing the exact same ad/offer to users who already passed on it [13:28].
* **Vanity ROAS Optimization:** Refusing to scale ad budgets out of fear that in-platform ROAS percentage will drop, ignoring higher total net cash profit [16:09–16:26].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Prioritize High-Volume Static Images over Studio Video:** Fashion brands often waste weeks on high-production video shoots. Produce high volumes of static image collages, flat-lays, or simple UGC photo variations to prevent ad fatigue under Meta’s Andromeda retrieval engine [01:01, 01:52].
2. **Use Long-Form Copy for Broad Targeting:** Ditch narrow interest stacking (e.g., targeting specific luxury clothing brands). Set campaigns to broad targeting (country-wide) and use detailed body copy describing the ideal customer/lifestyle as the targeting filter [09:51, 10:52].
3. **Use LLMs to Clone Winning Angles by Demographic:** When an item or ad style wins, use Claude/ChatGPT to rewrite the copy angle tailored specifically for different age groups or style preferences (e.g., prompt LLMs to adapt the winning hook for 25-year-olds vs 40-year-olds) [05:49, 06:06].
4. **Enforce Strict Message Match on Product Pages:** Match the exact hook or headline used in the ad creative directly on the top of the collection or product landing page to boost CVR by 15%–20% [12:25, 13:00].
5. **Retarget Bounced Visitors with Cross-Sells & Testimonials:** If a customer bounces off a specific garment page, do not show them the exact same ad. Retarget them with customer video/photo reviews, style objection handling, or complementary outfits/accessories [14:06, 15:00].
