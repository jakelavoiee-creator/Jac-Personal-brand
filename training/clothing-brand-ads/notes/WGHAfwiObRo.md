## One-line thesis
Connecting your e-commerce product feed to Meta allows its AI to dynamically serve personalized product catalog ads to high-intent shoppers, while custom Product Sets give you granular control over targeting specific categories, brands, or price ranges.

---

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Access Commerce Manager [05:07]:**
   * Open Meta Ads Manager, click **All Tools** (hamburger menu), and select **Commerce Manager**.
2. **Create a New Catalog [05:25 - 05:40]:**
   * In Commerce Manager assets screen, click **+ Add account** / **Create catalog** [05:25].
   * Select **Online products** as basic info [05:40].
3. **Choose Connection Method [05:41 - 06:05]:**
   * Option A: Connect directly via a partner platform (*Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, etc.*) [05:43].
   * Option B: Standard data feed/pixel integration (toggle off partner platform for direct catalog creation) [06:04].
4. **Connect Event Tracking / Meta Pixel [06:08 - 06:30]:**
   * Select your active **Meta Pixel or SDK** corresponding to your business asset and toggle **Connection** to **ON** [06:26].
5. **Set Up Product Data Feed [06:33 - 06:55]:**
   * Choose **Connect to a data feed** [06:33]. This automatically pulls product details, prices, images, and inventory status from your website.
6. **Audit Products & Stock Status in Commerce Manager [07:13 - 08:15]:**
   * Go to **Catalog -> Products** to view synchronized items [07:16].
   * Check for errors (*e.g., missing metadata*) under "Fix issues" [07:34].
   * Verify automated stock syncing (*out-of-stock items are automatically excluded from ad delivery*) [07:42].
7. **Create Custom Product Sets [08:17 - 10:32]:**
   * Navigate to **Catalog -> Sets** and click **Create set** [08:18, 08:58].
   * Choose filtering attributes (*Title, Product Type, Brand, Price Range, Sale Price, Color, Custom Labels, or specific Product IDs*) [09:05 - 09:33].
   * Add required products, enter a clear set name (*e.g., "Winter Collection" or "Brown Boots"*), and click **Create** [10:24 - 10:30].
8. **Build the Sales Campaign in Ads Manager [10:42 - 11:03]:**
   * Create a new campaign with the **Sales** objective [10:45].
   * Toggle **Advantage+ catalog ads** to **ON** at the campaign level [10:50].
   * Select the exact **Product Catalog** from the dropdown menu [10:53].
9. **Configure Ad Level Settings & Dynamic Creative [11:04 - 12:41]:**
   * Select identity: **Facebook Page** and **Instagram Profile** [11:12].
   * Under Ad Setup, select format: **Carousel** [11:18].
   * Destination: Enable **Advantage+ destination** [11:53].
   * Product Media: Select your preferred **Product Set** [11:04, 12:06].
   * Dynamic Headlines: Click `+` to insert dynamic tags such as `product.name` and `product.price` [12:28 - 12:35].
   * Write Primary Text variations [12:22].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Avoid Generic Ads:** Don't show generic broad creative to audiences when Meta AI can serve exact product matches based on user behavior [02:23].
2. **Setup Complexity Fallacy:** Businesses avoid catalog ads assuming they are too technical or set them up incorrectly and fail to see ROI [00:36].
3. **Data Disconnect Warnings:** Always verify that your pixel is linked to the catalog during initial setup; otherwise, Meta AI cannot match pixel intent events to catalog items [06:17].
4. **Data Feed Errors:** Monitor Commerce Manager "Fix issues" notifications for items blocked due to missing metadata or policy violations [07:34].
5. **CSV Manual Feeds:** Avoid manual CSV file uploads if pixel/feed connection is available, as formatting issues are common and labor-intensive [13:03 - 13:12].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Leverage Product Sets for Gender/Category Segmentation [03:34]:** Don't rely solely on an "All Products" catalog. Create gender-specific (*Men's vs. Women's*) or category-specific (*Boots, Coats, Sale Items*) Product Sets to ensure high relevance [03:34 - 03:40].
2. **Enable Advantage+ Catalog Ads at Campaign Level [10:50]:** Toggle Advantage+ Catalog Ads ON during campaign creation to give Meta’s AI full access to match dynamic items with user shopping intent [10:50].
3. **Use Dynamic Title & Price Tags [12:28]:** Format your Carousel headlines using `product.name` and `product.price` tags so the ad dynamically fills in accurate item details on a per-user basis [12:28 - 12:35].
4. **Use Advantage+ Destination [11:53]:** Enable Advantage+ destination in ad settings so traffic is automatically routed directly to the specific product detail page rather than a generic collection page [11:53 - 12:01].
5. **Rely on Pixel-Based Data Feed Syncing [13:10]:** Connect your catalog directly via Pixel/E-commerce platform feeds rather than manual CSV uploads to guarantee real-time stock removal and price updates [06:33, 13:10].
