## Title & channel
How to Run Profitable Facebook Ads for Clothing Brands in 2026 [Step by Step] - BitBranding [00:00]

## One-line thesis
Manual targeting (interests, lookalikes) is dead; the only way to scale in 2024/2026 is using "Broad" CBO campaigns where high-intent creative hooks perform the targeting for you. [01:11, 03:19]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Avatar Research:** Use Claude AI and WhisperFlow to build a detailed customer avatar based on real customer language, pain points, and objections. [11:02]
2. **Hook Generation:** Create 10 variations of hooks (Pain Point, Curiosity, Social Proof, Direct Offer) using Claude AI based on the avatar. [12:33]
3. **Creative Briefing:** Use AI to build a full brief including a 30-second UGC script, static image copy, and a 3-card carousel. [13:39]
4. **Campaign Setup:** Build one Sales campaign using CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization). [16:16]
5. **Ad Set Setup:** Create 3-5 "Broad" ad sets (No interest targeting, no lookalikes). [06:37]
6. **Launch & Wait:** Launch at $30-$50/day and let it run for 7 days before touching any settings. [16:05]
7. **Optimization:** Analyze performance based on Cost per Purchase. Pause losing ads/ad sets. [18:47]
8. **Scaling:** Increase budget by 5% daily if the 3-day trailing performance is above the break-even point. [18:02]

## Creative specifics
*   **Core Philosophy:** "Creative is the targeting." The words in the hook and copy provide signals to Meta's algorithm to find the right buyers. [03:19, 13:35]
*   **Formats:** 
    *   **UGC Video:** 30-second script, iPhone-style, natural light, customer talking to camera. [13:41, 14:37]
    *   **Static Image:** Focused on a heavy hook headline with high-contrast visuals. [13:51, 14:55]
    *   **Carousel:** 3-card structure. Card 1: Problem (Pain point). Card 2: Proof (Social). Card 3: Offer (Conversion). [14:02, 15:02]
*   **Hooks:** Must be under 8 seconds to read aloud. Categories include: Pain point, Curiosity, Social Proof, and Direct Offer. [12:45]
*   **Iteration:** Use a "Claude to Ads Manager" loop. If an ad fails, go deeper into Claude avatar research to find sharper hooks. [18:57]

## Mistakes / warnings called out
*   **Old Playbook:** Do not use interest stacks, manual audiences, or detailed targeting. These "put a ceiling" on the algorithm. [00:23, 03:15]
*   **Overcomplicating:** Avoid complex funnel structures (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU) with manual exclusions. Meta reads the copy to determine funnel position automatically. [03:25, 08:34]
*   **ABO Campaigns:** Ad set budget optimization is called "inefficient" for brands with smaller daily spends ($100-$300). [04:52]
*   **Editing too soon:** Do not touch the campaign for at least 3-7 days. [16:05, 18:34]
*   **Vanity Metrics:** Ignore CTR and CPM; focus solely on Purchase metrics. [18:41]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Targeting is Automated:** Broad targeting (Age/Gender/Location only) is now superior to human-selected interest groups. [03:00]
2. **Copy Drives the Pixel:** Meta uses the literal words in your primary text and headlines as targeting signals to find your audience. [18:12]
3. **Claude for Research:** High-performance creative is built on real customer language ("Shrunken old-man golf gear") rather than generic marketing speak. [12:05]
4. **The CBO Power:** Use one CBO campaign for the entire account to let Meta distribute the budget to the winning creative automatically. [06:27]
5. **Slow Scaling:** Protect profitability by scaling winners in small 5% increments based on 3-day trailing data, rather than massive jumps. [18:02]
