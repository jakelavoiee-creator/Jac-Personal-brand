## One-line thesis
Identify creative fatigue using Meta’s Opportunity Score and refresh ad content with new angles (use cases, benefits, and diverse talent) to lower costs and expand reach. [00:54]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Identify Performance Drop:** Monitor campaigns for declining performance (higher costs/lower reach) which often indicates creative fatigue. [00:03]
2. **Access Opportunity Score:** Open Meta Ads Manager and navigate to the "Opportunity score" column. [00:58]
3. **Locate Fatigue Warnings:** Look for the "Ad warning" icon next to specific ads. Hover over the "Creative fatigue" label to see the impact on your score. [00:59]
4. **Review Recommendations:** View the "Top recommendations" pop-up. In the example, resolving fatigue offers a "+10 points" increase to the Opportunity Score. [01:00]
5. **Apply Changes:** Click the "Apply in Ads Manager" button within the recommendation window. [01:02]
6. **Upload Diverse Creative:** Replace or add new creative assets that differ from the original in perspective or subject matter. [01:03]

## Creative specifics
* **Formats:** The video shows a mix of static images (lifestyle photography of handbags and sunglasses) and mobile-optimized vertical video (UGC style). [01:03]
* **Creative Iteration Framework:** To refresh content, change the approach using these four angles: [00:41]
    1. **Highlight a different product benefit:** Change the "why" behind the purchase.
    2. **Show a new use case:** Demonstrate the product in a different environment or scenario.
    3. **Offer a fresh perspective:** Change the visual or narrative point of view.
    4. **Feature different people and situations:** Use diverse talent and settings to appeal to new audience segments.
* **Creative Volume:** The video demonstrates moving from a single "fatigued" ad to a suite of four or more diverse creatives (stills and video). [01:04]

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **The "Pause" Mistake:** Don't simply pause top-spending ads when they show signs of fatigue; instead, use it as a signal to improve and refresh the creative. [00:24]
* **Fatigue Definition:** Fatigue occurs when the audience has seen the same creative "too many times," which is inherently connected to reach and frequency. [00:08]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Monitor Top Spenders Closely:** Your highest-budget ads will likely hit creative fatigue first because they reach the most people the fastest. [00:20]
2. **Don't Kill, Just Refresh:** Avoid pausing high-performing campaigns that start to dip; use the Opportunity Score to verify if "fatigue" is the cause and simply swap in new visuals. [00:24]
3. **Diversify Your "Angles":** For a clothing brand, don't just swap one model for another. Change the *use case* (e.g., from a studio shoot to an outdoor lifestyle shoot) or the *benefit* highlighted. [00:41]
4. **Use the Opportunity Score as a Roadmap:** Meta explicitly provides a "+Points" system in Ads Manager to tell you which ads need creative updates to improve account health. [01:01]
5. **Mix Formats:** Successfully resolving fatigue involves moving from a single image to a variety of "diverse creatives," including both statics and video. [01:04]
