This guide outlines the "Meta Andromeda" scaling strategy as presented by Manel Gómez. It focuses on bypassing the "Ad Retrieval" bottleneck through high-volume creative diversification and high-integrity data signals.

## Title & channel
* **Video:** [How To Scale With Meta Andromeda: Creative Diversification & Signal Quality Explained (With Examples)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O8Doria9dic)
* **Channel:** Manel Gómez

## One-line thesis
Scaling under the Andromeda algorithm requires moving away from "winner iterations" toward high-volume Horizontal and Vertical creative diversification combined with real-time, high-match-quality data signals. [00:05, 05:00]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Technical Foundation:** Ensure Conversions API (CAPI) is active and that "Automatic Advanced Matching" is fully enabled in Events Manager to maximize signal strength. [14:15]
2. **Consolidate into CBOs:** Move away from fragmented ABO testing. Create CBO campaigns containing 20–30 highly diverse creatives. [03:45, 04:32]
3. **Execute Horizontal Diversification:** Populate the campaign with different ad formats (static, video, carousel), visual styles, and different "avatars" (types of people) to appeal to different user behaviors. [05:10]
4. **Execute Vertical Diversification:** Include specific creatives designed for different funnel stages: Top of Funnel (Problem Aware), Middle of Funnel (Solution Aware), and Bottom of Funnel (Product/Offer Aware). [05:25]
5. **Implement Manual Bidding:** Use Bid Caps or Cost Caps (e.g., $70, $150, or $200) to control costs while the algorithm tests the large creative pool. [02:30, 03:45]
6. **Audit Visual Congruence:** Ensure the people featured in the ads visually match the target demographic to avoid misleading the "Ad Retrieval" engine. [12:35]

## Meta ads specifics
* **Account Structure:** CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) is preferred for scaling. One shown example is a "Full Funnel" campaign containing 3 ad sets and 30 ads. [04:32]
* **Campaign Objective:** Sales/Purchases.
* **Budgets:** Examples show daily budgets of $120, $190, and $320, scaling to over $13,000 in total spend during peak periods (Black Friday). [02:30, 03:14]
* **Bidding:** Manual bidding is central. 
    * Bid Caps: $70, $120, $200. [02:35, 03:45]
    * Cost Caps: $150. [03:45]
* **Targeting:** Broad/Full Funnel. The strategy relies on creative to perform the targeting. [11:30]
* **Attribution:** Uses standard Meta attribution, but emphasizes the importance of "Data Freshness" being set to "Real Time" within the platform. [14:40]

## Creative specifics
* **Volume:** Strategies involve mixing 20–30 ads within a single campaign to feed the retrieval engine. [03:45, 04:32]
* **Horizontal Diversification:** Ad format, visuals, message, concept, and avatar. [05:10]
* **Vertical Diversification:** Targeting the full funnel (TOF, MOF, BOF) via awareness levels (Problem, Solution, Product aware). [05:25]
* **The "Dr. Squatch" Framework (Ideal Mix):**
    * **Prospective/TOF:** Listicle videos ("Top 3 soaps"), Studio-shot high production, UGC unboxing/hauls. [08:05]
    * **Engagement/MOF:** Memes, product demos/ingredient breakdowns, street interviews, podcast-style ads. [08:30]
    * **Retargeting/BOF:** Static reviews, "Order More Save More" graphics (up to 51% off), "New Customer" specific offers, and free gift callouts. [08:50]

## Metrics & benchmarks quoted
* **ROAS:** Aim for >2 consistently. Examples shown include 2.70, 3.48, 4.29, and 5.17. [02:37, 03:50]
* **CPM:** Diversified campaigns achieved a $26.75 CPM in the US during Black Friday, compared to $37–$49 for standard campaigns. [03:25]
* **Event Match Quality:** Must be >9/10 for Purchase events. [14:30]
* **Ad Volume:** 22 statics mix or 20 ads mix cited as baseline "diversified" counts. [03:45]

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **The "Winner Iteration" Trap:** Finding one winning ad format (e.g., a listicle) and producing 20 slight variations of it. This fails because the Andromeda "Ad Retrieval" engine sees them as the same "signal," limiting reach to a single audience pocket. [06:00, 07:15]
* **Visual Mismatch:** Using 18-year-old models when selling to 40+ women. This sends the wrong signal to the Ad Retrieval engine, causing you to lose auctions for your actual target audience. [12:35]
* **Bad Technical Signals:** Tracking very little data, having poor deduplication, or having data freshness set to "Hourly" rather than "Real Time." [12:15, 14:45]
* **Fast Pausing:** Pausing campaigns too quickly before they can accumulate enough signal/data. [12:22]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Abandon the "Winning Hook" Loop:** Instead of making 10 variations of your best-performing UGC video, add a meme, a studio-shot editorial, and a press-review static to the same campaign. [06:10]
2. **Mirror Your Customer:** If your best fashion customers are 35+ women, stop using 20-year-old influencers exclusively. Visual congruence is a primary signal for the retrieval engine. [12:35]
3. **The 20-30 Rule:** To truly test Andromeda, you need a high volume (20+) of *meaningfully different* creatives (Vertical + Horizontal) in one campaign. [03:45]
4. **Fix Technical Debt:** If your Purchase Match Quality is below 9/10 or your CAPI is set to "Hourly" updates, you are significantly overpaying for CPMs. [14:30]
5. **Use Caps to Scale:** When running 30+ ads in a CBO, use Bid Caps or Cost Caps to prevent the algorithm from overspending on unproven creative concepts. [02:30]
