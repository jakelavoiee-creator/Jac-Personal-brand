This guide is based on the Meta "Ask an Expert" video featuring Allison Blumenfeld, a Product Manager from NYC. It details the process for using Advantage+ sales campaigns to acquire new customers during high-competition shopping periods.

## Title & channel
* **Title:** Ask an Expert: Advantage+ sales campaigns
* **Channel:** Meta for Business

## One-line thesis
Use Advantage+ sales campaigns combined with existing customer exclusions to leverage Meta’s AI for net-new customer acquisition during peak shopping moments. [00:11]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Initialize Campaign:** Select the "Advantage+ sales campaign" option to enable Meta’s most advanced AI optimizations. [00:15]
2. **Configure Advantage+ Settings:** Ensure "Advantage+ on" is active for Budget, Audience, and Placements. [00:20]
3. **Define Exclusions:** Navigate to "Custom audiences" and create a new segment. [00:27]
4. **Select Exclusion Criteria:** Choose "People who haven't engaged with my brand" or people who have already purchased to focus spend strictly on new prospects. [00:29]
5. **Set Demographics:** Set Age (e.g., 18–65+) and Gender (e.g., All). [00:30]
6. **Review Audience Definition:** Check the gauge to ensure the audience has shifted from "Broad" to a more specific "Net New" segment. [00:32]
7. **Fine-tune Levers:** Adjust the Budget slider and Audience definition to align with business goals. [01:02]

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Wasted Spend:** Do not waste budget on audiences who have already purchased when your goal is new customer acquisition. [00:38]
* **Manual Setup:** Avoid heavy manual setup; use Advantage+ to reduce the "manual" burden during high-competition moments. [00:18]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Automate During Peaks:** Use Advantage+ sales campaigns during "key shopping moments" (like Black Friday or product drops) when competition is highest. [00:04]
2. **Hard-Exclude Past Buyers:** Use the "Existing customer exclusions" lever to ensure 100% of your budget is hunting for "net new" customers. [00:49]
3. **Trust Broad AI:** When "Advantage+ on" is active, Meta uses default setups or suggestions to find the right people at the right time. [00:25]
4. **Use All Placements:** Don't cherry-pick placements (like IG Stories only); enable all placements to let the AI find the cheapest conversions across the ecosystem. [00:25]
5. **Simplicity is a Lever:** Focus on three main controls: Budget, Audience (Exclusions), and Placements. [01:01]
