## One-line thesis
In Meta’s post-Andromeda environment, clothing brands must stop using outdated ad-set budget testing and surface-level color tweaks, structuring accounts around concept-level ad sets with 20% visual/copy variations that target individual buyer personas while scaling budget strictly by 5% daily based on 3-day average ROAS [01:00–01:54, 09:28–09:32, 16:14–16:47].

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Set Up Account Hierarchy [01:36–02:07, 06:36–06:41]:**
   * **Campaign:** Broad performance/conversion campaign.
   * **Ad Set:** Defined by a single creative "Concept" or hypothesis.
   * **Ads:** Individual creative variations under that concept aimed at specific customer profiles.
2. **Select Concept Frameworks [03:05–04:53, 06:43–06:50]:**
   * Utilize one dominant angle from structured frameworks (e.g., 4F Lens, Moment + Motion, Body Identity Archetype, Price Psychology, Proof, or Offer Mechanics).
3. **Execute Creative Production Loop (20% Rule) [08:58–10:30]:**
   * **Imitate:** Take proven winning layout formats from the market (e.g., features & benefits grids, before/afters, bullet points).
   * **Iterate:** Change at least **20%** of the visual/copy/angle elements (headline, call-out, format, layout) rather than just background colors.
   * **Innovate:** Develop unique brand concepts sparingly based on insights gathered during iteration.
4. **Establish Launch Cadence [16:05–16:13]:**
   * Launch all new ad sets and creative variations on the **exact same day every week** (Mondays recommended) to maintain system stability.
5. **Apply Daily Incremental Scaling [16:14–16:47]:**
   * Review performance over a trailing 3-day window.
   * If the 3-day average ROAS exceeds the target ROAS, increase the daily campaign budget by **5%**.
   * Repeat daily.

## Creative specifics

### Formats & Layouts [06:07–06:27, 07:50–08:02, 17:09–17:20]
* Carousels
* Single Static Images (Flat lays, on-rack, multi-panel grids, 3-product + 1-model composite)
* Short-form Videos
* Text Graphics / Headlines
* "Us vs. Them" Comparison Cards

### Creative Frameworks & Angles [03:05–04:53, 06:48–06:50]
1. **4F Lens (Apparel Specific):**
   * **Fit:** Tailored, boxy, relaxed, petite, tall.
   * **Fabric:** Organic cotton, heavy GSM, stretch blend.
   * **Function:** All-day comfort, gym-ready, airport travel outfit.
   * **Fashion:** Minimalist, trend-forward, seasonal transition.
2. **Moment + Motion:** Real-life environment combined with a physical motion that drives the visual hook (e.g., jumping, stretching, walking).
3. **Body Identity Archetype:** Direct visual/copy targeting for specific body types (Tall, Petite, Curvy, Athletic) or lifestyles (Skater, New Mom, Minimalist).
4. **Care + Longevity Ladder:** Demonstrating wash durability ("100 washes, 1 shape"), seam strength, or anti-shrinkage.
5. **Price Psychology Anchors:** Cost-per-wear breakdown, Pro-standard vs. Luxury comparisons.
6. **Proof Library:** Torture tests, extreme stretch tests, washer/dryer comparison timelapses, lab tests.
7. **Offer Mechanics:**
   * Buy 2 Shirts, Get 1 Free
   * Buy 1 Hoodie, Get a Free Shirt
   * Buy 2 Hoodies, Get a Free Hat
   * Free brand pins/accessories included with hoodie purchases

### Iteration Rule (20% Variation) [09:28–09:32, 10:13–10:25, 17:00–17:04]
* Avoid minor background color changes (e.g., swapping blue for orange).
* Ensure ad iterations differ by at least **20%** in headline text, call-out hooks, visual layout structure, or format type so Meta’s Andromeda algorithm recognizes them as distinct creative concepts.

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Using Legacy Ad-Set Budget Testing [01:00–01:17]:** Ad-set level budget testing, forced spend across every ad, and creating separate campaigns for micro-variables no longer work under Andromeda.
2. **Superficial Color Swaps [10:13–10:30]:** Changing only the background color (e.g., blue to green) on an identical graphic gets suppressed by Meta's algorithm and receives zero spend.
3. **Over-Innovating Too Early [09:33–09:43]:** Spending time trying to invent completely novel ad formats instead of making 20% iterations on existing proven market concepts.
4. **Delaying Action to the Next Calendar Year [08:33–08:52]:** Waiting until January (typically a slow month for clothing) instead of executing campaign changes during Q4/December.

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Structure Accounts by Concept, Not Targeting:** Group ads into ad sets where each ad set represents a unique marketing concept/hypothesis, and let broad targeting find the individual customer for each ad [01:36–02:08].
2. **Apply the 20% Creative Iteration Rule:** When building creative variations, alter at least 20% of the visual layout, hook, or format. Do not rely on simple background color swaps [09:28–09:32, 10:13–10:25].
3. **Use Apparel-Specific Angles:** Structure copy and visual briefs around apparel frameworks like the **4F Lens** (Fit, Fabric, Function, Fashion) and **Body Identity Archetypes** [03:09–03:44].
4. **Scale Budgets Incrementally at 5% Daily:** Base budget increases on a trailing 3-day average ROAS/CPA rather than single-day spikes to maintain campaign stability [16:14–16:47].
5. **Maintain Weekly Launch Discipline:** Batch creative testing and launch new ad variations on the same day every week (e.g., every Monday) to build a consistent pipeline of winning creative [16:05–16:13].
