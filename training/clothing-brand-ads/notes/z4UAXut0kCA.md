## One-line thesis
Post-Andromeda, Meta ad testing must prioritize launching 80% completely new creative concepts and 20% variations (flipped from pre-Andromeda) using broad-targeted CBO campaigns segmented strictly by ad concept [00:48, 01:25].

---

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Shift Strategy [00:48]:** Shift your creative testing ratio from 80% variations / 20% new concepts to **80% new concepts / 20% variations**.
2. **Campaign Setup [01:23, 05:48]:** 
   * Go to Meta Ads Manager -> Click **Create** -> Select **Sales** objective [05:51].
   * Enable **Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO)** (referred to in Ads Manager as Campaign Budget) [01:27, 06:00].
3. **Determine Budget [04:58]:** 
   * Count total ads across the campaign. Multiply total ads by **$10** to get the CBO daily budget [05:08].
   * *Example:* 3 ad sets $\times$ 3 ads per set = 9 total ads = **$90/day** daily budget [05:13]. (Absolute minimum CBO daily budget is **$50/day**) [05:31].
4. **Ad Set Level Setup [01:48, 06:10]:**
   * Set Conversion location: **Website** [06:12].
   * Performance Goal: **Maximize number of conversions** [06:15].
   * Select your **Pixel** / Data set [06:17].
   * Conversion Event: **Purchase** [06:21].
   * Attribution Window: **7-day click, 1-day view**; turn off view-through / engaged view if separate [06:36].
   * Schedule: Set start time for **6:00 AM EST the next day** [06:51].
   * Location: **United States** (or target country) [07:05].
   * Audience/Demographics: **Broad** (Age: 18–65+, Gender: All / Male & Female) [02:34, 07:13].
   * Placements: **Advantage+ Placements** [02:31, 07:25].
5. **Ad Level Setup [03:09, 07:32]:**
   * Format: **Single image or video** [07:39].
   * Enable **Multi-advertiser ads** [07:41].
   * Destination: Input Website URL. **Uncheck "Optimize website destination"** [08:24].
   * Browser Add-ons: Set to **None** [08:33].
   * **Turn OFF all AI / Advantage+ Creative Enhancements [08:44, 09:20]:**
     * Disable Advantage+ creative image generation [09:16].
     * Disable overlays, visual touch-ups, music, text improvements, and animations [09:25].
   * Primary Text / Headline / Description: Fill in manually [09:08].
   * Call to Action: **Learn More** (or *Shop Now* for direct-to-cart offers) [09:36, 09:48].
6. **Duplication Workflow [09:58]:**
   * Create 1 ad completely.
   * Duplicate the ad 2 times within the same ad set (swap out creative assets to make 3 variations of Concept 1) [10:02].
   * Duplicate the Ad Set 2 times at the campaign level (swap out the creative concepts so Ad Set 2 = Concept 2 variations, Ad Set 3 = Concept 3 variations) [10:08].
   * Launch CBO with 3 Ad Sets / 9 Total Ads scheduled for 6 AM next day [10:14].

## Meta ads specifics
* **Account Structure:** 1 CBO Campaign $\rightarrow$ 3 to 5 Ad Sets (1 Ad Set per unique Creative Concept) $\rightarrow$ 2 to 5 Ads per Ad Set (Variations of that concept) [01:48, 03:13, 04:06].
* **Campaign Objective:** Sales (optimized for Purchase) [05:51, 06:21].
* **Budget Strategy:** CBO level budget. Calculated as **$10 per total ad** in the campaign [04:59]. Hard minimum floor of **$50/day** per CBO [05:31].
* **Bidding / Optimization:** Maximize number of conversions (Highest Volume) [06:15].
* **Targeting:** Completely Broad (No interest targeting, no Lookalikes). Location: US, Age: 18–65+, All Genders [02:30, 07:05, 07:13].
* **Placements:** Advantage+ Placements [02:31, 07:25].
* **Testing Cadence / Schedule:** Schedule campaigns to launch at 6:00 AM EST the following morning [06:51].
* **Attribution Window:** 7-day click, 1-day view (View-through disabled/turned off) [06:36].
* **Scaling & Kill Rules:** 
  * Let CBO distribute budget across concepts automatically [01:36, 13:20].
  * If Meta gives zero or very little spend to an ad or concept, it generally signifies Meta's algorithm evaluated the creative quality as lower relative to competing ads in the account [13:36].
  * *(Note: Additional scaling/killing rules are covered in an unlisted video claimed to be sent via Instagram DM keyword "LAW" to `@markbuildsbrands`)* [10:52].

## Creative specifics
* **Formats:** Images or Videos [04:17].
* **Segmentation Rule:** **Do NOT mix images and videos inside the same CBO/Ad Set [04:28].** Run pure image CBOs or pure video CBOs separately [04:48].
* **Concept vs. Variation Definition [00:32]:**
  * **New Concepts:** Completely unique creative material, hooks, and messaging angles [00:41].
  * **Variations:** Minor tweaks (e.g., small editing changes, color swaps, copy tweaks) to an existing winning concept [00:37].
* **Volume:** 3 to 5 unique concepts per testing CBO; 2 to 5 variations per concept ad set [04:06, 04:12].
* **Creative Enhancements:** Turn OFF all automated Meta/Advantage+ enhancements, AI image generation, automated music, visual touch-ups, and overlays [08:44, 09:20].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
1. **Mixing Images and Videos in the Same CBO/Ad Set [04:33]:** Videos will cannibalize all ad spend, preventing static images from getting properly tested [04:39].
2. **Running Paid Ads on Under $50/day Budget [05:31]:** Running CBOs below $50/day does not yield enough data. "If you don't have enough cash set aside to run a $50/day CBO, you should not be running paid ads." [05:38].
3. **Leaving Meta's Advantage+ Creative Enhancements ON [07:50, 08:44, 09:20]:** Meta pushes automated AI enhancements, music, visual touch-ups, and overlays that ruin ad quality and waste money [07:58].
4. **Leaving "Optimize Website Destination" ON [08:24]:** Meta will send traffic to alternative destinations outside your intended domain/landing page [08:27].
5. **Over-testing Variations instead of Concepts [14:05]:** Relying on slight variations post-Andromeda is no longer effective; unique creative concepts drive results [13:08].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Prioritize Concept Diversity Over Tweaks:** Dedicate 80% of your production pipeline to testing entirely unique visual concepts (angles, hooks, distinct styling/settings) and only 20% to minor variations.
2. **Segment Ad Sets by Concept, Not Targeting:** Keep targeting broad (18–65+, M/F, US, Advantage+ placements) across all ad sets. Use individual ad sets within a CBO to isolate and test different *creative concepts* rather than different audiences.
3. **Fund CBOs Based on Total Ad Count:** Calculate your CBO daily budget by multiplying the total number of ads across all ad sets by $10 (e.g., 9 total ads = $90/day), ensuring a minimum baseline of $50/day.
4. **Separate Static Images and Videos:** Never put image ads and video ads inside the same ad set or CBO campaign; video formats will hog the budget and starve your image tests.
5. **Disable Meta's Auto-Enhancements:** Manually opt out of Meta's Advantage+ creative enhancements, AI image generation, automated music, visual touch-ups, and "Optimize website destination" to keep full control over your fashion brand's aesthetic and landing page routing.
