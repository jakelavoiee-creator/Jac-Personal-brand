## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Apply the 80/20 rule to hero products:** Identify the 20% of products in your collection that offer high perceived value, broad market appeal, and strong profit margins, then focus 80% of your CRO and marketing efforts on optimizing those specific landing pages [[02:30]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=150s)–[[03:25]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=205s).
2. **Capture exhaustive product media:** Shoot clean flat-lays on neutral backgrounds, detailed close-ups of technical features (zips, pockets, fabric texture), and full-fit lifestyle photos including styling with footwear [[04:13]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=253s)–[[04:30]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=270s).
3. **Embed lifestyle/use-case video clips:** Add split-screen video loops or embedded GIFs showing models using the product in action (e.g., working out, running, casual wear, office) to help buyers visualize ownership [[11:31]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=691s)–[[11:53]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=713s).
4. **Implement multi-layered sizing guides:**
   * Display model height, weight, and size worn right below the color/size selector [[06:11]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=371s)–[[06:21]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=381s).
   * Provide a modal size guide with full garment measurements (waist, inseam, cuff opening) and a toggle for inches/cm [[06:39]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=399s)–[[06:48]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=408s).
   * For unisex/lifestyle apparel, display both male and female model dimensions [[13:02]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=782s)–[[13:12]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=792s).
5. **Surface BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later) options above the fold:** Put installment cost breakdowns (Klarna/Clearpay) directly under the product price before the customer reaches the cart [[05:14]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=314s)–[[05:37]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=337s).
6. **Design a high-contrast Call-to-Action (CTA) button:** Use a prominent black CTA button on a white background (or high-contrast color) with bold text ("ADD TO CART") and optional stock/delivery status badges [[14:49]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=889s)–[[15:42]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=942s).
7. **Handle purchase objections directly under the CTA:** Add micro-copy icons/bullets for "In Stock", "Free Shipping", "Easy Returns", and "Loyalty Points Earned" [[06:59]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=419s)–[[07:26]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=446s).
8. **Organize product details into collapsible tabs:** Group technical specifications, FAQs, and shipping/return policies into clean expandable dropdowns to keep the page scannable [[09:51]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=591s)–[[11:30]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=690s).
9. **Integrate an onsite LTV / Loyalty Rewards program:** Display a rewards pop-up (e.g., Prestige Club) directly on the product page to incentivize account registration and repeat purchases [[07:40]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=460s)–[[09:48]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=588s).
10. **Build layered social proof:** Feature star ratings above the fold [[13:35]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=815s), customer fit-rating sliders [[04:36]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=276s), and real customer photo/video reviews below the fold [[15:43]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=943s)–[[15:51]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=951s).

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Spending tens of thousands on web developers:** Unnecessary when CRO frameworks can be executed manually or using Shopify app integrations [[00:53]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=53s)–[[01:06]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=66s).
* **Relying solely on Meta ads for new customer acquisition:** Ignoring repeat buyer retention and LTV engines makes scaling unsustainable as acquisition costs rise [[07:44]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=464s)–[[08:34]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=514s).
* **Hiding BNPL and price breakdowns until checkout:** Failing to show broken-down installments on the PDP leaves price friction unaddressed [[05:38]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=338s)–[[05:50]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=350s).
* **Cluttering the landing page with unorganized text:** Long walls of product text should be placed inside clean accordion tabs to avoid overwhelming mobile/desktop users [[10:08]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=608s)–[[10:20]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=620s).
* **Low-contrast CTA buttons:** Buttons that blend into the background reduce click-through rates to cart [[15:23]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=923s)–[[15:34]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=934s).
* **Waiting passively for reviews:** Small brands shouldn't complain about a lack of reviews—they should manually call early customers on the phone and offer small incentives to secure their first 20 reviews [[14:14]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=854s)–[[14:30]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=870s).

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Fix CRO Before Scaling Spend:** Doubling your landing page conversion rate doubles your revenue from the exact same ad budget and traffic volume [[01:36]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=96s)–[[01:43]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=103s).
2. **Direct Cold Traffic to High-Margin Hero Items:** Direct Meta ad spend to items with low production costs ($10–$15) and high retail price points (£130+). High profit margins generate the cash flow needed to sustain higher customer acquisition costs [[03:20]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=200s)–[[03:58]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=238s).
3. **Eliminate Sizing Doubts Above the Fold:** Sizing uncertainty is the primary friction point for fashion ads. Address it on the PDP using model weight/height stats, imperial/metric measurement charts, and review-backed fit sliders [[06:04]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=364s)–[[06:48]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=408s), [[13:13]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=793s)–[[13:33]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=813s).
4. **Kill Price Friction On-Page:** Display BNPL installment costs (Klarna/Clearpay) directly under the price so buyers know their immediate out-of-pocket cost before clicking "Add to Cart" [[05:14]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=314s)–[[05:50]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=350s).
5. **Build LTV Integrations On the PDP:** Integrate loyalty rewards prompts on the product page to convert cold ad visitors into repeat buyers via email, lowering your blended CAC [[07:40]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=460s)–[[09:08]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHAHjtfKJjQ&t=548s).
