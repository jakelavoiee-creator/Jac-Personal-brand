## Mistakes / warnings called out
* Don't assume you need a web developer and tens of thousands of dollars to optimize your pages [00:53].
* Don't ignore landing page quality while pouring more money into ads [01:46].
* Don't rely solely on your own brand's opinion; customers trust peer reviews over brand claims [04:40].
* Don't hide pricing breakdowns; show buy-now-pay-later pricing upfront to handle price objections [05:22].
* Don't assume customers don't read size guides; multiple sizing formats are essential to reduce sizing friction [06:05].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Double your sales without spending more on ads** by fixing your landing page conversion rate rather than just acquiring new traffic [01:36].
2. **Apply the 80/20 rule to CRO:** Focus 80% of your conversion optimization efforts on your top 20% best-selling products [02:38].
3. **Leverage social proof above the fold** (star ratings, review highlights, and real customer photos) because customers trust peers over brand claims [04:40].
4. **Handle price objections upfront** by displaying clear buy-now-pay-later (Klarna/Clearpay) installment amounts directly under the product price [05:22].
5. **Reduce purchase friction with detailed sizing and fit guides** (including model height, weight, and size worn, plus toggleable inch/cm charts) to eliminate sizing uncertainty [06:12].
