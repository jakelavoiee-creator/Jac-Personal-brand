## One-line thesis
Meta’s Andromeda ad-delivery algorithm has shifted optimization from audience targeting to creative quality, volume, and diversity—requiring fashion brands to keep core brand values intact while wildly diversifying their creative formats, personas, angles, and volume.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Understand Andromeda:** Recognize that Meta rebuilt its AI ad-delivery system around creative rather than interest or lookalike targeting [00:02–00:19].
2. **Audit current creative diversity:** Check if current ads look identical (e.g., 5 variations of the same UGC video or the same product-on-white photography) [04:00–05:25].
3. **Map brand values vs. brand outfits:** Keep your brand values, mission statement, reason for existing, and ideal customer profile (ICP) identical, but completely vary your "outfits" (ad formats, visual styles, tones, production quality, settings, models, angles, personas) [10:03–10:55].
4. **Build the creative matrix across 3 variables:**
   - **Personas (Ps):** Identify who the product serves and why they want it (e.g., Quality, Identity, Occasion) [13:34–15:23].
   - **Angles (As):** Map persona to pain points to product solution to angles (e.g., "Zero tarnishing," "Buying X to be part of [look or group]," "Elevate your look to what's trending," "Get for a gift / The best Valentine's gift") [15:24–16:31].
   - **Formats (Fs):** Decide how the ad is delivered (e.g., iPhone Try-on, HiFi Static, Meme style, Lofi Static, Founder Led, Street Interview, GRWM, How-To Vid) [16:32–17:08].
5. **Calculate creative volume:** Multiply Ps × As × Fs to generate a high volume of diverse creatives (e.g., 3 Ps × 3 As × 8 Fs = 72 diverse creatives) [17:09–17:47].
6. **Refresh non-negotiably:** Inject fresh content and new creative concepts regularly to prevent audience fatigue and algorithmic penalties [06:47–07:18].

## Meta ads specifics (account structure, campaign objective, budgets, bidding, targeting, placements, testing cadence, scaling rules, kill rules, attribution)
- **Targeting shift:** Life before Andromeda = interest targeting, lookalike targeting, creative targeting [00:58, 01:45–02:13]. Life after Andromeda = creative quality and creative diversity drive performance; interest and lookalike targeting will not save you [03:35–03:59].
- **Audience / Auction volume:** 3.5 billion users, 10 million advertisers on Meta [02:13–02:21]. Meta decides within seconds which specific ad to serve to a specific user while allowing 10 million advertisers to upload new creative [02:21–02:32].
- **Algorithm engine reads 4 variables (emojis shown on screen):**
  - Visual features in creative (eyes emoji) [02:48, 08:02–08:35]
  - Audio (ear emoji) [02:51]
  - Motion within the ad / who's in it (running person emoji) [02:53]
  - Copy / text hooks (notepad and pencil emoji) [02:56]
- **Tracking / Signals:** Meta tracks engagement signals against the user's specific profile to build a preference model [08:02–08:39]:
  - Views (how long users linger on a post) [08:08]
  - Clicks and taps [08:12]
  - Shares, likes, comments, DMs [08:14]
  - Zooming in and out (why some big brands run "look at the top left corner" ads that force zoom interactions) [08:16–08:29]
- **Algorithmic data structure:** Algorithm acts like a 10D to 15D multidimensional table consisting of a ton of different interests the user has shown over time (visual cluster diagram shown on screen with circles for "interested in running," "athletic wear," "basketball," "bodybuilding") [08:40–09:53].
- **Kill rules for creatives:** If an ad/format compromises your brand values, don't run it [12:56–13:07]. If your ad format results in no fresh content, you get penalized (literally word-for-word by Meta) [07:04–07:18].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Creative is the targeting:** In the post-Andromeda era, interest and lookalike targeting take a backseat; the algorithm reads visual features, audio, motion, and copy to match ads to users [02:46–03:59].
2. **Preserve brand values, change brand outfits:** Your core brand values, mission, and ICP can stay identical, but the formats, models, settings, and visual styles of your ads must vary drastically [10:03–10:55].
3. **Diversity beats repetition:** Running multiple variations of the exact same style of ad causes reach to stall; diversifying across personas, angles, and formats unlocks net-new audience pockets [04:30–06:19].
4. **Volume drives luck:** Producing higher volumes of net-new creative concepts (e.g., 40+ per month vs. 4) exponentially increases your chances of finding new profitable audience segments [06:20–06:46].
5. **Fresh content is non-negotiable:** Sticking to stale ads leads to audience fatigue and algorithmic penalization, whereas combining quality with diversity yields 7.3x lower CPAs [06:47–07:44].
