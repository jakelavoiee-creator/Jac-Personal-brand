## Title & channel
* **Title:** New Way To Test Meta Ads Post-Andromeda
* **Channel:** [Ben Heath](https://www.youtube.com/@BenHeath)

## One-line thesis
Use Meta’s built-in "Creative testing" tool at the ad level to force delivery and spend onto minor creative variations (like hooks or text overlays) that the Andromeda update would otherwise suppress for lack of "creative diversity."

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Campaign Setup:** Create a standard campaign and ad set (e.g., Leads or Sales) [03:14].
2. **Access the Ad Level:** Navigate to the individual ad settings within Ads Manager [03:15].
3. **Locate Creative Testing:** Scroll down beneath the "Ad creative" section to find the "Creative testing" module [03:25].
4. **Configure Test Settings:**
    * Click "Set up test" [03:49].
    * **Number of ads:** Select between 2 and 5 versions to test [03:52].
    * **Budget Allocation:** Choose how much of the ad set budget should go to the test ads (e.g., 100% or a specific portion) [04:02].
    * **Duration:** Set the test length (default is 7 days; adjust based on conversion volume) [04:16].
    * **Comparison Metric:** Select the key performance indicator (KPI). Change from the default "Cost per post engagement" to "Cost per lead" or "Cost per result" [04:41].
5. **Add Creative Variations:** Upload the specific variations (e.g., different hooks for the same video or color changes for the same image) to the newly created "Test - [Ad Name]" slots [05:56].
6. **Analyze & Scale:** Once the test duration completes, Meta identifies a winner. Scale the winning variation by moving it into a standard ad set alongside other diverse creative types [12:33].

## Meta ads specifics
* **Campaign Objective:** Leads campaign used as the example [03:14].
* **Ad Level Tool:** The "Creative testing" tool is found at the **ad level**, not the campaign or ad set level [03:10].
* **Andromeda Impact:** Meta now suppresses spend on ads that are too similar, favoring "creative diversity." The testing tool bypasses this by forcing delivery to each variant [03:44].
* **Budgets:** Example shown with a £25.00 daily budget [03:56].
* **Comparison Metrics Available:** Cost per lead, cost per result, cost per landing page view, cost per mobile app install, cost per checkout initiated, etc. [04:57].
* **Attribution Warning:** Ben Heath uses **Hyros** for tracking, noting that Meta’s native reporting can miss significant revenue (e.g., missing £58k of a £96k total in one example) due to recurring billing or cross-device issues [08:51].

## Creative specifics
* **Creative Diversity Requirement:** Andromeda requires ads to look visually different (e.g., Image vs. Video, different formats) or Meta will only spend on one [00:53].
* **Static Image Variations to Test:**
    * Text overlays [01:46].
    * Background colors [01:49].
    * Black & White vs. Color [01:52].
    * Presence/absence of a price banner [01:54].
* **Video "Hook" Testing:**
    * **The Hook:** The first ~3 seconds of a video [10:50].
    * **The Body:** The remaining content of the video [10:51].
    * **The Formula:** Create 2 full video "bodies" and 10 different "hooks" to generate 20 variations without filming 20 entirely new ads [11:18].
* **Volume:** High volume of testing is recommended; the channel claims to spend $15M/month across hundreds of accounts [00:14].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Default Metric Trap:** Do not leave the comparison metric on "Cost per post engagement." You must manually change it to your actual conversion goal (e.g., Cost per lead) [04:41].
* **Statistical Significance:** If conversion volume is low, you must increase either the budget or the test duration to get reliable data [05:35].
* **The "Guessing" Mistake:** Many advertisers have stopped testing minor variations and started "guessing" which text overlay is best because Meta suppresses spend on similar ads [02:37].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Don't Filming 20 Ads:** For a clothing brand, film 2 high-quality product demos and 10 different opening hooks (e.g., lifestyle, close-up, unboxing). Use the testing tool to find the winning hook [11:18].
2. **Force Minor Tests:** If you have a winning product photo, test 5 different background colors or text overlays ("Best Seller," "20% Off") using the "Creative testing" tool to ensure each gets spend [01:46].
3. **Use Accurate Metrics:** When testing lifestyle vs. studio shots, set the tool to "Cost per Purchase" or "Cost per Checkout Initiated" rather than engagement [04:41].
4. **Solve Attribution Gaps:** If running ads for a subscription clothing box or a brand with high repeat purchases, use 3rd-party tracking to see the true LTV impact of your ads [09:16].
5. **Quality over Quantity:** It is better to have a creative production schedule you can stick to (e.g., 2 new bodies and 5 hooks a week) than trying to produce 20 unique ads and burning out [14:31].
