## One-line thesis
Fashion dropshippers can transform the "returns crisis" into a competitive advantage by leveraging AI sizing technology, data-driven supplier management, and detailed product education to reduce return rates (which average 40%+) and increase customer LTV.

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Profitability is found in the "Back-end":** With return processing costing up to 66% of the item's price, ROAS is a vanity metric if return rates are high; focus on "Net-Sales" after returns.
2.  **Leverage AI for Conversion & Retention:** Implementing tools like Virtusize or AR try-ons acts as a "silent closer" on the landing page and reduces the 10% return leak.
3.  **Audit the "Bracketing" Behavior:** If customers are ordering 3 sizes to keep 1, your "Free Returns" policy may be cannibalizing your profit margins.
4.  **Source via Samples, not Photos:** Never scale a Meta campaign for a garment until a physical sample has been verified for fit consistency to avoid a "mass return" event during scaling.
5.  **Use Sizing as a Creative Hook:** Educational "How it Fits" videos and specific fit notes (e.g., "Runs Small - Size Up") should be integrated into product pages to build the 73% fit-confidence required by modern consumers.
