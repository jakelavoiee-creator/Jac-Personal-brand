## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Pre-Requisite: Organic Validation & Meta Pixel Setup** [00:10, 01:35]
   * Do not start paid ads immediately when launching a new clothing brand [00:30]. Build organic reach first by posting TikToks, Instagram Reels, selling locally in the community, and getting sales organically [00:15, 00:58]. Paid ads are solely meant to *scale* what is already working [01:14].
   * Download the Facebook and Instagram apps on your Shopify store and complete the setup to install your Meta Pixel on your website [01:39].

2. **Create Campaign & Objective** [01:51]
   * Go to Meta Ads Manager, click **+ Create** [01:51].
   * Select **Sales** as the campaign objective [01:53]. Click **Continue** [01:59].
   * Choose **Manual sales campaign** (Do NOT choose Advantage+ shopping campaign) [01:58]. Click **Continue** [02:03].

3. **Configure Campaign Level (CBO Setup)** [02:04]
   * **Advantage+ catalog ads:** Toggle **OFF** [02:21].
   * **Advantage campaign budget (CBO):** Toggle **ON** [02:22].
   * **Daily Budget:** Set to at least **$50.00 USD/day** [02:29].
   * **Campaign Bid Strategy:** Highest volume or value [02:40].
   * Leave all other settings (e.g., A/B test) alone/default [02:41].

4. **Configure Ad Set Level (Targeting, Budget & Placements)** [02:45]
   * **Conversion location:** Select **Website** [02:54].
   * **Performance goal:** Select **Maximize number of conversions** [03:09].
   * **Dataset / Pixel:** Select your active Meta Pixel (e.g., "Metanoia Pixel") [03:17].
   * **Conversion Event:** Select **Purchase** [03:32].
   * **Budget & Schedule:** Set the Start Date to **12:00 AM (Midnight)** of the next day (e.g., April 1st) to allow the campaign time to warm up and run a full calendar day [03:59, 04:28].
   * **Audience Controls / Locations:** Select **United States** [04:34].
   * **Advantage+ Audience:** Click "Switch to original audience options" -> "Use original audience" [04:44].
   * **Age:** Set range to **18 to 35** [05:19].
   * **Gender:** All genders [05:17].
   * **Detailed Targeting / Demographics / Interests:** Leave completely **BLANK** (Broad targeting) [04:55, 06:05].
   * **Placements:** Click **Edit** under Advantage+ placements -> Select **Manual placements** [06:12].
   * **Platforms:** Uncheck *Facebook*, *Audience Network*, and *Messenger*. Check **Instagram ONLY** [06:20].
   * Ensure Instagram Feed, Instagram Profile Feed, Instagram Explore, Instagram Reels, Instagram Stories, and Instagram Search Results are selected [06:38, 07:05].

5. **Set Naming Conventions** [07:12]
   * **Ad Set Name Format:** `Broad - [Age Range] - [Placement] - [Start Date]`
     * *Example shown:* `Broad - 18–35 - IG only - 4/1` [07:31, 07:43].
   * **Campaign Name Format:** `CBO - [Start Date] - [Targeting]`
     * *Example shown:* `CBO - 4/1 - broad` [08:07, 08:10].

6. **Create Ad Creatives (Method A: Use Existing Instagram Posts)** [08:48, 09:27]
   * Navigate to the Ad level [08:13].
   * Select your **Facebook Page** (which auto-connects your linked **Instagram account**) [09:14].
   * **Ad Setup:** Select **Use existing post** [09:31].
   * **Ad Creative:** Click **Select Post** -> Switch tab to **Instagram** [09:42].
   * Choose an existing Instagram Reel or Post that has high organic engagement (views, comments, shares) to carry social proof into the ad [09:00, 09:50].
   * **Call to Action:** Select **Shop now** [10:02].
   * **Website URL:** Paste the exact **direct Product Page URL** (e.g., `https://gometanoia.com/products/saiyan-summit`), NOT the homepage [10:16, 10:25].
   * **Ad Name:** Name based on product and type (e.g., `saiyan summit mesh shorts ad post`) [11:45].

7. **Clean Up Meta Enhancements / Advantage+ Creative Settings** [10:50]
   * Click **Edit** under Enhancements / Advantage+ Creative [10:49].
   * Turn **OFF** the following enhancements:
     * Music [11:01]
     * Visual touch-ups [11:08]
     * Text improvements [10:57]
     * Adapt multi-image format / Image animation [10:58]
     * Add catalog items [11:10, 11:27]
   * Keep **Relevant comments** turned **ON** (keeps top comments visible) [11:29]. Click **Save** [11:33].

8. **Add Additional Ad Creative Formats (Method B: Manual Upload / Carousel / Flat Lays)** [12:08, 12:30]
   * Duplicate or create new ads within the same ad set (target 5–10 total ad creatives inside the CBO) [08:26, 12:15].
   * Click the 3 dots on the Ad Set -> **Create Ad** [12:15].
   * **Ad Setup:** Select **Create Ad** -> **Manual upload** [12:31].
   * **Format:** Select **Carousel** (or Single Image/Video) [12:33].
   * **Add Media:** Click **Add Cards** -> **Add image cards** [12:47].
   * Select flat-lay product photos or alternate product photo angles [12:08, 12:59].
   * **Headline:** Enter a urgency/scarcity incentive such as `LIMITED TIME ONLY` or `FREE SHIPPING` [13:13, 13:22].
   * **Call to Action:** Select **Shop now** [13:34].

9. **Generate Primary Text Copy via ChatGPT** [13:45]
   * Open ChatGPT and write a prompt describing product features, scarcity, and offer [13:51].
   * Copy the output generated by ChatGPT and paste it into the **Primary text** field [14:16].
   * Review Advantage+ creative text variations if offered by Meta [14:22].
   * Verify all enhancements remain turned off [14:35].
   * Publish campaign [11:35].

## Offer, product, landing page & Shopify CRO

* **Landing Page Destination:** Link directly to the **specific Product Page URL** (e.g., `https://gometanoia.com/products/saiyan-summit`), NEVER the general homepage [10:16, 10:28].
* **CRO Rationale:** Direct product links eliminate unnecessary clicks/friction. Sending traffic to a homepage forces users to manually search for the item, causing drop-offs [10:32].
* **Featured Product Example:** "Saiyan Summit" Mesh Shorts priced at $39.99 [10:16].
* **Offer Incentives:** Scarcity messaging ("only 50 pairs available"), urgency ("limited time only"), and Free Shipping [13:11, 14:09].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Jumping into paid ads too early:** Running paid ads on a brand without prior organic reach/sales validation will waste money [00:31, 00:43].
2. **Using Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns for new setups:** Manual sales campaign setup allows proper control over CBO and placements [01:58].
3. **Budgeting below $50/day:** Anything less does not give Meta sufficient budget to optimize and test multiple ad variations effectively [02:31].
4. **Targeting older age ranges (35+):** Older audiences generally do not wear modern streetwear or graphic fashion styles [05:47].
5. **Using Detailed Interest/Behavior Targeting on initial campaigns:** Niche interest targeting restricts reach; Broad targeting works best for initial testing [04:52, 05:03].
6. **Running ads on Facebook when the FB page lacks social proof:** Streetwear brands often have strong Instagram accounts but weak Facebook pages; advertising on FB wastes budget [06:27, 06:53].
7. **Linking ads to the Homepage:** Directing traffic to the main site URL increases customer friction and decreases conversions [10:32].
8. **Leaving Meta's Advantage+ Creative Enhancements ON:** Leaving catalog overlays, auto-generated music, or auto-formatting ON distorts ad creatives and distracts buyers [11:01, 11:23].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Validate Organically Before Spending $1:** Paid ads scale proven offers; they do not fix products that fail to generate organic interest or sales [00:12, 01:14].
2. **Run Instagram-Only Placements for Streetwear:** Unless a brand has an established, highly credible Facebook page, uncheck Facebook/Audience Network to ensure the full $50 daily budget spends where streetwear buyers engage [06:19, 06:48].
3. **Use CBO to Test 5–10 Creatives Simultaneously:** Set a $50/day CBO campaign with broad targeting (US, 18–35, no interests) and let Meta automatically allocate budget to the winning creative formats [02:22, 08:26, 08:38].
4. **Leverage Organic Social Proof in Ads:** Use existing top-performing Instagram Reels/Posts as ad creatives so that existing likes, comments, and shares transfer directly into the ad [09:01, 09:32].
5. **Eliminate Friction by Linking to Direct Product URLs & Disabling Enhancements:** Drive traffic straight to the specific product page and disable noisy Meta AI enhancements (catalog overlays, auto-music) that distract buyers from completing a purchase [10:28, 11:01, 11:23].
