*   **Title:** Perfect White Tee - Case Study (Client Interview)

## One-line thesis
Scale a clothing brand from $20M+ ARR toward nine figures by shifting focus from "brute force" acquisition to a high-retention "Hourglass" system that maximizes Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) through aggressive Klaviyo flow optimization and deep segmentation.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1.  **System Audit:** Conduct a deep audit of existing email/SMS systems to identify gaps in the "post-purchase" journey [02:51].
2.  **Cleanse List & Technical Fixes:** Address bot attacks on loyalty programs and deliverability issues that can cause a 50% drop in email revenue [02:30].
3.  **Implement "Hourglass" Theory:** Restructure flows to focus on increasing the frequency of purchases within the first 12 months [04:42].
4.  **Deep Segmentation:** Move beyond basic flows to highly segmented triggers based on specific customer behaviors [11:20].
5.  **Aggressive Testing:** Run split tests on existing flows and add new, specialized flows (e.g., specific browse abandonment or loyalty triggers) [11:15].
6.  **Frictionless SMS:** Implement "Reply to Buy" SMS technology to allow customers to purchase directly from a text message [10:44].
7.  **Increase Acquisition Spend:** Only once the retention "bucket" is fixed and LTV is optimized, increase Meta ad spend to capitalize on the compounding effect [13:40].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
*   **Large Agency Fatigue:** Warning against agencies that scale to 50+ clients; the quality of delivery and attention to detail often suffers [02:00].
*   **The "Double Revenue" Trap:** Acquisition agencies may double your revenue but ignore systemic issues like bot attacks or declining retention [03:40].
*   **Logistics Breakage:** Warning that scaling too fast at the 8-figure level can "break the logistics, customer service, and ops side of things" [10:33].
*   **Bot Attacks:** Loyalty programs are high-risk targets for bots, which can destroy email deliverability and revenue [02:30].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Retention is the Scaling Floor:** You cannot profitably scale Meta spend if your LTV is static. Perfect White Tee scaled to 8 figures only after LTV increased by 60%+ [13:40].
2.  **The $20M Audit:** When a brand hits $20M+ ARR, "brute force" marketing fails. You need deep technical audits of email deliverability and bot prevention to protect the bottom line [01:40].
3.  **SMS is for Purchasing, not just Notifying:** Implement "Reply to Buy" SMS to convert your existing list with zero friction [10:45].
4.  **Profitability > Revenue:** Aim for "Full Price" high-revenue months. If your retention flows are strong, you can outperform Black Friday's profit margins during non-sale periods [13:58].
5.  **Specialist vs. Generalist:** For high-revenue brands, general acquisition agencies often miss the nuances of Klaviyo flow segmentation. Seek "T-shaped" experts who specialize in retention architecture [05:15].
