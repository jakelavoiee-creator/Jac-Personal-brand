## ## One-line thesis
Stop duplicating campaigns and interest-targeting; scale a single Advantage+/CBO campaign by increasing the budget by 5% daily whenever your 3-day average ROAS exceeds your calculated break-even ROAS [06:42, 10:05, 12:20].

---

## ## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Calculate Break-Even ROAS** [03:07 - 04:50]:
   * Calculate all non-ad hard costs per product (COGS, shipping to customer, payment processing fees, taxes/returns buffer) [03:26 - 03:56].
   * Subtract hard costs from the retail price to find Gross Margin ($) [04:04 - 04:08].
   * Calculate Break-Even ROAS = $\frac{\text{Retail Price}}{\text{Gross Margin}}$ (or $\frac{1}{\text{Profit Margin \%}}$) [04:14 - 04:36].

2. **Consolidate Account Architecture into 1 Campaign** [06:42 - 07:14]:
   * Shut down fragmented creative testing, retargeting, and lookalike campaigns [01:27 - 01:41, 06:53 - 06:58].
   * Maintain a single campaign (Sales/Conversion objective) using Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) [06:42, 18:23].

3. **Segment Ad Sets by Creative Concept (Not Audience)** [07:16 - 08:22]:
   * Set targeting to broad (open age/gender/location, no interest stacking, no lookalikes) [01:51 - 02:04, 06:59].
   * Create ad sets around specific **creative concepts/angles/offers/hooks** rather than interest groups [07:18 - 07:23, 08:11].

4. **Evaluate Performance on a 3-Day Rolling Average** [09:45 - 10:05, 12:03 - 12:25]:
   * Every morning, look at the last 3 completed days of data (e.g., on Monday, analyze Fri–Sat–Sun) [12:08 - 12:18].
   * Do not make decisions off a single day or intra-day fluctuations [09:53, 15:48 - 15:50].

5. **Apply the 5% Daily Scale Rule** [10:05 - 10:11, 12:28 - 12:31, 18:40 - 18:45]:
   * If 3-day rolling average ROAS $\ge$ Break-Even ROAS: **Increase daily campaign budget by 5%** [10:05, 12:28].
   * If 3-day rolling average ROAS is slightly below break-even: **Hold budget constant** [13:50].

6. **Manage Fatigue & Drawdowns (3-Day Rule)** [13:52 - 14:15, 15:01 - 15:24]:
   * If performance drops for 3 consecutive days below break-even:
     * Check if best-selling inventory is sold out [13:58, 15:18].
     * Check if creative/offer concept is fatiguing [15:15].
     * Decrease budget by 5% OR turn off fatiguing ad sets and launch a new creative concept ad set [14:00, 15:19 - 15:23, 19:07 - 19:12].

## ## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:** 1 single campaign active at a time for all prospecting and retargeting [06:42 - 06:58].
* **Campaign Objective:** Sales / Website Purchases / Conversion [01:33, 06:42, 18:22].
* **Budgeting Strategy:** Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO / Campaign Budget) [06:49, 18:23].
* **Bidding Strategy:** Highest Volume / Highest Value [18:22, 18:48].
* **Targeting:** Broad targeting (open age/gender/location, no interest stacking, no lookalikes, no separate retargeting campaigns) [01:51 - 02:26, 06:59 - 07:14].
* **Placements:** Advantage+ Placements (with manual opt-out only if specific video formats look distorted on certain placements) [07:00 - 07:09].
* **Testing Cadence:** Launch new creative concept ad sets ~1 time per week [19:18]. Give new ad sets 2–3 days to stabilize before touching [19:39 - 19:43].
* **Scaling Trigger:** 3-day average ROAS $\ge$ Break-Even ROAS $\rightarrow$ Increase CBO daily budget by 5% every morning [10:05 - 10:11, 12:28 - 12:31, 18:57 - 19:01].
* **Kill Rules:** Do NOT kill ads on 1 bad day [15:01, 15:28]. Only act after 3 consecutive days below break-even ROAS [15:09 - 15:12]. Reduce budget by 5% or pause the fatiguing ad set and replace it with a fresh creative concept [14:00, 15:19 - 15:23].
* **Attribution:**
  * Meta Ads Manager reporting is ~75–80% accurate if spending $100–$200/day and running only Meta ads [16:04 - 16:16].
  * Recommends 3rd-party attribution software (**Triple Whale**) when spending higher volume ($5,000/day) or running multi-channel marketing (Google Ads, Email, etc.) [16:18 - 16:48, 17:44 - 17:53].

* **COGS (Fabric, Manufacturing, Labels):** -$18.00
* **Shipping to Customer:** -$7.00
* **Payment Processing Fees:** -$2.00
* **Taxes & Returns Buffer:** -$3.00
* **Total Non-Ad Hard Costs:** $30.00

## ## Mistakes / warnings called out

* **Guru Legacy Tactics (3–5 years outdated):** Duplicating ad sets/campaigns, manually tweaking daily budgets by 20%–100%, and constantly testing cold interest groups or lookalikes [00:06, 01:15 - 01:23, 02:27 - 02:31].
* **Large Budget Jumps (>20%):** Triggers Meta’s algorithm to reset the learning phase, wasting paid data [10:19 - 10:22, 10:49 - 10:53].
* **Inventory Stockouts:** Scaling spend too quickly causes best-sellers to sell out, collapsing ad performance overnight and leaving no inventory cushion [11:14 - 11:27, 15:18].
* **Panic Reaction / Emotional Edits:** Turning off ads or editing budgets after 1 bad day or 3 hours of poor morning spend [15:01 - 15:03, 15:28 - 15:50].

## ## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Consolidate into 1 CBO Campaign:** Kill fragmented testing/retargeting campaigns; run a single CBO campaign targeting Broad audiences [06:42, 06:58].
2. **Base Decisions on Break-Even ROAS:** Calculate exact unit economics ($65 price - $30 hard costs = $35 margin $\rightarrow$ 1.86 Break-Even ROAS). Never scale without knowing this exact number [03:07, 04:34, 06:19].
3. **Scale Gradually at +5% Daily:** Increase CBO budget by 5% every morning that your 3-day rolling ROAS exceeds break-even. This avoids learning phase resets and prevents supply chain/inventory stockouts [10:05, 10:20, 11:13].
4. **Use the 3-Day Rule Before Killing Ads:** Never kill or reduce budgets after 1 bad day. Require 3 consecutive days below break-even before intervening [15:01, 15:09].
5. **Differentiate via Creative Concepts:** Segment ad sets by distinct messaging angles (Founder stories, product shots, carousels) rather than interest targeting [07:18, 08:11, 19:29].
