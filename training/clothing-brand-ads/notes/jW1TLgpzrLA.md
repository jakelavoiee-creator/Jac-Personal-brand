## Title & channel

* **Channel**: [Jajo](https://www.youtube.com/@jajoprint)

## One-line thesis
Scalability in clothing brands is driven by high-volume creative testing and aggressive offers (BOGO) on broad-targeted Sales campaigns, rather than technical interest-targeting or lookalikes.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Infrastructure Setup**: Ensure a Shopify store is active with a functioning Meta Pixel [02:14].
2. **Campaign Creation**: Select the "Sales" objective in Ads Manager to optimize for bottom-of-funnel conversions [01:10].
3. **Budgeting**: Set a CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) at a minimum of $50–$75/day to allow Meta's algorithm enough data to test [01:40].
4. **Ad Set Configuration**: Use "Website" as the conversion location and optimize for the "Purchase" event [02:12].
5. **Broad Targeting**: Use "Advantage+ Audience" but switch to "Original Audience Options" to keep targeting completely broad (no interests, no lookalikes) [02:41].
6. **Creative Testing**: Build multiple ad formats (Single Image, Carousel) featuring different product presentations (White background, Flatlay, Model shots) [03:00 - 07:05].
7. **Offer Integration**: Use high-conversion offers like "Buy 1 Get 1 50% Off" or "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" in the primary text [03:29, 05:22].
8. **Analysis**: Wait 3–5 days before making changes. Evaluate metrics based on profit margins rather than just ROAS [10:07, 14:00].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Low Budgets**: Warning that spending less than $50/day will result in Meta "burning your money" because it lacks data to optimize [01:41].
* **Emotional Management**: Media buyers act "like a girl" when they react to one bad day of sales. Meta's auction is a 7-day cycle; don't touch it based on 24-hour fluctuations [13:58, 15:08].
* **Advantage+ Auto-Recommendations**: Warning against blindly applying Meta's suggestions (like adding music or expanding placements) as they can ruin performance [13:07, 13:37].
* **Creative Sizing**: Ensure images are cropped to square (1:1) in Ads Manager so the top of the product isn't cut off in the feed [08:05].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Minimum Budget**: Never start a Sales campaign with less than $50/day; the algorithm cannot learn on smaller spends.
2. **Offer over Targeting**: A "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" offer on a Broad audience will outperform complex interest targeting with no offer every time.
3. **Highlight Carousel**: When running carousels, enable "Highlight carousel card" so Meta automatically shows the product that attracts the most clicks first.
4. **The 3-5 Day Rule**: Do not edit, kill, or tweak ads until they have run for at least 72–120 hours. 
5. **Creative Variety**: Mix "on-white" studio shots with organic-style "flatlays" on the floor to capture different user psychological triggers.
