## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Step 1: System Stability Over Tweaking** [00:39 - 01:09]
   * Stop constantly killing ads, launching random ad sets, or adjusting budgets intraday. Constant edits throw Meta's machine learning back into the learning phase.
   * Restrict core operational actions to three specific weekly buckets: 3 concepts tested/week, logging ad performance, and 1 major account optimization/week [01:10, 08:32].

2. **Step 2: Creative Research** [02:18 - 04:19]
   * Scan platforms where attention already exists: Instagram Reels (For You feed), TikTok (FYP), Reddit forums, and past brand ad history [02:20 - 02:37, 03:51, 04:08].
   * Identify content formats and hooks that proved viral or highly engaging organically (e.g., looking at high view/like counts) [02:46 - 03:08, 04:57].
   * Save promising concepts to an "Inspo Board" or swipe file [02:58].

3. **Step 3: Hypothesis Formulation & Messaging Framework** [04:42 - 05:47]
   * Take the organic concept and define the underlying marketing fundamentals before shooting: Target Persona, Core Customer Desire, Market Awareness Level (Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Product Aware, Most Aware), and Market Sophistication Level (based on Eugene Schwartz’s *Breakthrough Advertising*) [05:10 - 05:46].
   * Write full ad copy and scripts (Hooks, Body, CTAs) directly addressing objections uncovered during research (e.g., slow shipping times in streetwear) [05:14 - 05:30].

4. **Step 4: Creative Production & Asset Output** [05:47 - 06:33]
   * Film native-style, relatable, high-relevance video creative (acting out real-world skits, street-level situations showing the clothing fit) [05:54 - 06:26].

5. **Step 5: The Operating Cadence (Weekly Calendar)** [10:50 - 12:35]
   * **Monday:** Log previous performance, analyze feedback loops, kill underperforming ads [11:16 - 11:27]. Check scaling rules.
   * **Tuesday:** Evaluate scaling rules only. Do not touch or edit the ad account [11:28 - 11:32].
   * **Wednesday:** Edit the new creative video concepts planned the prior week [11:33 - 11:49]. Check scaling rules.
   * **Thursday:** Launch the 3 new edited ad concepts. Plan next week's concepts/scripts [11:50 - 11:58]. Check scaling rules.
   * **Friday – Sunday:** Let the machine learning run without touching. Check daily performance against scaling rules [11:59 - 12:05].

6. **Step 6: Logging and Systematic Feedback Loops** [07:12 - 08:31]
   * Record every single test in a centralized tracker ("AdPlan"). Note dates, personas, angles, awareness level, results (Winner/Loser, High/Low Spend, CPA), and why the ad won or failed to inform the next creative sprint [07:34 - 07:49].

7. **Step 7: Daily Metric Audits & Scaling Execution** [12:36 - 14:44]
   * Run the daily decision tree based on 3-day and 7-day performance against Break-Even and Target ROAS/CPA thresholds to either scale up, hold spend, or scale down to the hard floor [13:30 - 14:44].

* **Scaling Rules (Daily Scaling Flowchart):**
  * Check performance daily against target metrics [12:38, 13:30].
  * **Rule 1 (3-Day Average):** Is the 3-day trailing average CPA/ROAS better than the Target?
    * **YES:** Scale budget up by **20%** [13:34]. (Note: If target is only marginally hit or spend is exceptionally high, scale more conservatively by 10% or less; if beating target by 30–40%, scale confidently by 20%) [13:40 - 13:55].
    * **NO:** Move to Rule 2 [13:57].
  * **Rule 2 (7-Day Average):** Is the 7-day trailing average CPA/ROAS better than the Target?
    * **YES:** Do not decrease spend. Maintain current spend and focus on launching new creative tests [14:04 - 14:10].
    * **NO:** Move to Rule 3 [14:30].
  * **Rule 3 (Hard Floor Protection):** If both 3-day and 7-day averages are below target, are you currently at the **Hard Floor**?
    * **YES:** Maintain spend at the hard floor; do not cut further. Focus entirely on debugging via new creative/landing page tests [15:32 - 15:38].
    * **NO:** **Decrease total spend by 20%** [15:40].
* **Hard Floor Definition & Calculation:**
  * The absolute minimum amount of daily spend required to sustain enough top-line gross margin (contribution dollars) to pay fixed expenses [14:47, 15:07].
  * *Example shown:* If a brand has **$500/day in fixed overhead expenses**, ad spend must be maintained at a minimum hard floor over **$500/day** to generate adequate gross profit to remain solvent [14:56 - 15:09].
* **Kill Rules:**
  * Ad killing occurs strictly on **Mondays** [11:16].
  * Kill ads that are sucking up disproportionate spend, failing KPIs, and dragging down total blended business efficiency [12:07 - 12:20].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

* **Over-managing the ad account:** Constantly toggling ads on/off, adjusting budgets mid-day, and launching ads erratically destroys ad performance by throwing Facebook’s system back into the learning phase [00:39 - 00:58, 08:49].
* **Prioritizing Quantity over Quality in Creative Testing:** Launching 30 haphazard ad concepts per week leads to a near-zero hit rate [06:42].
* **Reacting to 1-Day Spikes or Dips:** Making optimization decisions on single-day results causes knee-jerk budget cuts because ad delivery fluctuates naturally [12:48 - 13:28].
* **Ignoring Attribution Lag & Cross-Channel Reality:** Trusting in-platform Facebook ROAS blindly leads to bad decisions. Meta ad views often trigger purchases that show up via email, SMS, or direct search [12:24, 13:07].
* **Scaling Past the Hard Floor When Underperforming:** Cutting ad spend too low starves the business of top-line cash flow, preventing fixed costs from being paid [15:10 - 15:28].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Enforce the "Rule of Three":** Do not burn out creative resources trying to produce 20–30 mediocre ads a week. Script, produce, and launch exactly **3 high-conviction, hypothesis-backed 9:16 video concepts per week** [01:10, 06:42].
2. **Mine Organic Feeds for Proven Paid Hooks:** Scroll Instagram Reels and TikTok FYPs to find organic streetwear videos with tens of thousands of likes. Transcribe their visual hooks, skits, and pacing, and adapt them directly to paid ads [02:37 - 03:08].
3. **Address Industry Objections Directly in Creative:** The biggest objection in independent fashion is fulfillment reliability. Explicitly stating shipping speed (e.g., *"They ship in 3-4 days"*) inside the first 15 seconds of an ad can turn an underperforming angle into a winner [05:14, 05:25].
4. **Scale and Cut on Trailing 3-Day & 7-Day Averages (Never 1-Day):** 
   * If 3-Day Average ROAS > Target $\rightarrow$ **Scale spend by +20%**.
   * If 3-Day is bad but 7-Day Average ROAS > Target $\rightarrow$ **Hold spend flat**.
   * If both 3-Day and 7-Day are below target $\rightarrow$ **Cut spend by 20%** (unless you reach your operational Hard Floor) [13:30 - 15:43].
5. **Establish Your Hard Floor Budget:** Determine your daily fixed business expenses and calculate the minimum daily spend required to cover overhead. When ad performance drops, never cut spend below this floor; instead, maintain spend at the floor and fix creative and on-site conversion rates [14:47 - 15:38].
