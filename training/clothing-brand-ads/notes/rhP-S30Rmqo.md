1. **Amplify Giveaway Posts to Build Cheap Top-of-Funnel Retargeting Pools:** Run Boost Post or Traffic campaigns to carousel giveaway posts. 4. **Build a "Burner Account" Algorithm Loop for Continuous Creative Testing:** Create a separate IG burner account, follow 10–15 top direct competitors, and engage with their content. Use the trained Explore feed to continuously mine winning ad concepts, audio, and hooks to adapt for ad testing [06:55 - 07:12].
5. **Drive High LTV / Returning Customer Rate via Broadcast Channels:** Maintain high returning customer rates (benchmarked at 38% in the video) by utilizing IG Broadcast Channels 2–3 times per week for direct product drop announcements, subscriber polls, and order updates [05:31 - 06:11].
