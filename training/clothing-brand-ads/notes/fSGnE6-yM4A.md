## Title & channel
* **Title:** How I spent $35,000 to make $168,000 with Meta ads (Clothing Brand)
* **Channel:** Ray in the Dark

## One-line thesis
Scale clothing brands by combining broad-audience ABO campaigns with "Highest Value" bidding and extreme scarcity-driven ad copy (FOMO), supported by high-frequency organic posting.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Account Structure:** Use ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization) rather than CBO to force budget into specific test sets and separate performance [01:10].
2. **Bidding Strategy Test:** Run two distinct bidding types: "Highest Volume" for mass reach and "Highest Value" to target customers with higher predicted Average Order Value (AOV) [02:35].
3. **Targeting:** Keep targeting "absolutely broad" (18–65+ or 18–44 Men) with no interest or behavior filters for scaling to six figures [03:25].
4. **Creative Development:** Produce high-contrast static images or low-production "lifestyle" videos (e.g., in a laundry room) featuring clear pricing [04:34, 08:04].
5. **Copywriting:** Apply aggressive FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) tactics, claiming the website is shutting down or pieces are "disappearing forever" [05:08].
6. **Organic Synergy:** "Spam" the algorithm with high-frequency organic content (Reels) to build brand trust and improve paid ad performance [06:25].

## Meta ads specifics
* **Campaign Objective:** Sales/Conversions [00:30].
* **Budget Optimization:** ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization) [01:07]. 
* **Bidding Strategies:**

* **Targeting:**

    * Gender-specific: 18–44 Men only [03:45].

* **Budgets:** Examples shown at $100/day per ad set [04:22] and $25/day for smaller sets [01:35].

## Creative specifics
* **Formats:** 
    * **Static Images:** High-contrast photos of products (e.g., orange sweatsuit) with large text overlays like "$99 SWEATSUITS" [04:34].
    * **Low-Fi Video:** Simple shots of product in "real" environments, such as a laundry room [08:04].
* **Copy Formulas (FOMO-centric):**
    * **Formula 1:** "[Time Limit] hours. $[Price]. This piece disappears forever. No restock. No second chances." [05:08].
    * **Formula 2:** "Website shutting down in [Time Limit] hours. $[Price] and it's yours. Never restocking. Buy now, pay later. Ships next day." [05:45].
* **Hooks:** Urgent scarcity and low-price entry points (e.g., "$55 hoodie") [05:32].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Meta Setting Changes:** Meta removing the "Website and Shop" conversion location option, which can "cook" (ruin) an ad set's performance [01:36].
* **Organic Neglect:** The biggest struggle for brand owners is not being consistent organically. If you haven't posted in 6–12 months, the brand looks "dead in the trenches" and ads will fail [09:00, 07:02].
* **Stagnant Creative:** Failing to "spam" the algorithm with content; you must edit your own videos and record constantly [09:02].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Prioritize ABO for Testing:** Use Ad Set Budget Optimization to ensure Meta doesn't prematurely dump your budget into one creative before others have had a chance to perform [01:21].
2. **Utilize "Highest Value" Bidding:** For male-specific clothing audiences, test value-based bidding to find customers willing to spend more per order [02:40].
3. **Broad Targeting is Superior for Scale:** Once you hit six figures, stop over-tweaking interest/behavior targeting and let the creative find the audience within a 18–65 broad set [03:31].
4. **Copywriting must create "Panic":** Use "Website Shutting Down" or "Disappearing Forever" angles (even if partially exaggerated) to trigger immediate purchase behavior [05:08].
5. **Paid Ads Need Organic "Spam":** Ad performance is a direct reflection of organic presence; use "Trial Reels" and high-frequency posting to keep the brand's social proof "alive" [09:25].
