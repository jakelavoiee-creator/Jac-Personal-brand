## One-line thesis
Leveraging Meta Partnership Ads and Whitelisting Ads bypasses traditional ad blindness and boosts hook rates by running creator-native UGC from third-party handles rather than brand accounts.

---

## Method (ordered, step by step)

### Step 1: Determine Ad Format (Whitelisting vs. Partnership Ad) [00:10, 00:22, 02:13]
* **Partnership Ad:** Co-branded format displaying both handles (`Creator_01 & Brand_01`) at the top of the ad [00:37].
* **Whitelisting Ad:** Fully controlled by the brand but entirely displayed under the Creator’s handle/page, leaving the brand name completely hidden at the top [00:24, 01:24].

---

### Step 2: Request Creator Access & Set Up Connections

#### Option A: Setting up Partnership Ads [04:48 - 05:35]
1. Go to **Meta Business Manager** -> **Partnership Ads Hub** [04:48].
2. Click **Partners** -> **Add Partnership** [04:58, 05:03].
3. Search and select the creator’s **Instagram or Facebook account** [05:05].
4. Send the partnership request. The creator receives an in-app notification on Instagram/Facebook to approve [05:13].

#### Option B: Setting up Whitelisting Ads [05:36 - 06:39]
1. Go to **Business Manager Settings** -> **Accounts** -> **Pages** [05:52].
2. Click **Add** -> **Request Access to a Facebook Page** [05:55].
3. Enter the creator’s Facebook Page name or URL, select required access permissions, and click **Send Request** [05:58]. Creator accepts via Facebook Page settings.
4. *Note on Instagram:* Directly linking a creator’s standalone Instagram account requires their raw IG login credentials [06:13]. To avoid asking for passwords, run whitelisting via their connected Facebook Page [06:21].

---

### Step 3: Configure Ads in Meta Ads Manager [06:42 - 07:42]

#### Setting up a Partnership Ad:
1. Open the campaign ad level in **Ads Manager** [06:42].
2. Toggle **Partnership ad** to **ON** [06:51].
3. Click **Select partnership** and choose the active creator partner [06:53].
4. Under **Identities**, select the primary brand account and associated partner handle [06:59].
5. Complete standard ad creation (media, copy, destination URL) [07:14].

#### Setting up a Whitelisting Ad:
1. Open the campaign ad level in **Ads Manager** [07:18].
2. Keep the **Partnership ad** toggle **OFF** [07:18].
3. Under **Identity** -> **Facebook Page**, select the **Creator’s Facebook Page** from the dropdown menu instead of your brand page [07:22].

---

### Step 4: Agency Workflow — The UGC to Partnership Pipeline [09:28 - 09:59]
1. Launch raw UGC ads directly from the **Brand Account** first [09:31].
2. Identify winning UGC ads that demonstrate strong baseline organic/paid performance [09:33].
3. Contact the winning UGC creator to negotiate a **Partnership Ad deal** [09:37].
4. Run the winning video as a **Partnership Ad for 5 to 7 days** to evaluate incremental lift [09:44].
5. If performance improves, negotiate a **1-month or 3-month extended usage contract** [09:50].

## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:** Standard ad sets using standard campaign objectives (e.g., Sales / Conversions) [06:42].
* **Placements:** Highly effective on vertical placements like **Instagram Stories & Reels**, where Meta places the "Sponsored" label at the very bottom, making whitelisted creator posts look non-commercial [02:58, 03:48].
* **Algorithm & Reach Preference:** Meta currently favors Partnership Ads in its algorithm, granting them increased organic/paid distribution to promote adoption of the Partnership feature [02:28, 02:39].
* **Testing Cadence:** Run Partnership lift tests on top-performing UGC creatives for **5 to 7 days** before committing to longer creator licensing contracts [09:44].
* **Custom Agency Handles / Whitelisting Hacks [08:29 - 08:54]:**
  * **Persona Accounts:** Create fake persona pages (e.g., `life_withsavvv`) to run whitelisted creator-style ads internally [08:29].
  * **Media / Review Pages:** Create third-party review/blog pages (e.g., `Nice Finds`) in Business Manager. Grant clients access to white-label ads through this page, framing product promos as editorial roundups [08:41].

## Creative specifics

* **Ad Formats:** Low-fi Vertical Video (9:16 UGC), Shot on Mobile [10:29 - 10:38].
* **Core Creative Principles:**
  * Must be **low-fi, raw, and unedited** [10:37].
  * Person-led narrative (influencer, creator, or founder on camera) [10:33].
  * Strong initial hook and engaging script [10:11].
* **What Flops / What Not to Do [07:43 - 08:11]:**
  * **High-production commercials:** Studio-shot or polished brand ads run via creator/partnership handles look completely out of place and fail because they lack authentic context [07:50].
  * **Mismatched creators:** Running ads through creator handles that don't match the product niche destroys credibility [08:05].
  * **Relying solely on the handle:** Simply changing the handle from the Brand name to a Creator name will not save a poorly structured or unengaging ad [10:16, 10:21].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **IG Credential Trap:** Do not ask creators for their direct Instagram passwords to add their IG account under Business Manager -> Instagram Accounts; it frictionally breaks deals. Request Facebook Page access instead [06:13 - 06:29].
* **Ad Blindness & Logo Rejection:** Showing a prominent brand logo or handle in the first 2 seconds triggers immediate swipe-aways. Whitelisting delays ad awareness, protecting the hook rate [02:53, 04:04, 04:31].
* **Misaligned Creative Styles:** Never run polished studio ads under creator handles; Partnership/Whitelisting ads must look like native, authentic UGC [07:51].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Build a "Review / Media" FB Page for Your Brand:** Create a generic fashion curation page (e.g., *"Daily Style Finds"*) inside Meta Manager. Request page access for your ad account so you can run third-party editorial/listicle ads for your apparel without needing influencer approval [08:41].
2. **Execute the 5–7 Day UGC-to-Partnership Pipeline:** Never start by paying full price for creator partnership handles. Test UGC on your brand account first; if it wins, negotiate a 5–7 day Partnership Ad test with the creator to verify ROAS lift before signing a 1–3 month contract [09:31].
3. **Protect the First 3 Seconds (Hook Rate):** Clothing consumers scroll past brand handles instantly. Running UGC through a creator’s handle removes the brand logo at the top of Stories/Reels, preventing immediate ad-rejection and boosting 3-second watch times [04:04, 04:31].
4. **Use Native, Low-Fi Mobile Video Only:** Save studio photography and polished campaign videos for Retargeting or Brand Ads. Whitelisting and Partnership formats only convert when the creative looks raw, mobile-shot, and unedited [07:51, 10:29].
5. **Streamline Creator Onboarding via FB Pages:** To avoid asking influencers for Instagram passwords or confusing them with complex Meta Business Manager invites, instruct them to link their Instagram to their Facebook Page and send a simple Facebook Page access request [05:51, 06:13].
