## One-line thesis
A successful Black Friday clothing brand strategy relies on building a warm audience pre-event at normal CPMs ($8–$10), saving 80% of ad budget for retargeting during BF week when CPMs spike ($30–$35), and executing a synchronized Email/SMS sequence and December post-BF extension.

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Step 1: Build The Foundation** [01:03]
   - **Product Mix Strategy:** Structure the Black Friday drop with 80% proven winners ("Hero Products" with high past sell-through) and 20% newly hyped items or colorway/wash variations [01:25, 01:41].
   - **SKU Deck Size:** Maintain a curated 24–27 SKU deck [01:53].
   - **Inventory Scaling Target:** Order 4x normal monthly sales volume (e.g., if generating $10k/month normally, inventory $40k for BF month) [03:06].
   - **Fulfillment Model Setup:** Choose between Pre-Made Drop (place PO 1–3 months prior), Pre-Order (collect orders, close batch, 4-week production lead time), or Print-on-Demand via Printify for zero upfront risk [04:40 - 05:46].
   - **Website CRO Optimization:** Remove popups, heavy animations, and intro screens. Create a clear hero banner with the discount code, clear sizing guides, and a frictionless single-page checkout [06:55 - 07:35].

2. **Step 2: Build the Acquisition Table (Pre-BF Campaign Planning)** [08:35]
   - Map out daily content across every owned traffic channel (Brand IG, Personal IG, TikTok, Email, SMS, YouTube) in an "Acquisition Table" grid [08:41].
   - **Phase 1: Awareness Stage (Early Nov / 2-3 weeks pre-BF):** Post non-pitch awareness content across 5 pillars: Product Showcase, Lifestyle/Vibe, Behind-The-Scenes (BTS/Friction), Founder's Story, and Outfit Styling/Framing [09:05, 09:28 - 13:20].
   - **Batch Content Creation:** 2–3 weeks prior to BF, execute 3–4 shoots over 4 consecutive days to batch-produce 30–50+ creative assets for social and paid ads [18:32 - 19:12].

3. **Step 3: Execute the Meta Ad Strategy** [19:18]
   - **Pre-BF Phase:** Run Meta ads 2–3 weeks before BF at standard CPMs ($8–$10) using conversion objective campaigns to feed the Pixel with high-intent site visitors and purchasers [20:35, 21:23].
   - **BF Week Phase:** Reserve 80% of the total ad budget to retarget site visitors, engagers, and cart abandoners accumulated during Phase 1 [22:27].

4. **Step 4: Deploy Email & SMS Sequences** [23:56]
   - Collect contacts via a password/waitlist page prior to drop [24:17].
   - **Email Cadence (5 emails over 4–5 days):**
     - *Day 1:* Sale Live (Big & Bold 30% OFF) [25:16].
     - *Day 2:* Best Sellers / Stock Running Out [25:27].
     - *Day 3:* General Black Friday Push [25:48].
     - *Day 4 (Morning):* Sale Ends Tonight / Last Chance [25:57].
     - *Day 4 (Evening):* 6 Hours Left / Final Warning [26:14].
   - **SMS Cadence (3–4 texts max):**
     - *Text 1:* Early Access link/password [27:17].
     - *Text 2:* Main Drop Announcement ("30% OFF Live") [27:19].
     - *Text 3:* Final Chance / Scarcity warning [27:26].

5. **Step 5: Structure Discounts & Offers** [28:46]
   - Set a blanket **30% OFF** site-wide headline discount [28:56].
   - Alternatively, implement a Tiered Discount Structure based on profit margins: High Margin = 40% OFF, Medium Margin = 20% OFF, Low Margin = 10% OFF [29:28 - 29:58].
   - Add Average Order Value (AOV) boosters: Free shipping threshold at $200, bundled item discounts ("Buy X + Y, get $10 off"), or free gift tier [31:07 - 31:15].

6. **Step 6: Execute Black Friday Week Operations** [30:39, 32:00]
   - *1 Day Before:* Send hype preview Email/SMS [32:10].
   - *Drop Day (Friday):* Release top-tier "big gun" video content, fire main Email/SMS, activate retargeting ads [32:21 - 33:10].
   - *Saturday–Sunday:* Maintain high Story posting frequency, answer customer DMs immediately to eliminate conversion exit points, run cart-abandonment retargeting [33:41, 35:19 - 35:34].
   - *Cyber Monday:* Execute "Full Send" scarcity messaging ("Final hours", "Cyber Monday Recovery Day") [34:49 - 34:57].

7. **Step 7: Extend Sales into December** [36:11]
   - Do not stop ads or marketing after Cyber Monday [36:31].
   - Lower-cost retargeting ads stay active as CPMs recover post-BF [36:53].
   - Pivot Email/SMS copy to holiday/gifting themes (Gift Guides, "Last Chance for Christmas Delivery", Restocks) at 1–2 messages per week through Christmas [37:09 - 37:27].

## Meta ads specifics

- **Account structure:** Pre-BF Prospecting / Conversion campaigns + Retargeting campaign pool [21:08, 22:27].
- **Campaign objective:** Conversion (Purchase) campaigns ONLY [22:15].
- **Budgets & Allocation:** 
  - Pre-BF Phase: 20% of ad budget spent 2–3 weeks prior to BF [20:35, 22:27].
  - BF Week Phase: **80% of ad budget reserved strictly for Retargeting** [22:27].
- **Targeting:** 
  - Cold/Prospecting: Broad or lookalike audiences during pre-BF phase [21:23].
  - Retargeting: Custom Audiences of Instagram/Facebook engagers, past 30–90 day site visitors, and cart abandoners [22:31, 28:16].
- **Placements:** Meta Feed, Stories, Reels [20:51].
- **Scaling & CPM Dynamics:** 
  - Standard pre-BF CPMs: **$8–$10 CPM** [19:52].
  - Peak BF Week CPMs: **$30–$35 CPM** [19:56].
  - Scaling rule: Do NOT scale cold prospecting during BF week due to high competition from major brands [20:17 - 20:25].
- **Kill / Negative Rules:**
  - **DO NOT** use Instagram "Boost Post" [21:33].
  - **DO NOT** run "Engagement" campaigns (they optimize for window-shoppers, not buyers) [21:39].
- **Attribution:** Meta Pixel purchase tracking to feed intent data into the pixel before sale day [21:23, 28:12].

## Mistakes / Warnings called out

- **Creating content during BF week:** Content must be fully batch-shot and edited 2–3 weeks in advance [18:18].
- **Dumping ad spend into cold audiences during BF week:** Competing for cold attention when CPMs are $30–$35 against $600k+ enterprise budgets is financial suicide [19:29 - 20:25].
- **Using Instagram "Boost Post":** Wastes ad budget without collecting proper conversion pixel data [21:33].
- **Running "Engagement" Objective Campaigns:** Trains Meta's pixel on non-buying window-shoppers [21:39 - 21:44].
- **Fearing Email Unsubscribes:** Sending too few emails due to fear of unsubscribes results in getting buried by competitor email volume [26:25 - 26:45].
- **Going Silent in December:** Turning off marketing after Cyber Monday leaves tens of thousands of dollars on the table during prime Christmas purchasing windows [36:30, 37:29].
- **Pre-order Lead Time Miscommunication:** Failing to explicitly inform customers of 4-week shipping windows leads to chargebacks [05:27 - 05:32].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Build warm retargeting pools pre-BF at low CPMs:** Run organic content and conversion Meta ads 2–3 weeks prior to Black Friday while CPMs are $8–$10. Feed purchase intent data into the Meta Pixel early.
2. **Allocate 80% of BF ad spend to retargeting:** Do not waste budget buying cold attention during Black Friday week when CPMs surge to $30–$35. Retarget past site visitors, social engagers, and email/SMS clickers.
3. **Batch-produce 30–50 creative assets in advance:** Execute 3–4 shoots across a 4-day creation period 2–3 weeks before BF. Never shoot or edit creative during BF week.
4. **Leverage SMS and Email as primary conversion engines:** Deploy a 5-email sequence and a 3-to-4-message SMS flow with aggressive scarcity messaging ("30% OFF", "Stock Running Low", "6 Hours Left") to drive site traffic that feeds Meta retargeting pixel pools.
5. **Capture post-BF December demand:** Keep retargeting ads active after Cyber Monday as CPMs drop back to normal. Shift copy to holiday gifting, gift guides, and Christmas delivery deadlines to capture late-year purchasing intent.
