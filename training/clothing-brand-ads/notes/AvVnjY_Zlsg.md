## One-line thesis
Q4 accounts for 70–80% of yearly e-commerce revenue; to remain profitable as CPMs peak, brands must separate promotional campaigns from evergreen ads, replace sitewide discounts with AOV-boosting bundle offers, and execute intraday "surf-scaling" during live conversion rate spikes based on 1-day click attribution and MER.

## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Pre-Q4 Financial & Seasonality Mapping [01:24 – 06:35]**
   * Pre-map CPM seasonality: CPMs climb in early November, peak during BFCM (+21% to +65% vs. Oct), decline through December, and drop sharply (~42%) during the Q5 window (Dec 26 – Jan 15) [03:18, 04:00].
   * Calculate required AOV lift: Rising CPMs increase CPCs, reducing traffic volume per dollar spent. Structure offers to increase Average Order Value (AOV) to offset higher CPMs/CPCs [04:36 – 06:05].

2. **Halloween Campaign Execution (Oct 24 – Oct 31) [06:50, 10:30]**
   * Launch a 7-day Halloween promo 1 week prior to Oct 31 [10:37].
   * Setup: 1 separate CBO/ABO campaign with 2 Prospecting Ad Sets (Ad Set 1: pure offer statics; Ad Set 2: benefit + Halloween messaging) and a low-budget Retention campaign (5–10% of campaign spend) [16:58 – 18:22]. Keep Evergreen running [16:00].

3. **Early BFCM & VIP Lead Capture (Nov 1 – Nov 26) [07:25, 18:39]**
   * **VIP Sign-Up Track (Nov 1–23):** Run Lead ads driving to an external web Landing Page (not native Meta forms) offering 48-hour VIP early access [18:55, 27:06].
   * **Early Deals Track (Nov 1–26):** Run limited-quantity bundle deals and small volume discounts (e.g., Buy 5+ save 20%) to capture early buyers before CPMs peak [18:43, 22:20].

4. **Main BFCM Campaign (Nov 24–26 VIP Early Access, Nov 27–30 Public BFCM) [27:36]**
   * Launch dedicated CBO/ABO campaigns per offer (VIP Early Access Nov 24–26, Public BFCM Nov 27–30) [27:37].
   * Deploy AOV-boosting offers: Buy More Save More, 20–25% effective bundle savings, 30–40% first-order subscription discounts, and spend-threshold cart progress bars [28:45 – 31:10].
   * Auto-apply all discounts at checkout (zero promo code friction) [36:20].
   * Creative Volume & Cadence: Load 5–10 new creatives per $100/day spend (proportionally scaling down at higher spend levels) and refresh creatives every 5–7 days from Nov 19 onward [32:04, 32:26].
   * Intraday Surf Scaling: Monitor Shopify / Triple Whale hourly. During conversion rate spikes (typically 12 PM–2 PM and 8 PM–10 PM), double (2x) campaign budgets every 1–2 hours [38:13 – 39:41].

5. **Christmas Promo (Dec 1/2 – Dec 21 Cut-Off Date) [40:16]**
   * Shift campaign framing from discount-centric to "Gifting" (Gift Guides, Gift for Him/Her) [41:00].
   * Target gender-opposite audiences (e.g., target women for "Gift for Him" fashion/products) [42:00].
   * End campaign strictly on the shipping cut-off date (e.g., Dec 21) to prevent late delivery refunds [40:27].
   * Scale budget on weekends [47:45].

6. **NYE Sale / Q5 Window (Dec 26 – Jan 10/15) [45:15]**
   * Capitalize on the lowest CPM window of the year (~42% CPM drop) [03:18].
   * Reframe messaging around "New Year New Me", resolutions, and late Xmas gifts [45:32].
   * Run 1 x NYE Prospecting Campaign per offer and 1 x Retention Campaign optimized for Highest Value [47:28].

## Meta ads specifics

* **Account Structure:**
  * **Evergreen Campaign:** Always keep active and untouched across all promo windows [16:00, 26:43, 36:12, 44:52, 47:25].
  * **Promo Campaigns:** Build separate, dedicated CBO/ABO campaigns for each event phase (Halloween, Early BFCM, BFCM, Xmas, NYE) [16:08, 26:48, 36:37, 44:57, 47:28].
  * **Prospecting Setup:** 1 CBO/ABO campaign x offer containing 1 Ad Set with all creative formats (or 2 Ad Sets separating pure offer ads vs. benefit + offer ads) [16:58, 26:55, 36:44].
  * **VIP Lead Gen Campaign (Nov 1–23):** Lead objective driving to an external Landing Page URL with an embedded form (avoid Meta native instant forms to prevent low-quality leads) [27:06 – 27:30].
* **Retention Campaigns:**
  * Separate low-budget campaign (5%–10% of total promo budget) targeting non-renewed subscribers, past purchasers, email/SMS lists, and pixel purchase events [17:51, 18:18, 45:00].
  * For Xmas and NYE, optimize Retention campaigns for "Highest Value" (AOV maximization) [45:01, 47:31].
* **Targeting & Exclusions:** Exclude 30-day purchasers or existing customers from all prospecting promo campaigns [17:48, 27:02, 36:15, 44:59]. For Xmas "Gift for Him" ads, target female audiences [42:00].
* **Attribution:** Use **1-Day Click (1DC)** attribution (not 7-Day Click) during BFCM for real-time decision-making [36:27, 38:55].
* **Primary KPI:** Track **Blended MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)** rather than in-platform Meta ROAS [36:28, 38:55].
* **Testing Cadence & Volume:**
  * Volume: 5–10 creatives per $100/day spend (e.g., ~30 creatives for $1,000/day, scaled down proportionally at multi-thousand/day spends) [32:26 – 32:50].
  * Refresh Rate: Add new creatives every 5 to 7 days from Nov 19 onwards due to rapid frequency escalation [32:04, 33:04].
* **Scaling Rules ("Surf Scaling") [37:18 – 40:15]:**
  * **BFCM Intraday Surf Scaling:** Pre-map historical peak sales hours from the previous year. Monitor live Shopify/Triple Whale sales.
  * When conversion rates / sales spike (typically 12 PM–2 PM lunch break and 8 PM–10 PM evening), double (2x) ad budgets every 1 to 2 hours in Ads Manager [38:43, 39:28].
  * **Xmas/NYE Scaling:** Scale on weekends rather than intraday [47:45].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

* **Running Blanket Sitewide Discounts (e.g., flat 20% off sitewide):** Slashes AOV and destroys profit margins at the exact time CPMs/CPCs are highest [05:40 – 06:05, 31:12].
* **Pausing or Editing Evergreen Campaigns:** Always leave winning evergreen campaigns running separately [16:00, 26:43, 36:12].
* **Using Meta Native Instant Lead Forms:** Yields low-quality leads; always drive to a web landing page form [27:00 – 27:08].
* **Running Xmas Campaigns Past Shipping Cut-Off Dates:** Triggers shipping delays, customer dissatisfaction, and massive refund/chargeback rates [40:35 – 40:42].
* **Relying on 7-Day Click Attribution or Meta ROAS During BFCM:** Causes poor scaling decisions due to lag and over-reporting; rely on 1-Day Click (1DC) and Blended MER [36:27, 38:55 – 39:22].
* **Manual Coupon Code Entry:** Creates checkout friction; ensure discounts auto-apply [36:20].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Replace Blanket Discounts with "Build Your Outfit" Bundles:** Avoid "20% off sitewide." Instead, use "Build Your Outfit Set & Save 25%", "Buy 2 Get 25% Off / Buy 3 Get 35% Off", or tier-based spend threshold gifts to increase AOV and preserve margins against peak CPMs.
2. **Isolate Seasonal Promo Campaigns from Evergreen:** Keep winning evergreen prospecting ads active and untouched. Build separate CBO/ABO campaigns for Halloween, Early BFCM, BFCM, Xmas, and NYE.
3. **Execute Intraday "Surf Scaling" on 1-Day Click:** Track hourly sales in Shopify/Triple Whale during BFCM (Nov 27–30). When conversion rates surge (typically 12–2 PM and 8–10 PM), double (2x) ad set budgets every 1–2 hours using 1-Day Click attribution and Blended MER.
4. **Target Gender-Opposite Audiences for Xmas Gifting:** From Dec 1 to Dec 21, reframe apparel ads around gifting ("Gift Guides"). Explicitly target female audiences with "Gifts for Him" ad sets to drive purchases for husbands, boyfriends, and fathers.
5. **Capture VIP Email/SMS Leads Early in November:** Run a dedicated Lead campaign from Nov 1–23 driving to a concise landing page. Launch a 48-hour VIP Early Access sale on Nov 24–25 to capture high-intent purchases before public CPMs reach peak levels on Black Friday.
