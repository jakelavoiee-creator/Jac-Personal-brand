## Method (ordered, step by step)

1. **Modify Shopify Product Title [01:52 - 02:53]:**
   * Edit the product title in Shopify Admin to clearly show pre-order status and delivery timeframe at the very front using parentheses.
   * *Format:* `(Pre-Order [X] Weeks) [Product Name] - [Color]`
   * *Example typed on screen:* `(Pre-Order 6 Weeks) Light-Tech Short - Black` [02:46]

2. **Add Bold Pre-Order Notice in Description [02:54 - 04:15]:**
   * Insert a bold text block at the very top of the product description to explain shipping terms clearly.
   * Explicitly state that if an order contains both in-stock and pre-order items, the entire order will ship in **ONE package** on or by the estimated arrival date [03:07 - 03:15].
   * *Verbatim copy typed on screen [02:57]:*
     > `This product is a PRE-ORDER item. If your order includes this item, your order will be shipped in ONE package on/by August 15th. This is when we will receive our inventory shipment. Thanks for your support and patience!`

3. **Input Variant Inventory Quantities [04:51 - 05:20]:**
   * In the Shopify product variants section, input the expected stock quantity per SKU/size corresponding to what was ordered from the manufacturer (e.g., S: 100, M: 200, L: 200, XL: 100, XXL: 100) [05:01 - 05:10].

4. **Preview & Verify Product Page [05:21 - 06:02]:**
   * Click **Preview** in Shopify to verify that the product title, description warning, size selectors, pricing ($44.00 USD), and "Add to Cart" buttons display correctly [05:23 - 05:58].

5. **Configure Automated Post-Purchase Email Flow [06:03 - 06:45]:**
   * Set up an automated email trigger/flow in email marketing software (e.g., Klaviyo, Mailchimp) for customers who purchase pre-order items [06:13 - 06:21].
   * The initial order confirmation email reiterates the estimated shipping date [06:27 - 06:38].
   * Send weekly update emails to customers throughout the wait period to maintain communication [06:42 - 06:45].

6. **Configure Backend Fulfillment Filters [07:06 - 07:35]:**
   * In shipping software (e.g., ShipStation) or Shopify Fulfillment, filter out all orders containing items tagged or titled "Pre-Order" [07:11 - 07:20].
   * Hold these orders so shipping labels are not accidentally printed or fulfilled before inventory reaches the warehouse [07:18 - 07:31].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Discounting Pre-Orders:** Avoid leading with discounts for pre-orders; customers will buy at full price if you communicate value and scarcity properly [00:48 - 00:53].
* **Lack of Transparency:** Failing to clearly communicate pre-order lead times on product titles and descriptions leads to customer confusion, negative reviews, and support tickets [04:28 - 04:40].
* **Splitting Shipments:** Splitting orders that contain both in-stock and pre-order items into multiple shipments forces the business to pay double shipping costs out of pocket [03:39 - 03:50].
* **Accidental Fulfillment:** Failing to filter pre-order titles out of ShipStation or backend fulfillment systems, resulting in labels being printed prematurely [07:18 - 07:33].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Fund Inventory Upfront at Full Price:** Run pre-orders at full retail price to secure cash flow before supplier invoices are due, avoiding profit margin erosion from launch discounts [00:45 - 00:53, 08:16 - 08:21].
2. **Leverage Scarcity in Copy:** Structure pre-order campaigns around size reservation ("reserve your size before pre-orders sell out") rather than long wait times [01:08 - 01:26].
3. **Protect Margins with Single-Package Shipping Rules:** Explicitly clarify on the product page that mixed orders (in-stock + pre-order) ship together in one shipment to prevent extra shipping costs [03:32 - 03:50].
4. **Automate Update Flows to Reduce Support Costs:** Set up dedicated Klaviyo/Mailchimp pre-order flows with weekly update emails to nurture buyers and minimize chargebacks or cancellation requests [06:08 - 06:45].
5. **Set ShipStation Holds on Pre-Order Titles:** Configure backend fulfillment tools to automatically hold orders tagged with "Pre-Order" to prevent warehouse teams from processing missing inventory [07:08 - 07:33].
