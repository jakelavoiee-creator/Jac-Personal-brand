## One-line thesis
- "Simple scales, complex fails" — avoid constant ad account changes that throw the algorithm back into learning; instead, use a systemic weekly rhythm of 3 high-intent creative concepts, rigorous logging/feedback loops, and a clear ROAS/CPA decision flowchart.

5. **Feedback loops:** Review performance data weekly to identify what worked and what didn't, feeding learnings into future tests [07:25].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Protect the algorithm:** Avoid daily reactive edits; maintain a stable system where changes happen on a structured weekly cadence (3 new concepts, 1 optimization per week) to keep Meta out of perpetual "learning" status.
2. **Calculate your exact Hard Floor & Breakeven ROAS:** Know your COGS, shipping, and fee breakdowns down to the dollar so your breakeven ROAS (e.g., 1.88) and minimum daily spend to cover overhead are mathematically defined before scaling.
3. **Use organic data as your ad research engine:** Look at high-performing organic Reels, TikToks, and Reddit pain points (like slow shipping) to source proven hooks and angles before building ad hypotheses.
4. **Apply rigorous framework-driven hypothesis testing:** Document every ad test using structured criteria (Persona, Angle, Desire, Awareness, Sophistication) rather than throwing random creative at the wall.
5. **Scale systematically with a trailing 3-day average rule:** Make scaling and cutting decisions based on a 3-day and 7-day ROAS moving average relative to your target, scaling by 20% increments only when metrics justify it.
