## One-line thesis
In 2026, Meta ads are entirely AI-driven and creative-driven (via the Andromeda update), meaning manual optimization and complex audience targeting are dead; your success is 90% determined by creative variety, high-quality flat lays/lifestyle content, and solid offers.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Pre-requisite Audit [00:32]:** Ensure you have a winning product/collection, an optimized website with clean branding, a solid offer (not just base price + high shipping), and good designs/content before spending on ads.
2. **Creative Production [03:34]:** Build high-quality creative assets (clean flat lays without shadows/wrinkles, body/detail shots, lifestyle shots, and collection overviews) following top industry brand standards (e.g., Ditch, YoungLA).
3. **Campaign Setup [09:49]:** Set up your Meta ad campaign using ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization) most of the time, or CBO for scaling/mass testing.
4. **Ad Set Configuration [10:06]:** Set targeting to broad (no interests or lookalikes), age/gender open, and select Advantage+ placements.
5. **Budget Allocation [11:23]:** Set a sweet spot budget of around $30–$50 a day (dependent on creative volume and brand situation).
6. **Attribution Window Setup [11:39]:** Configure attribution settings to **7-day click + 1-day view**.
7. **Testing & Iteration Loop [02:10]:** Rotate creatives aggressively; let Meta's Andromeda algorithm push high-performing creatives into 80–90% of spending and penalize bad creatives (low watch time, low CTR, low engagement) with increased CPMs and zero spend.

## Meta ads specifics (account structure, campaign objective, budgets, bidding, targeting, placements, testing cadence, scaling rules, kill rules, attribution)
- **Account Structure / Campaign Level [09:54]:** Use Campaign -> Ad Set -> Ad. ABO is recommended for most testing; CBO is recommended for scaling/mass testing.
- **Campaign Objective:** Conversions / purchases (implied by 7-day click + 1-day view attribution for e-commerce purchases).
- **Budgets [11:23]:** Sweet spot is around **$30–$50 a day** per ad set (depends on how many creatives you have).
- **Targeting [10:20]:** **Broad, no interests or lookalikes.** Age and gender should be **Open**. Broad targeting works because the ad itself filters the audience [03:22].
- **Placements [10:37]:** **Advantage+ placements.** Meta's system is driven by bidding and engagement. If Instagram CPMs rise too high, Meta temporarily shifts spend to Facebook to get cheaper clicks/engagement, lowering overall CPMs before moving delivery back to Instagram.
- **Attribution Settings [11:39]:** **7-day click, 1-day view** (explicitly *not* 7-day click + 1-day engaged view, because engaged view inflates metrics, gives skewed metrics, and people don't buy instantly).
- **Scaling Rules [02:19]:** High-performing creatives get shoved into **80%–90% of spending**. Creatives now determine 80%+ of performance, not audiences [02:29].
- **Kill / Penalty Rules [02:45]:** Andromeda penalizes bad creatives. Low watch time, low CTR, low engagement lead to a **CPM increase** and **no spend**. High watch time leads to cheaper traffic and cheaper conversions [03:05].

## Creative specifics (formats, hooks, angles, UGC/static/video, copy formulas, volume of creatives, iteration loop)
- **Formats [03:34]:** High-quality flat lays, lifestyle photos, detail/on-body shots, collection overviews, and carousel/grid showcases.
- **Good Creative Examples [03:35]:**
  - **Ditch:** Clean and consistent backdrops, high-quality flat lays with no shadows/wrinkles, reviews at the top of the site building trust, Black Friday offer banner (up to 60% off), email/SMS incentive ($5 off).
  - **YoungLA:** Details + on-body shots (models/athletes), lifestyle shots (e.g., at a racetrack), collection overview mockups, Instagram video skits for top-of-funnel engagement.
  - **Flowar.la:** Eye-catching grainy background, good designs, high-quality flat lays/collection showcase with space left at the bottom for CTAs [05:33].
  - **Valvetronic (Non-clothing) [05:58]:** Showing products being made by employees to build trust, search-by-year/make/model selection to reduce friction, precise shipping times, bundle offers, and embedded YouTube installation/sound videos at the bottom of the product page [06:43].
- **Bad Creative Examples [07:52]:**
  - Hoodies photographed with wrinkles, weird sideways positioning, hangers, and unwanted shadows [07:53].
  - AI-generated slop / bad overall design with text at the bottom where people might not see it or where CTAs get cut off [08:24].
  - T-shirts photographed on flat black backgrounds where the background color matches the shirt and creates conflict [08:41].
  - Car dealership ads with random items piled all over a car and weak financing offers ("£335.00 P/M & NO DEPOSIT") [08:55].
- **Ranking / Metrics [09:34]:** Meta ranks creatives by high watch time (boost), high engagement (boost), high click-through rate (boost), and low feedback score (penalty).
- **Volume & Iteration [02:13]:** Creative rotation and asset ranking are aggressive; bad creatives burn out fast.

- **Warning on manual optimization [01:52]:** Manual optimization is almost dead; Meta is very AI-driven now.

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Creatives are the new targeting:** 90%+ of Meta's algorithmic learning signals come from creative performance, making broad targeting with open age/gender the most effective approach [03:18].
2. **Prioritize creative volume and quality:** Eliminate wrinkles, shadows, and bad lighting on flat lays; feature clean backdrops, lifestyle shots, and on-body model details [03:54].
3. **Use Advantage+ placements:** Let Meta dynamically allocate traffic between Facebook and Instagram based on real-time CPM and engagement bidding dynamics to lower overall customer acquisition costs [10:37].
4. **Lock attribution to 7-day click + 1-day view:** Avoid 7-day click + 1-day engaged view to prevent inflated, skewed metrics from non-converting scrollers [11:39].
5. **Pair free shipping with urgency offers:** In 2026 media buying, a solid offer (free shipping + discount or BOGO) paired with high-quality content is what converts buyers immediately [12:32].
