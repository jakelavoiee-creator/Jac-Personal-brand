## One-line thesis
Competitor ad research and ad creation can be automated and scaled efficiently using the Facebook Ad Library combined with AI tools like Superscale, Claude, or ChatGPT to recreate winning frameworks, ride marketplace trends, and iterate on top-performing ads.

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Manual Competitor Research (Traditional Way):**
   * [01:12] Open the Facebook Ads Library.
   * Search for a main competitor (e.g., SKIMS [1:05], Honeylove [1:57]).
   * Filter by active ads [2:04] and sort by impressions (high to low) to find ads running the longest [2:06].
   * Manually copy ad images/videos [2:31] and paste them into AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Nano Banana) with a prompt to swap branding/products [2:47].
2. **Creative & Efficient Research Workflow (SuperScale Method):**
   * **Step 1: Recreate top competitor ads** [08:18]
     * Use FB Ad Library to find top ads based on impression count and recency [8:22].
     * Use Claude/Chat/Gemini/AI to recreate with your products/brand [8:26].
   * **Step 2: Recreate marketplace trends** [8:31]
     * Use Superscale to find the biggest trends [8:38].
     * Auto recreate in Superscale (recreate on your own) [8:42].
   * **Step 3: Iterate existing top-performing ads** [8:50]
     * Define "winners" in your account [8:55].
     * Tell AI to locate your winners [8:56].
     * Claude/Chat/Gemini/AI to iterate on winners [8:57].
3. **Automated Ad Research & Re-creation (SuperScale Tool):**
   * [04:34] Inside SuperScale, navigate to **Competitor Research** tab.
   * Manage competitors and add relevant brand pages (e.g., Good American, Aerie, Victoria's Secret) [4:48].
   * Click **"Create this ad for me"** on any competitor ad [5:02].
   * Select your own product (e.g., Cotton Rib Legging) and generate [5:09].
4. **Leveraging Ad Trends in SuperScale:**
   * [06:08] Navigate to the **Ad Trends** tab in SuperScale.
   * View aggregated market trends across 924,377 analyzed ads [7:05] (e.g., *Features into Benefits* [6:26], *Value Stacking* [7:08], *Moody Cinematic Lighting* [7:58]).
   * Click **"Recreate this trend"**, select brand-level or specific products, and let the AI generate a full creative pack [8:03].
5. **Iterating on Account Winners:**
   * [09:14] In Meta Ads Manager, sort ads by total amount spent (or identify ads with >2x ROAS and >$5,000 spend) [10:13].
   * Use AI prompt integration (MCP) in SuperScale to automatically locate the top 5% of ad account winners and generate 2 bold version iterations for every single winning image ad [11:35].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Watermarks:** Facebook Ad Library images often contain clear "PROTECTED" watermarks, making it difficult to feed them directly into vanilla AI tools like Claude or ChatGPT [1:25].
* **Ad Fatigue:** Ads running for over a month (e.g., started running on July 23) can easily be fatigued and lose relevance [3:37].
* **Inactivity / Lack of Brand Alignment:** Manual AI recreation without brand voice results in ads with "no flavor" and "no love to it," making it off-brand [3:56].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Automate Competitor Ad Discovery:** Use the Facebook Ads Library (or tools like SuperScale) sorted by impression count and recency to identify your competitors' longest-running, highest-performing ads.
2. **Ride Rising Marketplace Trends:** Instead of guessing what creative to build next, monitor aggregate ad trend categories (like *Features into Benefits* or *Moody Cinematic Lighting*) and focus on what is rising rather than fatiguing [7:44].
3. **Define Clear Winner Criteria:** Establish strict performance benchmarks for your ad account (e.g., >2x ROAS and >$5k spend over 30 days) to accurately identify your true creative winners before iterating [11:30].
4. **Scale Creative Iteration with AI:** Use AI workflows (via Claude, ChatGPT, or SuperScale MCP integrations) to feed in winning ad assets and automatically generate multiple fresh variations featuring your brand's products and updated headlines [11:20].
5. **Maintain Brand Voice:** When recreating competitor ads or marketplace trends via AI, ensure your brand's specific product imagery, tone, and visual identity (such as SKIMS aesthetic) are explicitly prompted to avoid generic, off-brand outputs [3:56].
