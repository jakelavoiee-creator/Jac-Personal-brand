## One-line thesis
- Meta’s ad system has changed so much over the past 6–12 months that old campaign structures (separating cold/warm audiences, separating interests, separating ad copy variations into separate ads) hurt your results; the updated recommended structure relies on consolidation, broad/open targeting, and high ad creative volume with built-in text variations per ad [00:00–13:51].

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Set up Custom Audiences / Audience Segments:** Go to Ads Manager > All tools > Advertising settings > Audience segments. Ensure "Engaged audience" and "Existing customers" are defined by adding your website visitors, video viewers, email list, Instagram engagers, etc. [02:52–03:26].
2. **Consolidate Campaigns and Ad Sets:** Run as few campaigns and ad sets as possible—ideally 1 campaign and 1 ad set for your product range (or product category) [05:14–05:27].
3. **Use Broad/Open Targeting:** Do not separate cold and warm audiences into different ad sets or campaigns. Rely on Meta's "Suggest an audience" / "Audience controls" and let Meta target a mixture of new, engaged, and existing customers within the same ad set [02:14–02:44].
4. **Load up Creative Volume:** Put 20+ different ads (creative diversity: UGC, founder-led, demonstration, testimonials, images, carousels) into a single ad set [10:14–11:00].
5. **Use Advantage+ Creative Text Generation:** Inside each ad, use the multi-text variation options (up to 5 primary texts, 5 headlines, 5 descriptions) rather than creating separate ads for every headline/copy test [12:18–13:46].
6. **Track Accurate Revenue via HYROS:** Install attribution software like HYROS to track true lifetime value, recurring billing, and cross-channel revenue that Meta under-reports [13:46–14:52].
7. **Separate Campaigns Only When Necessary:** Only create separate campaigns for different product ranges/services (e.g., Hats vs. Shoes), different countries/international markets, or distinct business offers (e.g., Agency vs. Mentorship) [18:52–21:11].

## Meta ads specifics (account structure, campaign objective, budgets, bidding, targeting, placements, testing cadence, scaling rules, kill rules, attribution)
- **Account Structure:** Consolidated structure. 1 campaign, 1 ad set (or very few), with 20+ ads inside that ad set [05:14–05:27, 10:14–11:00].
- **Campaign Objective:** Sales Campaign (Conversion location: Website, Performance goal: Maximize number of conversions) [00:59–01:03].
- **Budgets:** Daily budget set at the ad set level (e.g., £20.00 daily shown in example) [01:01–01:03].
- **Bidding / Optimization:** Optimized for maximum conversions; relies on Meta's algorithm to distribute budget across new audience (50%), engaged audience (30%), and existing customers (20%) [04:13–04:32].
- **Targeting:** Broad / Open targeting combined with Audience Segments (Engaged audience, Existing customers). Location targeting is kept in controls for hard geographical boundaries (e.g., United Kingdom) [02:14–02:44, 09:27–10:09].
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements / automatic placements [11:27–11:40].
- **Testing Cadence:** Test 20+ ad creatives simultaneously within one ad set rather than testing one by one [10:14–11:00].
- **Scaling Rules:** Scale budget when conversion volume is high enough in a consolidated ad set to feed Meta's learning phase (e.g., aiming for 70+ conversions/week in one ad set) [05:35–05:56].
- **Kill Rules / Fatigue:** If some ads consistently spend money with few conversions while others thrive, do not turn off the top-of-funnel ads feeding the broader sequence; analyze via HYROS before killing ads [18:00–18:48].
- **Attribution:** Meta's native attribution under-reports backend/recurring revenue; use third-party tracking (HYROS) for accurate multi-touch attribution [13:46–14:52].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
- Separating cold and warm audiences into distinct ad sets or campaigns (causes audience and auction overlap and splits conversion data) [02:14–02:44, 06:26–06:51].
- Testing interest targeting vs. lookalikes in separate ad sets (Meta's suggest/audience controls render this redundant) [08:10–08:25].
- Having too many ad sets with low conversion counts per ad set, which starves Meta's learning phase [05:35–05:56].
- Turning off top-of-funnel ads in a consolidated ad set just because direct attributed conversions look low, ignoring the full customer journey tracked by HYROS [18:00–18:36].
- Blindly following Meta's default automated ad recommendations without understanding your business model [22:24–22:37].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Consolidate Structure:** Combine cold, warm, and existing customer targeting into a single campaign and ad set rather than fragmenting budgets across a traditional TOF/MOF/BOF funnel structure [02:14–02:44, 05:14–05:27].
2. **Maximize Creative Volume:** Load 20+ diverse ad creatives (founder stories, product demos, UGC, testimonials) into your ad set to give Meta's algorithm the creative diversity it needs to personalize delivery [10:14–11:00, 11:00–11:21].
3. **Use Built-In Text Variations:** Utilize Advantage+ text options (up to 5 variations for primary text, headlines, and descriptions) within a single ad unit instead of creating duplicate ads for copy testing [12:18–13:46].
4. **Separate Only by Product Range or Geography:** Only split campaigns when you have entirely different product ranges (e.g., Hats vs. Shoes), distinct international markets, or fundamentally different business offers [18:52–19:12].
5. **Rely on Advanced Attribution:** Use third-party tracking tools like HYROS to capture true customer lifetime value and backend revenue that Meta's native pixel misses, preventing you from prematurely killing top-of-funnel ads [13:46–14:52, 18:00–18:36].
