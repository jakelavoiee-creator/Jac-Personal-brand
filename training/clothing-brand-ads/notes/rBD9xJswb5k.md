## Title & channel
How To Scale Facebook Ads On A Small Budget — [ADVT Media](https://www.youtube.com/@advtmedia)

## One-line thesis
On a small budget, Meta’s AI ("Andromeda") lacks the data to find winners automatically; you must manually force equal spend across creatives to 1,000–3,000 impressions, identify the true statistical winners, and graduate them into a simplified scaling structure. [00:43], [06:40]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1.  **Launch a Creative Test (CT):** Create a CBO campaign with broad targeting and 10–16 ads. [04:45]
2.  **Force Spend Manually:** Set a daily budget (e.g., $30–$80). Monitor impressions. When an ad hits 1,000–3,000 impressions, manually turn it off to force Meta to spend the remaining budget on the other ads in the set. [07:30]
3.  **Identify Real Winners:** Once all ads have hit the impression threshold, evaluate CTR, CPM, Add to Cart, and ROAS. Do not trust "lucky" winners that only spent $12. [08:10], [10:51]
4.  **Cut the Fat:** Turn off all underperforming or unproven assets. Concentrate the budget on only 2–5 top-performing creatives. [11:15]
5.  **Launch a Landing Page Test (LT):** Use the winning creative from the CT and test it against 4–6 different destinations (Homepage, Collections, Product Page, Custom/Unique Landing Page). [15:00]
6.  **Graduate to Scaling:** Move the winning Ad + Landing Page combination into Advantage+ Sales Campaigns (ASC) and Interest-based targeting sets. [21:00]
7.  **Iterative Testing:** Run a new "New Batch" CT on the side while the main scaling campaigns run. Compare the new batch’s average ROAS/CTR to the "Current Assets" baseline before integrating. [22:30]

## Meta ads specifics
*   **Account Structure:** Simplified. One CT campaign (Top of Funnel), one LT campaign, and one scaling campaign (ASC/Interests). [21:18]
*   **Campaign Objective:** Sales / Conversion. [00:10]
*   **Budgets:** Tests run on $30/day to $100/day. Scaling examples shown at $5,000/day. [00:22], [14:40]
*   **Bidding:** CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) used for testing to see where Meta *wants* to spend, though manual intervention is required to override it. [04:34]
*   **Targeting:** Broad (no interests/lookalikes) for CT/LT tests to avoid adding extra variables. [04:37] Later, Interest targeting is added (e.g., "Climbing," "Camping"). [20:25]
*   **Kill Rules:** Manually kill ads in the test phase exactly at 1,000–3,000 impressions to ensure data parity. [07:35]
*   **Testing Cadence:** Monthly or bi-monthly "New Batch" tests of 10 new creatives. [22:12]

## Metrics & benchmarks quoted
*   **Impression Threshold:** 1,000 (minimum) to 3,000 (ideal) impressions per ad for a valid test. [06:41]
*   **CTR:** "Healthy" CTR (All) is 6%+. A 2% CTR vs. 6% represents a 3x difference in engagement. [08:18]
*   **CPM:** Watch for outliers. One ad may cost $54.60 CPM while another is $18.31. Lower CPM with higher CTR is the "Golden Nugget." [09:18]
*   **CRO Benchmark 1:** At least 10% of Content Views should result in an Add to Cart. [24:18]
*   **CRO Benchmark 2:** No more than a 50% drop-off from Add to Cart to Checkout Initiated. [24:50]

## Mistakes / warnings called out
*   **The "Volume Trap":** Pumping out 15–20 creatives on a $30/day budget spreads money too thin; Meta spends 90% on 2 ads and $0.20 on others. You learn nothing. [00:27], [02:15]
*   **Handing Control to Meta:** Small accounts cannot afford to let Meta "figure it out." You must decide where every dollar goes. [21:30]
*   **Shocking the Algorithm:** Never 5x a budget instantly because an ad had one good day with a 16x ROAS. Scale in measured steps (e.g., add $20/day). [18:45], [20:04]
*   **Misunderstanding "Variety":** Variety is needed, but "Variety + Underperforming assets" just weighs down the account average. [13:00]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Stop the "Spray and Pray":** If your budget is under $100/day, do not launch 20 creatives. Launch 10 and manually cap their impressions so you can actually compare them.
2.  **Test the Destination:** For clothing, the destination (Product Page vs. Collection vs. Lifestyle LP) is often as important as the ad itself. A 70% increase in purchase rate was shown by switching to a Homepage destination. [16:30]
3.  **The 10% ATC Rule:** If your clothing brand has high traffic but fewer than 10% add to cart, the problem is your Shopify site (likely bloated descriptions or bad imagery), not your ads. [24:18]
4.  **Concentrate Your Fire:** It is better to have 3 winning ads getting $30/day than 100 ads getting pennies. Cut the bottom 70% of your ads immediately. [12:45]
5.  **Scale the "Lego Blocks":** Once you find an ad angle that wins (e.g., a specific flat-lay photo), make 3 more versions of that *exact* angle. Don't go back to the drawing board for every test. [20:50]
