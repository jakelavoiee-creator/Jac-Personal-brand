## Mistakes / warnings called out
* [03:01] Forgetting to turn off "Use a suggestion" when setting strict age parameters will keep Advantage+ audience targeting active.
* [10:01] Don't optimize ad sets for the wrong events (e.g., optimizing for all leads when you want qualified calls/purchases will make optimization harder).

## Claims vs. evidence (results claimed, and what proof was actually shown on screen)
* **Claim:** [04:14] Turning off Advantage+ audiences makes virtually no significant difference in performance compared to running with it on.
* **Proof Shown:** [04:01] Live Meta Ads Manager dashboard showing nearly identical spend ($1,284 vs $1,280), results (71 vs 72), and cost per result ($18.09 vs $17.78) across a $2,500+ test budget.

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Creative is Targeting:** Post-Andromeda, the creative does the heavy lifting for targeting; audience toggles like Advantage+ vs. Broad yield nearly identical performance when creatives are identical ([01:26], [06:47]).
2. **Keep Account Structure Simple:** Stays with broad targeting to reduce complexity in the ad account ([00:35]).
3. **Optimize for the Right Event:** Align campaign optimization events strictly with business goals (e.g., qualified leads/purchases rather than top-of-funnel vanity metrics) ([10:10]).
4. **Retargeting Happens Naturally:** Both broad and Advantage+ campaigns automatically allocate a portion of the budget (~1/3) to retargeting existing engaged audiences without needing separate retargeting ad sets ([05:53]).
5. **Test Before Reacting:** Do not blindly follow shifting industry consensus ("turn off Advantage+" vs "leave it on") without running your own split tests with real budget ([01:40]).
