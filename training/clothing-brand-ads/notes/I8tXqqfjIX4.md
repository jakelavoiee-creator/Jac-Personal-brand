## One-line thesis
Winning on Meta requires testing 3 to 6 distinct, psychologically-driven video hook variations (combining screen text, voiceover, frame-zero visuals, and vibe) rather than making minor single-word script tweaks [00:46, 09:34, 09:48].

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Audit Current Winners:** Identify the top 5 to 10 performing ad scripts/hooks currently running in your ad account [01:43, 05:58, 09:58].
2. **AI Hook Enrichment:** Upload winning scripts into Claude and prompt the AI to inject emotional and sensory trigger words into proven hook structures [01:45-01:52].
3. **Optimize Second Zero:** Format frame zero of every video to align four distinct elements simultaneously: text read on screen, voiceover heard, visual scene, and overall aesthetic vibe [00:46-00:56].
4. **Develop 3–6 Hook Variations:** Produce 3 to 6 major structural variations of the hook per core creative asset rather than testing minor single-word edits [09:34, 09:48].
5. **Map Hooks to Funnel Stages:** Match hook types to consumer awareness levels:
   - *Awareness / Top of Funnel:* "How to know if...", "How to start...", "A day without pain" [02:50, 04:01, 04:52].
   - *Consideration / Middle of Funnel:* "Why is it important...", "How do I pick/choose...", "If you..." [01:58, 04:31, 05:11].
   - *Conversion / Bottom of Funnel:* "This 1 thing...", "You're welcome" [06:11, 07:27].
6. **Iterate Post-Production Format:** Take winning video concepts and re-edit the first 3 seconds using audio tricks (whispering/ASMR) or visual overlays (Snapchat text filters) [07:47, 08:41, 10:00].

## Creative specifics
- **Formats:** Vertical 9:16 UGC, native phone-recorded videos, screen recordings, text-on-screen overlay clips [00:16, 01:20].
- **The 4-Part Second Zero Framework [00:46]:**
  1. What the viewer reads on screen
  2. What they hear (voiceover audio)
  3. What they see in frame zero (visual action)
  4. Overall vibe/package
- **The 9 High-Converting Hook Frameworks:**
  1. **The Trigger Word Hook [01:17]:** Injects high-emotion/sensory power words into the hook line (e.g., "ruining my life", "diabolical", "insane") [01:21, 01:37].
  2. **"Why is it important" Hook [01:58]:** Injects native education into the funnel (e.g., "Why is it important that your undies are organic?") [02:06].
  3. **"A day without pain" Hook [02:50]:** Flips traditional Problem $\rightarrow$ Solution into Solution/Transformation $\rightarrow$ Problem (leads directly with the transformed outcome) [02:59, 03:04].
  4. **"How-to" Hooks (3 Sub-angles) [03:32]:**
     - *How to know if...* (Problem aware / Top of funnel) [04:01].
     - *How do I pick/choose...* (Solution/Option evaluation) [04:31].
     - *How to start...* (Onboarding / Action-oriented) [04:52].
  5. **The "If you..." Hook [05:11]:** Direct persona and demographic targeting (e.g., "If you are someone who likes to have a sweet treat after dinner...", "If you're new to Jones Road...") [05:20, 05:35, 05:57].
  6. **The "This 1 thing..." Hook [06:11]:** Solves cognitive dissonance by promising simplicity/ease to relieve complex issues (e.g., "Adding this ONE thing to my dog's food...", "Your WHOLE makeup bag in ONE set") [06:26, 06:34, 06:42].
  7. **"We're sorry" / "You're welcome" Hooks [06:48]:** Uses psychological curiosity ("We are sorry...") or native intimacy ("You're welcome") as pattern interrupts [06:55, 07:27].
  8. **Snapchat Filter Hook [07:47]:** Visual overlay mimicking Snapchat black banner text with emojis or filters [07:53, 07:59].
  9. **Whispering Hook [08:41]:** Takes a top-performing normal voiceover and converts the audio in second zero into an ASMR-style whisper [08:52, 08:59].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
- **Defining a hook as just a text line:** A hook is the combined combination of on-screen text, audio voiceover, frame-zero visuals, and overall vibe [00:44-00:55].
- **Dismissing "How-To" hooks as boring:** How-to content drives essential middle/top-of-funnel education and signals learning rather than aggressive selling [03:34, 03:51].
- **Making micro-changes:** Changing only one word in a hook test rarely produces meaningful performance swings; test broad structural variations [09:44, 09:48].
- **Stereotyping native filters:** Assuming Snapchat filter formats only appeal to Gen Z; data shows strong scaling among men aged 45–50+ [08:05, 08:10].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Scale Organic/Material Education:** Test the *"Why is it important that your [clothing item] is organic / ethically made?"* hook angle (proven by Pact's organic underwear top-performing ad) [02:06].
2. **Leverage "Pick / Choose" Fit Hooks:** Implement *"How do I pick the right size/fit for my body shape?"* as a core consideration hook; this framework remained a top performer for 6 consecutive months in fashion and apparel accounts [04:31, 04:37].
3. **Execute Persona Callouts:** Use *"If you only have time for a 'less but better' capsule wardrobe..."* or *"If you are a busy mom on the go..."* to segment your buyer personas natively through creative targeting [05:22, 05:35].
4. **Deploy "You're Welcome" Pattern Interrupts:** Test visual static/video UGC showing garment details overlaid with *"You're welcome"* text to convey native product intimacy and drive promo/sale conversions [07:27].
5. **Run 3–6 Macro-Hook Variations per Winner:** Take your #1 best-selling clothing video asset and test 3 to 6 distinct second-zero variations (e.g., standard voiceover vs. ASMR whisper hook, standard text vs. Snapchat filter banner) without altering the main body of the ad [08:52, 09:34, 10:00].
