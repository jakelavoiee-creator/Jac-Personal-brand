## Title & channel
How I Made This Clothing Brand $491,674 in 1 Month using Meta Ads (6.36x ROAS) — Luca De Gregorio [00:00]

## One-line thesis
Success in modern Meta advertising (post-Andromeda/GEM) requires replacing interest-based targeting with high-volume, creative-led broad targeting and a sequence-based account structure organized by Average Order Value (AOV) and product category. [01:10]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1.  **Restructure the account by AOV:** Group products into CBO campaigns based on price ranges to ensure Meta optimizes for similar margins. [02:50]
2.  **Segment by Category:** Create ad sets within those campaigns for specific product types (e.g., T-shirts, hats) to allow Meta to find the right audience for that specific item at the ad set level. [03:25]
3.  **Implement Creative-Led Targeting:** Use broad targeting with no interests. Allow the visual style and model demographic in the creative to dictate who Meta serves the ads to. [16:06]
4.  **High-Volume Creative Deployment:** Launch 15–30 ads per ad set to feed Meta’s sequencing system (GEM/Andromeda). [03:41]
5.  **DPA Segmentation:** Set up Dynamic Product Ads split by category rather than "all products." [06:35]
6.  **Scale via Cost Caps:** Move winning creatives (found in CBO campaigns) into a dedicated Manual Bidding/Cost Cap campaign using Post IDs. [07:26]
7.  **Retargeting Maintenance:** Run a small ABO retargeting campaign (5–8% of budget) specifically for existing customers to sell them new products/collections. [09:14]

## Meta ads specifics
*   **Account Structure:**
    *   **Campaign 1 & 2 (CBO):** Segmented by AOV range (e.g., $80–100 and $100–120). [02:50, 05:51]
    *   **Campaign 3 (DPA):** Segmented by product category or "occasion to use." [06:05]
    *   **Campaign 4 (Winning Ads - Cost Cap):** Dedicated scaling campaign for proven winners. [07:27]
    *   **Campaign 5 (ABO Retargeting):** Focused on existing customers. [09:14]
*   **Campaign Objective:** Sales/Conversions. [00:15]
*   **Budgets:** 
    *   Retargeting: 5–8% of total daily budget. [09:18, 10:25]
    *   Prospecting: Remaining budget in CBOs. [13:17]
*   **Bidding:** 
    *   Highest Volume/Lowest Cost for prospecting CBOs. [14:00]
    *   Manual Bidding (Cost Cap) for the scaling campaign. [07:34]
    *   "Maximize Conversion Value" can be used for single CBO structures with varied AOVs but offers less control. [13:45, 15:52]
*   **Targeting:** Broad targeting. No interests, no stacks, no brand names. [01:00, 11:17]
*   **Placements:** Not specified, but implied broad/automatic.
*   **Testing Cadence:** Add new creatives directly into existing ad sets. [12:12]
*   **Scaling Rules:** Increase budget at the campaign level as performance holds. [13:18]
*   **Kill Rules (Ad Level):**
    *   Ad has spent 3x the target CPA and ROAS is below target. [12:25]
    *   Ad has been live for 14–30 days and spent less than $10 total (indicating it lost the internal auction). [12:40]
*   **Attribution:** 7-day click (if organic is strong) or 7-day click / 1-day view / 1-day engage. [11:24, 11:47]

## Creative specifics
*   **Creative-Led Targeting:** Meta analyzes the visual content (e.g., "Streetwear style t-shirt with a man") to target the relevant demographic. [16:11]
*   **Winning Stack vs. Winning Creative:** Meta now uses a sequencing system where multiple ads work together to lead to a conversion. Do not kill low-conversion ads if they are early in the sequence. [01:25, 03:51]
*   **Ad Formats & Angles:**
    *   **Clothing on ground:** Simple product shots. [26:45, 31:18]
    *   **UGC photos:** Simple, relatable images. [33:30]
    *   **Split Screen:** Full outfit vs. detail close-up. [18:31, 33:41]
    *   **Offer-led:** "Buy 1 Get 1 Free" or "6-Pack Save $35." [18:41, 21:13]
    *   **Eye-Catching:** Bright colors (e.g., pink) and bold claims ("Our T-shirt won't break"). [26:08, 41:10]
    *   **Founder Videos:** Stories/Mission to build brand awareness. [19:50]
    *   **Grid Style:** Showing multiple outfits in one image. [18:22, 20:41]
*   **Volume:** 15–30 ads inside each ad set. [03:41]
*   **Iteration Loop:** Test new creative in CBO; move winners to Cost Cap via Post ID to retain engagement/social proof. [08:08]

## Mistakes / warnings called out
*   **Interest Targeting:** No longer works for clothing brands; it limits Meta's ability to optimize. [01:00]
*   **Killing Sequence Ads:** Warning against turning off ads with low direct conversions if they are part of the early sequence leading to a final conversion ad. [04:18]
*   **DPA "All Products":** Putting every product into one DPA campaign prevents Meta from optimizing for specific buyer categories. [06:15]
*   **Scaling without Post ID:** Duplicating winners without the Post ID loses engagement data and lowers the ad's rank in the auction. [08:11]
*   **Overspending on Retargeting:** Do not pay twice for customers you already acquired; keep retargeting spend low and rely on email/SMS for retention. [10:38]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1.  **Group by AOV:** Stop putting $20 socks and $200 jackets in the same CBO; Meta will almost always spend on the lower-cost item regardless of ROAS. [02:50]
2.  **Creative is Targeting:** The people in your photos, the location, and the style *are* your audience settings. Broad targeting is now the default. [16:06]
3.  **Post IDs are Mandatory for Scaling:** When moving a winner to a Cost Cap campaign, you must use the existing Post ID to maintain the ad's "auction rank." [08:23]
4.  **Exclude Customers from Cold Traffic:** prospecting campaigns should strictly acquire new customers. Use a separate, low-budget campaign for retention. [09:25]
5.  **Audit Visuals with AI:** Use Claude or ChatGPT Vision to see how Meta’s algorithm likely categorizes your creative before spending money on it. [16:29]
