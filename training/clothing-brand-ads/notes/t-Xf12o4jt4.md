## One-line thesis
Under Meta's Andromeda algorithm, creative diversification and top-of-funnel conversion require moving beyond immediate hard product pitches to psychological, identity-driven, and visually dynamic hooks stacked in the first 3 seconds. [00:20–00:31, 03:09–03:20]

## Method (ordered, step by step)
1. **Mine Customer Failed Attempts via Review Data:** Export a CSV of customer reviews and paste/upload it into an LLM to identify the exact "investment" moments (time, money, failed alternatives) customers experienced prior to finding your brand. [01:00–01:10]
2. **Select & Draft Psychological Hook Angles:** Choose from tested high-converting hook structures:
   - *Investment Hook* (stating time/money wasted before finding the solution). [00:46–00:59]
   - *Scam Hook* (leveraging loss aversion and curiosity). [02:12–02:51]
   - *Give Me Time Hook* (asking for an explicit time commitment like "give me 10 minutes"). [05:36–05:55]
   - *POV: You Hate [X] Hook* (combining point-of-view framing with high-friction trigger words). [06:06–06:42]
   - *Founder Story / Introduction Hook* (stating why the company was created or introducing founder by name/title). [07:02–07:35]
   - *Why Did No One Tell Me Hook* (creating cognitive dissonance and insider authority). [10:36–10:58]
   - *If You... Hook* (triggering demographic or behavioral self-selection). [11:17–11:40]
3. **Build the 4-Part Hook Structure for the First 3 Seconds:** Ensure every video creative integrates: (1) Text overlay hook, (2) Sound hook, (3) Visual hook, and (4) General vibe/aesthetic. [03:09–03:34]
4. **Implement "Show, Don't Tell" Visual B-Roll Hooks:** Prioritize dynamic visual clips (liquid drips, fruit squeezes reversed/sped up, abstract surreal art, explosions) to capture immediate attention rather than relying solely on talking heads. [03:42–04:46]
5. **Stack Hooks (The Multiple Hook Technique):** Stack 2 to 3 hooks in the intro (e.g., verbal hook + text overlay paired with a visual explosion hook immediately following). [04:47–05:04]
6. **Deploy via Meta Partnership Ads:** Run creator UGC, founder content, or third-party publication handles through Meta's Partnership Ads feature rather than standard brand page handles. [08:43–09:13]
7. **Iterate Top Performers:** When an ad fatigues or performs well, swap the initial visual hook or prepend a new hook angle (e.g., adding a "scam" or "POV" intro) while keeping the winning body. [02:52–02:59, 03:42–03:52]

## Creative specifics (formats, hooks, angles, UGC/static/video, copy formulas, volume of creatives, iteration loop)
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical video for Reels/TikTok/Meta Feed. [00:09, 02:15, 03:15, 09:27]
- **Creative Hook Typology:**
  1. **Investment Hook [00:47]:** Explains time or money spent before finding the product.
     - *Formula:* "I spent $[Amount] on [failed alternatives] to [desired result] before I found this..." [00:48]
     - *Formula:* "I spent $[Amount] on [category] last year." [00:51]
  2. **The Scam Hook [02:13]:** Uses the visceral trigger word "scam" to induce loss aversion and curiosity.
     - *Formula:* "I honestly thought [Brand/Product] was a scam. I bought it off [Platform]..." [02:15]
     - *Formula:* "It was a scam. We lost everything." [02:19]
     - *Formula:* "I thought this was a scam, but..." [02:44]
  3. **Give Me Time Hook [05:37]:** Asks for an upfront time commitment in exchange for education/entertainment.
     - *Formula:* "Hey girl, give me [X] minutes to [major transformation/benefit]." [05:44]
     - *Formula:* "Give me [X] minutes and I'll make your [skill] 10x better." [05:45]
     - *Formula:* "Give me [X] minutes and you'll learn how to [desired life state]." [05:46]
  4. **POV: You Hate [X] Hook [06:07]:** Relatable point-of-view angle paired with negative emotion word "hate".
     - *Formula:* "POV: You hate [common daily annoyance/pain point] because you're [identity/trait]." [06:32]
     - *Formula:* "POV You hate sleeping alone" / "I hate sleeping without my [person/item]." [06:38–06:41]
  5. **Founder Hooks [07:03]:**
     - *Angle 1:* "Explain why you built this company from the get-go" (highlighting personal failed attempts). [07:10–07:23]
     - *Angle 2:* Direct greeting: "Hi y'all, my name is [Name], and I am the founder of [Brand]." [07:30–07:35]
     - *Visual Element:* Name and "Co-Founder / Founder / CEO" explicitly displayed in text overlay (e.g., "Veda Gogineni, Co-Founder, EARTHFUL", "Nutrafol's CEO and Co-Founder Giorgos"). [08:14–08:24]
  6. **Partnership "In-Action" Hooks [09:25]:**
     - Place creator in the physical action of daily life (making breakfast, walking to car, grabbing keys) rather than sitting static in front of a ring light. [09:27–09:35]
  7. **The Emotional Hook [09:57]:**
     - High-emotion opening/cliffhanger showing crying, panic, or shock to provoke viewer intrigue.
     - *Formula:* "My biz partner is gonna be mad at me for showing you this..." [10:24]
     - *Formula:* "No but why did I start crying again thinking about last night?..." [10:26]
     - *Formula:* "No bc why did I literally start crying when I saw [event]..." [10:28]
  8. **Why Did No One Tell Me Hook [10:37]:**
     - *Formula:* "Why didn't anyone tell me [unexpected truth/hack]?" [10:39]
     - *Formula:* "Why did no one tell me I was doing [routine task] all wrong?" [10:42]
     - *Formula:* "Why didn't anyone tell me?!" [11:08]
  9. **If You... Hook [11:18]:**
     - *Formula:* "If you're someone who likes to [habit/behavior]..." [11:20]
     - *Formula:* "If you only have time for [routine]..." [11:22]
     - *Formula:* "This is for my [Profession/Group] who spend all day [activity] and struggle through [pain]." [11:29]
- **The True Hook Structure (4-layer framework for 0:00–0:03) [03:09–03:25]:**
  1. *Text overlay hook* (onscreen title/captions). [03:14]
  2. *Sound hook* (auditory trigger/music/SFX). [03:16]
  3. *Visual hook* (dynamic onscreen movement). [03:19]
  4. *General vibe* (lighting, color palette, font selection). [03:20–03:34]
- **B-Roll Visual Hooks [04:12–04:46]:**
  - *Liquid drip:* Thick paint/serum dripping or hanging suspended. [04:12–04:18]
  - *The squeeze:* Close-up of fruit/liquid being squeezed, sped up or played in reverse. [04:19–04:24]
  - *Abstract surreal art:* Melting/liquid psychedelic digital art (ideal for heavy emotional hooks). [04:25–04:32]
  - *Explosions:* Fruit/items exploding in slow motion (best stacked *after* the initial 1-second hook). [04:33–04:46]
- **Multiple Hook Stacking [04:58–05:04]:** Stacking 2 to 3 hooks within the first 3 seconds (e.g., verbal hook + text hook + explosion visual clip).

## Mistakes / warnings called out
- **The "old" media buying hook strategy:** Media buyers and strategists wrongly think they must state the problem immediately or show the physical product/service within the first 5 seconds to target consumers; under modern Meta algorithms, this restricts scale. [00:06–00:19]
- **Listening to marketing gurus regarding founder ads:** Gurus claim introducing the founder ("Hi, my name is Dani, founder of...") should be avoided because "no one cares." Presenter proves this intro regularly becomes a top-spending winner because it disarms the viewer and mimics organic content. [07:35–07:54]
- **Omitting founder title in text overlay:** Split testing shows that not placing the founder's name and title in text overlay almost always underperforms having it. [08:13–08:24]
- **Static creator product reviews:** Creators sitting still reading scripted ad copy signal "paid ad" immediately; they should be filmed mid-action doing daily routines. [09:27–09:54]
- **Misplacing explosion clips:** Avoid using explosion clips as the very first frame; place them immediately *after* the initial 1-second hook. [04:38–04:46]

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Apply the "Investment Hook" to Wardrobe Frustrations:** Pull review data or reddit complaints on cheap fabrics or bad fits, and open with: *"I spent $650 on fast-fashion jeans that ripped and shrank in 2 washes before I found this pair."* [00:47–00:59, 01:05–01:10]
2. **Utilize "POV: You Hate [Clothing Problem]":** Capitalize on the 10–15% winner statistic by scripting UGC hooks like: *"POV: You hate buying linen shirts because they wrinkle the second you sit down"* or *"POV: You hate stiff trousers when sitting at a desk all day."* [06:07–06:37]
3. **Run Founder Origin Creatives via Partnership Ads:** Film the brand founder explaining why they created the garment line (e.g., could never find dresses that fit both bust and waist), introduce themselves by name/title, and run the ad through the founder's personal Instagram account via Meta Partnership Ads. [07:10–07:35, 08:13–08:24, 09:02–09:10]
4. **Shift UGC from Talking Heads to "In-Action" Hooks:** Instruct creators to style the apparel while in motion (e.g., throwing on a coat while grabbing coffee, putting on sneakers while walking out the door) rather than standing statically in front of a wardrobe. [09:25–09:54]
5. **Implement the 4-Layer Hook Structure with Visual B-Roll:** Stack dynamic visual B-roll (e.g., macro fabric texture stretch, water droplets beading off water-resistant outerwear, rapid movement) within the first 3 seconds alongside high-contrast text overlay and audio soundbites to lift hook and hold rates. [03:09–03:34, 03:42–04:46]
