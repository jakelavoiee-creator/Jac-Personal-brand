## Creative specifics

### Formats & Structures
* **Facebook Ad Frameworks Shown [01:23]:**
  * **Ad 1: The "Future Pacing" Ad** (Frank Kern signature style: paint the picture of life after purchase).
    * *Headline:* "Imagine opening the package and your son immediately grabs it."
  * **Ad 2: The "Mass Desire" Ad** (Tap into an existing desire, attach product).
    * *Headline:* "You know that kid who always looks effortlessly cool? Here's his mom's secret."
  * **Ad 3: The "Conversational Story" Ad** (Talk like a human, establish trust).
    * *Headline:* "A boy mom told me something that stuck with me."
  * **Ad 4: The "Agitate & Elevate" Ad** (Identify exact frustration, validate it hard, offer the exit).
    * *Headline:* "Boys' graphic tees are either ugly, overpriced, or falling apart by wash #4."

### Kern-Style Ad Copy Rules [01:28]
1. **No hard sell up front** – Earn trust before the ask.
2. **Second-person intimacy** – "You know that feeling", not "Our product does X".
3. **Specific over vague** – Example: "$88 for 4, organic cotton, wash #4" vs general claims.
4. **One CTA, stated plainly** – No fake urgency, no caps lock screaming. Example: `Build his bundle -> littleradthings.com` [01:25].

### Three Universal Rules for Prompt Engineering [15:08 - 15:42]
1. **Rule 1: Give Claude a Role.** Define who it is before asking anything (e.g., *"You are a copywriter for a premium streetwear brand"*). Every response filters through that lens [15:08].
2. **Rule 2: Show it what GOOD looks like.** Paste text or copy styles you love (e.g., Frank Kern, Eugene Schwartz, or specific competitor copy) [15:22].
3. **Rule 3: Specify the Output.** Explicitly set constraints: sentence count, word length, tone, exact inclusions, and explicit exclusions (e.g., "no hashtags", "no guru jargon") [15:34].

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Generic Typing = Generic Output [01:39]:** Opening a raw chat window, typing a one-sentence prompt, and expecting high-converting brand copy is the #1 mistake. Garbage in, garbage out [01:39 - 01:44].
2. **Not Using Projects (Zero Context State) [02:41 - 02:48]:** Starting every new chat session from scratch forces you to re-explain your brand, voice, products, and customer profile every single time [02:46].
3. **Skipping System Prompts / SOP Setup [07:58 - 08:00]:** Most brands skip taking the time to define roles and voice guidelines upfront, which leads to AI output sounding like generic "guru hype" [08:26].
4. **Copy-Pasting Output Back and Forth [11:27 - 11:32]:** Prompting AI to change single lines in a large document via chat causes context clutter. Use Canvas/Artifact inline editing instead [11:57 - 12:02].
5. **Paying Freelancers for Early Concept Mockups [18:27 - 18:35]:** Do not spend $800+/month on freelancers or design hours just to mock up campaign ideas or landing page layouts before they are validated. Use Claude Design to prototype in 10 minutes [18:38].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Build a Dedicated Claude Project for Every Ad Account / Brand [02:40]:** Stop using plain chat windows. Upload the brand's style guide, PDP catalog, historical top-performing ads, and customer demographics into a Claude Project once so every prompt carries 100% brand context [02:53].
2. **Dictate Prompts with Whisper Flow to 10x Iteration Speed [06:09, 12:17]:** Typing detailed prompts is a bottleneck. Dictating rich, conversational instructions via voice-to-text allows media buyers to give complex context in seconds [06:25].
3. **Use the 3-Rule Prompting Framework [15:08]:** Always assign a **Role** (e.g., *Streetwear Copywriter*), provide an **Example of Good** (e.g., *Frank Kern style*), and set **Explicit Output Constraints** (e.g., *2 sentences, no hashtags, soft CTA*) to eliminate generic AI fluff [15:08 - 15:38].
4. **Refine Deliverables Directly inside Canvas/Artifacts [10:40, 12:00]:** Generate full PDP sets, email launch sequences, or ad copy angles into an Artifact panel, then highlight and edit specific lines inline rather than re-running full chat prompts [12:07].
5. **Use Claude Design to Prototype Landing Pages Before Dev [17:16, 18:37]:** Test landing page concepts, offer structures, and advertorial layouts in minutes via Claude Design before sending specs to design teams or developers, saving hundreds of dollars in prep costs [18:50].
