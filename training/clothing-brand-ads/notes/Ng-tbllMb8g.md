### Targeting & Scaling Rules [01:40–02:07, 05:07–05:21]
* **Creative IS Targeting:** Meta’s algorithm uses the creative itself to find the target audience.
* **The Scaling Mismatch Failure:** If creative is built only for warm/retargeting audiences (Most Aware) but deployed into cold prospecting campaigns, the warmest sub-audience converts quickly, after which the ad performance collapses and CPA/CAC drastically spikes as budget scales.
* **Channel Strategy:** Master **one** primary traffic channel (e.g., Meta ads) before diversifying [13:20–13:45].

### Creative Formulas by Awareness Stage [01:08–05:21]

| Stage | Buyer State | Recommended Creative / Angle | Traffic Source / Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Most Aware** | Knows product, needs a reason to act today | Hard offer, price drop, limited drop, direct promo, deadline | Email, SMS list, Brand Search, Retargeting |
| **2. Product-Aware** | Knows competitors, comparing options | "Us vs. Them" comparison (e.g., *Brand vs. Lululemon*), reviews, support DMs, founder story | Comparison articles, review sites, competitor retargeting |
| **3. Solution-Aware** | Wants outcome, doesn't know your brand | Styling guides, how-to content, "Effortless capsule wardrobe", "One tee for everything" | Organic search, SEO, Pinterest, styling content |
| **4. Problem-Aware** | Has pain point ("shirts shrink", "leggings roll down") | Before/After, pain-first hooks, calling out bad fits/fabrics | Cold social ads, UGC, problem-agitation video ads |
| **5. Unaware** | Doesn't recognize problem or category | Identity-driven content, scroll-stoppers, trend/social acceptance angles | UGC seeding, influencer ads, cold TikTok/IG/YT ads |

## Offer, product, landing page & Shopify CRO

### The 6x1 Method Framework [13:00–14:37]
* **Six Ones / One Year:**
  1. **One Audience:** Single defined target avatar.
  2. **One Message:** Clear core value proposition.
  3. **One Offer:** Strong, simple offer structure.
  4. **One Product:** Focus on scaling 1 hero SKU.
  5. **One Traffic Source:** Master 1 ad channel (e.g., Meta).
  6. **One Year:** Hold steady for 4 x 90-day sprints.

### Offer Calculator & Promo Math [13:47–14:14, 15:32–16:30]
* **Inputs required:** Average Order Value ($125 default), Product Cost ($40), Fees % ($6), Shipping & Fulfillment ($12), Returns ($2), Cost Per Customer ($35), Orders/Month (150), Discount % (20%), Gated Min Spend ($150), Gift Cost ($10).
* **Offer Risk & Margin Analysis:**
  * *Flat 20% off:* **Risky** (5% margin, requires +470% order volume to break even).
  * *20% off orders $150+:* **Moderate** (20% margin, +20% volume lift).
  * *Gift with purchase:* **Safe** (15% margin, +25% volume lift).
  * *BOGO (Buy 1 Get 1):* **Unprofitable** (-9% margin).
  * *Buy 2 Get 1:* **Safe** (26% margin, -57% breakeven order requirement).
  * *Buy 3 Get 1:* **Safe** (38% margin, -80% breakeven order requirement).
  * *BOGO 50% off:* **Safe** (25% margin, -50% breakeven order requirement).
* **Recommended Core Offers:** Buy 3 Get 1, Buy 2 Get 1, or BOGO 50% off paired with risk reversals (fit guarantee, free exchanges) [14:14].

### Clothing Offer Equation (COE) Math Formula [15:32–16:30]

$$\text{COE Score} = \left[ \frac{S \times QF}{W + SF + PS} \right] \times 9$$

* **Variables (Each scored 1–10; 10 = excellent/no friction, 1 = severe problem):**
  * $S$ = **Style Aspirations** (Brand aesthetic vs. customer identity) — *Numerator (Pull)*
  * $QF$ = **Quality & Fit Confidence** (Trust in fit, durability, construction) — *Numerator (Pull)*
  * $W$ = **Waiting Time** (Fulfillment + shipping speed) — *Denominator (Friction)*
  * $SF$ = **Shopping Friction** (Checkout ease, site speed, return policy clarity) — *Denominator (Friction)*
  * $PS$ = **Price Sensitivity** (Perceived value vs. discount reliance) — *Denominator (Friction)*

* **Score Benchmarks:**
  * **Perfect (100):** $\left[\frac{10 \times 10}{1 + 1 + 1}\right] \times 9 = 100$
  * **Worst (0.3):** $\left[\frac{1 \times 1}{10 + 10 + 10}\right] \times 9 = 0.3$
  * **Average / All 5s (15):** $\left[\frac{5 \times 5}{5 + 5 + 5}\right] \times 9 = 15$
  * **Ranges:**
    * `70–100`: Strong brand, low friction.
    * `35–69`: Potential, specific areas need work.
    * `0–34`: Major friction blocking growth.

## Mistakes / warnings called out

1. **Generic Ad Messaging:** Running a single ad creative expecting it to convert all 5 awareness stages simultaneously [01:25].
2. **Scaling Retargeting Creatives to Cold Audiences:** Scaling Meta budget on warm-audience ad formats causes customer acquisition costs (CAC) to surge uncontrollably [01:53–02:07, 05:07–05:21].
3. **Fatigued Market Claims:** Relying on overused claims like "premium quality" or "limited drop" in saturated market sophistication stages [07:29–07:36].
4. **Fleeting Trend Content:** Relying exclusively on temporary pop-culture trends that quickly fatigue instead of evergreen positioning angles [09:00–09:14].
5. **Greenwashing Skepticism:** Making generic "eco-friendly" claims that trigger consumer distrust due to market saturation [09:48–10:00].
6. **Strategy Fragmentation:** Splitting ad budget across multiple products, channels, and target avatars at once instead of committing to the 6x1 framework for a full year [13:30–13:45, 14:20–14:37].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer

1. **Match Creative Hooks to Awareness Stages:** Do not run product-heavy or discount-heavy ads to cold audiences; use problem-agitation and identity-driven UGC for top-of-funnel (Unaware / Problem-Aware) prospecting.
2. **Ditch Saturated Claims:** Audit competitor ad messaging. If competitors all claim "premium quality" or "best fit", test mechanism-focused copy (e.g., cost-per-wear, zero-shrink construction).
3. **Audit Offers Prior to Scaling Budget:** Calculate profit margins and breakeven order volumes using bundle offers (Buy 2 Get 1, Buy 3 Get 1) rather than flat 20% discounts to keep AOV high enough to absorb Meta CAC.
4. **Commit to the 6x1 Method:** Focus Meta ad spend on 1 winning hero product, 1 offer structure, and 1 core message for 90-day sprints before introducing new SKUs.
5. **Leverage AI SOP Automation:** Use Claude's "Record a Skill" tool to capture media-buying workflows (e.g., daily campaign triage, reporting, offer auditing) and turn them into repeatable prompts.
