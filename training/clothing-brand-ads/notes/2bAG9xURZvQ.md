## One-line thesis
Meta is replacing manual sales campaigns with a new defaulted "Advantage+ Sales Campaign" setting (requiring CBO, broad Advantage+ audiences, and automatic placements), which differs from "Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns" by allowing multiple ad sets (creative batch folders) inside a single CBO campaign [00:00–04:30].

1. **Check Ad Account Interface Version:** Create a new campaign under the "Sales" objective to check if the account shows the legacy setup selection screen or defaults directly into the new interface [01:00, 01:30].

## Mistakes / warnings called out
* **Confusing ASC Sales with ASC Shopping:** Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC Shopping) do not allow multiple ad sets and include existing customer budget caps, whereas Advantage+ Sales Campaigns allow multi-ad set structures under CBO [00:10–00:15, 04:15–04:25].
* **Accidentally Disabling Advantage+ Status:** Switching a campaign from CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) to ABO (Ad Set Budget) automatically toggles off the "Advantage+ Sales Campaign" status [01:56–02:03].

## Top 5 takeaways for a clothing brand media buyer
1. **Understand ASC Sales vs ASC Shopping:** Advantage+ Sales Campaigns give you the benefit of automated delivery optimization while preserving the ability to create separate ad sets for creative organization [02:12–02:45, 04:15].
2. **Keep CBO Enabled:** Always use Campaign Budget Optimization (Advantage Campaign Budget) when launching Advantage+ Sales Campaigns to keep the automated status active [01:55–02:08].
3. **Use Ad Sets as Creative Folders:** Organize creative batches into separate ad sets (1 ad set per batch, 3 ad variations inside) under a single CBO campaign to manage testing efficiently [05:00–05:10].
4. **Use Custom Exclusions for Prospecting:** Set custom audience exclusions at the ad set level within Advantage+ Sales Campaigns to ensure budget goes toward acquiring new customers rather than retargeting [03:40–03:45].
5. **Adapt to Meta's Broad Defaults:** Prepare for Meta to progressively eliminate manual campaign controls by structuring ad testing around broad targeting, quality creative variations, and campaign-level budget allocation [01:25–01:50, 05:35–05:48].
