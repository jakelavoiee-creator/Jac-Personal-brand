## One-line thesis
High-converting Meta video ads require a strict 3-part formula (**Hook -> Body -> CTA**), where the Hook purely stops the scroll with curiosity, the Body sells using static-ad-proven messaging (UMP/UMS), and the CTA handles price/quality objections using risk reversal and stacked social proof.

### Full 6-Part Winning Ad Script Structure [08:34 - 10:45]

| # | Script Stage | Section Type | Editing/Messaging Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HOOK** | Intro (0–3s) | Call out ICP + Call out specific angle/curiosity message. |
| 2 | **STORYTELLING** | Body | Identify the core problem; relate directly to the ICP's pain points. |
| 3 | **UMP / UMS** | Body | **Unique Mechanism Problem vs. Unique Mechanism Solution:** Explain why your product works while competitors fail. |
| 4 | **RISK REVERSAL** | CTA / Outro | Provide guarantees (30-day money back, free US returns, warranties). |
| 5 | **TESTIMONIALS** | CTA / Outro | Stack social proof (star ratings, volume sold, customer review cards). |
| 6 | **CTA** | Outro | Strong call to action paired with an offer, urgency, or scarcity. |

## Mistakes / warnings

* ❌ **Mistake #1 (Hook):** Trying to sell or force a website click in the first 3 seconds. *The hook's only job is to stop the scroll* [00:32 - 00:38].
* ❌ **Mistake #2 (Body):** Letting creators or influencers "freeball" scripts or film unscripted GRWM (Get Ready With Me) videos without a data-backed copy strategy [03:26 - 03:35].
* ❌ **Mistake #3 (Body):** Writing video ad scripts on unvalidated angles. *Always test messaging using static ads first* before investing in video production [02:27 - 02:44].
* ❌ **Mistake #4 (CTA):** Ending ads with weak lazy CTAs like *"Check it out on our website"* or *"Selling out fast"*. You must explicitly resolve price/quality objections, present risk reversal, and show stacked proof [05:00 - 05:18].

## Reproduction recipe with ffmpeg/scripts

### 1. Build Side-by-Side Dual Frame (Presenter 45% Left, 9:16 Ad 45% Right)
```bash
ffmpeg -i presenter_1080p.mp4 -i ugc_ad_720x1280.mp4 -filter_complex \
"[0:v]scale=960:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=960:1080[left]; \
 [1:v]scale=607:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease[right]; \
 [left][right]hstack=inputs=2[v]" \
-map "[v]" -map 0:a -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset fast output_side_by_side.mp4
```

### 2. Burn Staggered Highlight Captions (White text with Yellow key words)
```bash
ffmpeg -i raw_input.mp4 -vf \
"drawtext=fontfile=Montserrat-ExtraBold.ttf:text='WORKER MADE TANKS FOR THE BOYS':x=(w-text_w)/2:y=h-th-150:fontsize=48:fontcolor=white:borderw=3:bordercolor=black, \
 drawtext=fontfile=Montserrat-ExtraBold.ttf:text='WITH CHEST ENHANCING TECH':x=(w-text_w)/2:y=h-th-90:fontsize=48:fontcolor=yellow:borderw=3:bordercolor=black" \
-c:a copy output_captioned.mp4
```

### 3. Audio Mix with Voiceover Ducking
```bash
ffmpeg -i voiceover.wav -i background_music.mp3 -filter_complex \
"[1:a]volume=0.12[bg]; \
 [0:a][bg]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.03:ratio=4:release=300[mixed]" \
-map 0:v? -map "[mixed]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k final_ad_audio_mix.mp4
```

## Top 7 takeaways for editing Aura Miami founder and UGC ads

1. **Structure Every Aura Miami Ad Around 3 Pillars:** Edit strictly to the sequence: **Hook (0-3s)** -> **Body (Proven USP/UMP)** -> **CTA (Objection Handling & Social Proof)**.
2. **Design Visual Hooks to Filter ICP:** For an Aura Miami streetwear ad, open with immediate visual contrast—e.g., side-by-side comparison of boxy oversized heavyweight tees vs. thin, stretched-out fast-fashion shirts within second 1.
3. **Use Curiosity Copy in the Hook:** Avoid saying *"Buy our new drop"*. Use curiosity hooks like *"Why streetwear guys are throwing away their thin store-bought tees..."*
4. **Script the Body Using Static Ad Winners:** Edit the video body to hit the messaging angles (heavyweight GSM cotton, shape retention, tailored drop-shoulder fit) that already won during static image testing.
5. **Reframe Price Objections in the CTA:** When editing founder-led footage addressing high price points, overlay high-impact text cards (*"NOT CHEAP FAST FASHION"*) followed by close-ups of reinforced stitching and thick collar ribs [06:39].
6. **Stack Risk Reversal + Real Social Proof at the Outro:** End video ads by displaying dynamic 5-star review carousels, verified buyer metrics (*"Over 45,000 hats shipped"*), and text callouts for **Free Shipping + 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee** [06:52 - 07:34].
7. **Pace Captions for Rapid Mobile Consumption:** Style captions with center-weighted, bold 2–4 word lines in high-contrast yellow/white to maximize retention on mute feeds [01:15].
