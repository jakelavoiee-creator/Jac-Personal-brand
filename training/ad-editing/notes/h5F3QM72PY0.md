## Motion graphics / animations (types, timing, how to reproduce simply)

1. **Text Animators (After Effects Native):**
   * **Position & Range Selector:** Set Position Y offset to `+70px`. Animate Range Selector `Start` from `0%` to `100%`.
   * **Ease High / Ease Low:** Change `Ease High` to `100%` for exponential deceleration.
   * **Shape Modes:** Switch from `Square` to `Ramp Up` / `Smooth` for flowing entrance.
2. **Graph Editor Curves:**
   * Apply `F9` (Easy Ease).
   * Pull the right velocity handle to the far left (approx. `85% - 90%` influence) to create an aggressive ease-out curve.
3. **Per-Character 3D:**
   * Enable `Enable Per-Character 3D` in the text layer Animator menu.
   * Animate `X Rotation` or `Z Position` per character using the Range Selector.

## Top 7 takeaways for editing Aura Miami founder and UGC ads

1. **Open with Immediate Kinetic Energy (0-3s):** Combine a bold, high-contrast text pop with an audible pop/whoosh SFX right at frame 0 to stop the scroll on TikTok and Meta.
2. **Use Graphic Cutouts as Pattern Interrupts:** Interspersed paper-cutout collages or bright color overlays every 10–12 seconds prevent visual fatigue when the founder is speaking to the camera.
3. **Always Custom-Ease Text Keyframes:** Never leave text slide-ins on linear keyframes. Use After Effects Graph Editor (80%+ speed influence) so drop announcements and promo codes snap dynamically onto the screen.
4. **Implement "Before vs. After" Visual Splits:** When demonstrating product quality, fabric details, or fit (or software tools like HitPaw in the video), use a direct side-by-side comparison screen with bold labeled captions.
5. **Keep On-Screen Text Punchy and Chunked:** Display captions in short 3-to-6 word phrases using heavy, clean sans-serif typography. Highlight key terms (e.g., "100% COTTON", "LIMITED DROP") in brand accent colors (Yellow/Magenta).
6. **Integrate On-Screen Keyboard / Shortcut Callouts for How-To UGC:** When creating "How to Style" or "Behind the Brand" tutorials, add animated callout boxes with icon overlays to make steps feel actionable and structured.
7. **Maintain Tight Audio-Visual Synchronization:** Pair every visual entry (text pop, image cut, zoom shift) with a subtle audio cue (click, paper rustle, or pop SFX) to establish a polished, premium brand feel.
