## Editing techniques

### 1. Dynamic Graphic Text Pop-Ins (Talking-Head Overlays)
* **What it is:** Animated text and lower thirds appearing in sync with spoken key phrases to reinforce messaging without obscuring the presenter.
* **WHEN to use it:** Every time a key term, metric, quote, or structural concept is introduced in talking-head footage.
* **Parameters:**
  * **Duration:** 1.5s to 4.0s per phrase.
  * **Cut / Motion Frequency:** Pop/slide in every 2–4 seconds during talking-head segments.
  * **Font:** Sans-serif bold (Gothic / Inter / Montserrat family).
  * **Size:** Title/Headers ~60–80pt, Subtext ~36–48pt.
  * **Position:** Left-third or right-third of frame when main subject is centered, or centered lower third (`y=0.75*h`).
  * **Colors:** Deep Magenta / Hot Pink (`#C2185B` or `#E91E63`) paired with stark White (`#FFFFFF`) on dark/shaded backgrounds.

### 2. Split-Screen UGC / B-Roll Comparison Layout
* **What it is:** Dual vertical frames (1:1 or 9:16 side-by-side or stacked top/bottom) showing a presenter on one side/top and product demonstration / B-roll on the other side/bottom.
* **WHEN to use it:** Demonstrating a problem vs. solution, showing podcast-style interviews, or displaying product usage alongside explanatory voiceover.
* **Parameters:**
  * **Layout Split:** 50/50 horizontal split (top/bottom) or 50/50 vertical split (left/right) with a 4–8px solid divider line (`#E91E63` accent or neutral dark).
  * **Cut Frequency within B-roll frame:** Cuts every 1.2–2.5 seconds while talking-head video runs continuously.
  * **Zoom / Scale:** B-roll scaled to fill bounding box (105–115% crop to remove black bars).

### 3. Screen Mockup & UI Card Overlays
* **What it is:** Floating 2.5D or flat rounded-corner UI cards showing social media reviews (Reddit, Trustpilot, Amazon, Google Reviews) or ad interfaces.
* **WHEN to use it:** Presenting social proof, research sources, or competitor ad examples.
* **Parameters:**
  * **Corner Radius:** 16px–24px rounded rectangles with light dropshadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`, blur: 20px, offset `y=10px`).
  * **Scale:** 75%–90% of screen width.
  * **Animation:** Spring/elastic ease-out pop-up (0.25s duration: scale `0.8 -> 1.0` with `cubic-bezier(0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.275)`).

## Captions & on-screen text

* **Style:** Modern bold sans-serif, mixed case, high-contrast flat graphics.
* **Words Per Line:** 2 to 5 words max per line for high readability.
* **Positioning:**
  * Talking-head overlays: Left or right side of subject (`x=0.08*w` or `x=0.55*w`), vertically centered (`y=0.4*h`).
  * Full-screen text graphics: Center-aligned (`x=center`, `y=center`).
* **Animation:**
  * Entrance: Quick directional slide-in from left (`x`: `-100px -> 0`) or scale-up pop (`0.85 -> 1.0`).
  * Exit: Hard cut or quick fade-out (0.1s).
* **Emphasis Rules:** Key value propositions, numbers, or emotional trigger words are highlighted in **Magenta/Pink** (`#E91E63`), while supporting text remains clean **White**.

## Sound design

* **Voiceover:** Clean, studio-grade condenser mic recording (Røde/Shure style), compressed with a tight limiter, noise-gated, and EQ’d with a slight low-end boost (~100Hz) and high-end clarity sparkle (~5kHz).
* **Music:** Low-fi instrumental hip-hop / chill electronic beat running continuously under the speech.
  * **Relative Level:** Speech at 0 dB; music bed set to **-18 dB to -22 dB**.
  * **Ducking:** Automatic sidechain ducking lowering music by additional **-3 dB to -4 dB** whenever voiceover is present.
* **SFX Placement & Types:**
  * **Pop / Click SFX:** Placed exactly at the keyframe of every text graphic slide/pop-in (`00:00`, `00:05`, `00:20`, `01:11`, `06:16`, `10:25`, `15:13`).
  * **Whoosh / Swip SFX:** Accompanying fast slide transitions, UI cards, or graphic screen swaps (`00:01`, `00:13`, `00:27`, `02:01`, `02:41`, `04:20`).
  * **Camera Shutter / Paper Tear SFX:** Used when displaying Reddit thread screenshots or review card cutouts (`02:10`, `02:42`).

## Pacing & structure

* **Cut Rhythm:**
  * **Talking Head:** Main camera angle cuts or scales up slightly (5% push-in) every 4–7 seconds to prevent visual fatigue.
  * **Graphic / B-Roll Overlays:** Motion graphics, text overlays, or external video clips insert every **2 to 3.5 seconds**.
* **Pattern Interrupts:**
  * `00:15` Full screen tablet mockup swap.
  * `00:20` 5-point circular roadmap overlay surrounding the presenter.
  * `00:40` Historical black-and-white printing press footage insert.
  * `01:15` Behind-the-scenes B-roll of editor typing at desk.
  * `09:26`, `09:42`, `11:18`, `11:41`, `11:49`, `12:27`, `13:12` External ad examples (UGC, street interviews, founder videos, podcast clips).
* **B-Roll Ratio:** ~35% primary talking-head studio footage, ~65% motion graphics, screenshots, slide cards, and external UGC/ad examples.
* **Length Target:** 18 minutes overall (educational breakdown format). Direct response ad clips shown within range from 15s to 45s.

## Workflow & tools

* **Software:** Adobe Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve (editing & audio ducking) + After Effects (custom motion graphic templates & overlays).
* **Aspect Ratios:**
  * Main YouTube Video: **16:9** (`1920x1080` or `3840x2160`).
  * Ad Examples & UGC inserts: **9:16** (`1080x1920`) framed in 16:9 canvas using side-by-side or blurred background fills.
* **Export Settings:**
  * Video Codec: H.264 / MP4 or ProRes 422.
  * Target Bitrate: 20–35 Mbps (1080p60) or 50–80 Mbps (4K60).
  * Audio: AAC 320 kbps, 48kHz Stereo.
* **Safe Zones:** Keep all lower third graphics at least 10% inside margins (`y = 0.85` or higher) to avoid native YouTube/Meta UI overlays.

---

## Mistakes / warnings

* **Don't Copy Competitors Blindly (`04:45`):** Copying competitor ads directly makes your product blend in with everyone targeting the same audience.
* **Style Over Substance (`16:28`):** Prioritizing glossy aesthetics over messaging clarity ruins conversion rates. *"Clarity > Aesthetics"*.
* **Over-Editing / Cheap Template Transitions (`16:46`):** Using random flashy transitions distracts from the core emotional message. The edit must serve the emotion, not mask weak creative ideas.
* **Ignoring Research (`01:23`):** Trying to write ad scripts by staring at a blank document without analyzing real customer reviews or complaints leads to generic messaging.

## Reproduction recipe with ffmpeg/scripts

The following bash script creates a side-by-side comparison template (e.g., Founder/UGC video on left, B-roll/Product Demo on right) with a pink accent divider line, burned-in text overlays, and automatic music ducking using `ffmpeg`:

```bash
#!/usr/bin/env bash
# Extrapolated FFmpeg recipe for dual-frame ad layout with text overlay & audio ducking

LEFT_INPUT="founder_talking_916.mp4"
RIGHT_INPUT="product_broll_916.mp4"
MUSIC_INPUT="background_track.mp3"
OUTPUT="aura_miami_ad_output.mp4"

ffmpeg -y \
  -i "$LEFT_INPUT" \
  -i "$RIGHT_INPUT" \
  -i "$MUSIC_INPUT" \
  -filter_complex "
    [0:v]scale=960:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=960:1080[left];
    [1:v]scale=960:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=960:1080[right];
    [left][right]hstack=inputs=2[combined];
    
    color=c=#E91E63:s=8x1080[divider];
    [combined][divider]overlay=x=956:y=0[base_layout];

    [base_layout]drawtext=fontfile=/path/to/Montserrat-Bold.ttf: \
      text='STREETWEAR REDEFINED': \
      fontcolor=white:fontsize=48: \
      box=1:boxcolor=black@0.6:boxborderw=12: \
      x=(w-text_w)/2:y=80[video_out];

    [0:a]volume=1.0[voice];
    [2:a]volume=0.15[music];
    [voice][music]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:dropout_transition=2[audio_out]
  " \
  -map "[video_out]" \
  -map "[audio_out]" \
  -c:v libx264 -preset fast -crf 18 \
  -c:a aac -b:a 256k \
  "$OUTPUT"
```

## Top 7 takeaways for editing Aura Miami founder and UGC ads

1. **Lead with Customer Language, Not Brand Jargon (`01:45`, `04:15`):** Extract exact phrases from customer reviews, Reddit threads, and ad comments to write on-screen text overlays for Aura Miami.
2. **Anchor the Hook in the First 3 Seconds (`07:08`):** Use a high-contrast text reveal, pop SFX, and an intriguing problem statement or visual pattern interrupt right at `00:00`.
3. **Open an Information Loop (`07:43`):** Structure founder introductions or UGC intros so the viewer must keep watching to discover the result (e.g., *"We tested 5 heavyweight hoodies..."*).
4. **Match Format to Funnel Intent (`10:36`, `14:21`):**
   * **Top of Funnel:** Authentic street interviews & founder origin stories.
   * **Middle of Funnel:** Split-screen mashups & mini VSL product breakdowns highlighting quality/fabric details.
   * **Bottom of Funnel:** High-contrast static ads, social proof review compilations, & time-sensitive discount offers.
5. **Prioritize Speed and Output Over Excessive Perfection (`16:11`):** Fast creative testing reveals winning angles faster than spending weeks polishing a single edit.
6. **Emphasize Clarity Over Flashy Effects (`16:28`):** Ensure every text caption, fabric detail, and brand callout is instantly legible on mobile screens without cluttered motion graphics.
7. **Use Editing to Amplify Emotion (`16:46`):** Time cuts and music cues to heighten key moments of enthusiasm, frustration with competitors, or pride in product craftsmanship.
