## One-line thesis
Direct-response UGC and founder-led video ads convert highest when they open with an immediate visual pain-point hook (0–3s), cut fluff to stay under 20–30 seconds, use conversational "friend-to-friend" language rather than scripted sales pitches, and present native social-first captions and close-up product/branding within the first 10–15 seconds.

## Editing techniques

### 1. Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Split Layout
* **What it is:** The host/reviewer remains full-frame on the left side of the screen while the reviewed 9:16 vertical UGC clip plays inside a vertical smartphone frame overlay on the right side.
* **When to use it:** For video reviews, reaction-style commentary, split comparison, or showing product usage while a founder or creator speaks.
* **Parameters:**
  * **Main Canvas:** 16:9 widescreen (1920x1080).
  * **Inset Canvas:** Vertical 9:16 (approx. 540x960 inside canvas), positioned at X = 1250px, Y = 60px.
  * **Border Radius:** 24px corner rounding on the inset video frame.
  * **Drop Shadow:** Dark shadow opacity 0.35, blur radius 20px, Y-offset 10px.

### 2. Grid Graphic Overlays & Text Callouts
* **What it is:** Retro 3D wireframe grid backdrop used during lower-third text callouts and section transitions.
* **When to use it:** At section transitions or key thematic emphasis (e.g., displaying numbers "1" through "7" or key text terms like "THAT CONVERTS").
* **Parameters:**
  * **Font:** Bold Montserrat or Helvetica Heavy, all-caps.
  * **Text Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  * **Transition Duration:** 0.25s fast slide-in from right or pop-in scale (0% to 100% with cubic-bezier easing).

### 3. Green-Screen / Website Scroll Overlay
* **What it is:** Creator overlay superimposed over a brand website or product landing page screenshot (seen in Ad #4 [06:50]).
* **When to use it:** Demonstrating price comparisons, product collections, or social proof.
* **Parameters:**
  * **Scale:** Creator cut-out scaled to ~45% height in bottom-right corner.
  * **Background Scroll Speed:** Smooth linear scroll (150px/sec down the page).

## Hook construction (first 0–3s)

### Visual Hook Analysis
* **Bad Hook Example ([00:57], [11:16]):** Static close-up of product packaging or static talking head. Host notes that viewers scroll past immediately because it looks like a commercial.
* **Good Hook Example ([04:49], [06:46]):** Dynamic movement or clear visual problem depiction—e.g., creator hunched over in bed in pain [04:49] or creator walking with luggage in full stylish outfit [06:46].

### Multi-Sensory Formula for 0–3s
1. **Visual (0–1s):** Start *in media res* with high action or raw emotion (e.g., holding ruined hair, dramatic motion, unboxing pop).
2. **On-Screen Text (0–2s):** 3–5 word bold headline calling out the exact target audience or problem (e.g., *"Perfect Bottega look-alike for Europe"* [06:46] or *"If you're a new mom who lost her booty..."* [10:21]).
3. **Sound (0–3s):** A sharp sound effect (swoosh/pop) synchronized with a clear, relatable verbal opening line (no "Hey guys!").

## Captions & on-screen text

* **Style:** TikTok/Reels native aesthetic. White text with black rounded rectangle background pill or semi-bold sans-serif font (TikTok Sans / Proxima Nova Bold).
* **Pacing & Words per Line:** 2 to 4 words per line max. Word-by-word or short-phrase captioning sync.
* **Screen Position:** Lower-middle third (Y-position: 70–80% from top of 9:16 frame) to avoid native UI overlay obstruction (like TikTok/Reels comment & like buttons).
* **Emphasis Rules:** High-contrast color highlight (Bright Green `#00FF66` or Yellow `#FFE600`) on emotional trigger words (*"pain"*, *"free shipping"*, *"save $60"*).

---

## Sound design

* **SFX Types & Placement:**
  * **Whoosh/Swoosh:** Placed at every video cut/transition and visual text pop-in.
  * **Pop / UI Click:** Placed when stickers, thumbs up/down icons, or star ratings appear [00:26].
* **Music Choice & Mixing Levels:**
  * Upbeat lo-fi / electronic beat playing continuously under dialogue.
  * **Relative dB Levels:** Voice normalized to **-3 dB to -1 dB** peak; background music ducked to **-22 dB to -18 dB** relative to voice.
* **Audio Quality Tip ([06:37]):** Host explicitly advises creators to record voiceovers under a heavy blanket to eliminate room echo/reverb for clean, professional studio-quality audio.

## Pacing & structure

### Cut Rhythm & Pacing Guidelines
* **Cut Frequency:** Every 1.2 to 2.5 seconds in high-performing ads (e.g., Ad #4 at [06:46]).
* **Pattern Interrupts:** Alternate shot angles every 2–3 cuts:
  1. Full body fit check / action shot.
  2. Close-up texture / product detail.
  3. Green-screen website or review screenshot.
  4. Talking head to camera.
* **Target Video Length:** **15 to 25 seconds** maximum for direct response. Host emphasizes that cutting a 40–60 second ad down to 20 seconds drastically increases average watch time and completion rate, boosting algorithmic performance.

## Mistakes & warnings highlighted in video

1. **"Sounds Like a Commercial" ([01:07], [05:59], [12:18]):** Avoid formal, polished sales language like *"restoring my hair's vitality"* or listing ingredients monotone. Script like a text to a friend.
2. **Delayed Product Close-Up ([10:01]):** Failing to show a clear close-up of the product or brand logo in the first 10–15 seconds damages brand recall.
3. **B-Roll Dump at the End ([09:47]):** Tacking a random gym or usage montage at the very end drags out runtime and kills completion rate. Integrate B-roll *during* voiceover instead.
4. **Weak Static Hooks ([01:05]):** Opening on a box or plain pouch with audio asking a generic question (*"Ever wish for a quick...?"*) causes immediate drop-off.

## Reproduction recipe with FFmpeg / Python scripts

### 1. FFmpeg Command: Build Picture-in-Picture Layout with Rounded Corners
To recreate the review layout (Speaker on left, 9:16 vertical ad on right with rounded corners and drop shadow):

```bash
ffmpeg -i host_16x9.mp4 -i ugc_9x16.mp4 -filter_complex \
"[1:v]scale=450:800,format=rgba,geq=r='r(X,Y)':a='if(gt(abs(W/2-x),W/2-20)*gt(abs(H/2-y),H/2-20),if(lte(hypot(20-W/2+abs(W/2-x),20-H/2+abs(H/2-y)),20),255,0),255)'[rounded_ugc]; \
 [0:v][rounded_ugc]overlay=x=1350:y=140:shortest=1[outv]" \
-map "[outv]" -map 0:a -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset fast -c:a copy output_review.mp4
```

### 2. Python (MoviePy) Script: Auto-Ducking Background Music Under Voiceover
```python
from moviepy.editor import VideoFileClip, AudioFileClip, composite_audioclips

# Load host video and background track
video = VideoFileClip("host_spoken_audio.mp4")
voiceover = video.audio
bg_music = AudioFileClip("upbeat_beat.mp3").volumex(0.12) # Duck music to ~12% (-18dB)

# Loop background music to match video duration
bg_music = bg_music.time_transform(lambda t: t % bg_music.duration).set_duration(video.duration)

# Combine audio tracks
final_audio = composite_audioclips([voiceover, bg_music])
final_video = video.set_audio(final_audio)

final_video.write_videofile("final_edited_ad.mp4", codec="libx264", audio_codec="aac")
```

## Top 7 takeaways for editing Aura Miami founder & UGC ads

1. **Cut to the Pain Point Immediately (0–3s):** Open Aura Miami ads with a dramatic street-style visual—show the outfit problem or model dynamic movement right away instead of a static logo shot.
2. **Keep Runtime to 15–20 Seconds:** Condense founder talk and product showcases. High completion rate on 20s ads dramatically outperforms 40s ads on Meta/TikTok.
3. **Use Conversational "Friend-to-Friend" Scripting:** Replace formal brand buzzwords with authentic street language (e.g., replace *"our luxurious heavy-weight fabric restores style"* with *"I was obsessed with finding a hoodie that doesn't feel cheap after one wash"*).
4. **Show Product Close-Up Early (<10s):** Highlight Aura Miami's fabric quality, stitching, tags, or logos in a crisp macro shot within the first 10 seconds to anchor brand identity.
5. **Use Native TikTok-Style Text Overlays:** Use high-contrast pop-up text captions placed in safe zones with highlighted offer details (*"Free Shipping"*, *"30% Off"*).
6. **Dynamic Pattern Interrupts Every 2 Seconds:** Rapidly cycle between founder talking-head, green-screen website scrolling, outfit fit-checks, and close-up garment details.
7. **Record Clean Voiceovers Under Soundproofing:** Ensure narrator voiceovers are dry and reverberation-free (record in a closet or under a blanket if necessary) to maintain high perceived production value.
