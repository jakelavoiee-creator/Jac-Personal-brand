## Editing techniques

### 1. The Green Screen Effect [00:39 - 01:48]
* **What it is:** A composite visual technique where a creator cutout is keyed over a background image, website, product shot, macro texture video, or poor-quality image [00:41, 01:01, 01:12, 01:33].
* **WHEN to use it:** Use as a primary hook angle during creative testing to stop the scroll, or to simulate a realistic location (e.g., gym, retail store) without a location shoot [00:42, 01:40 - 01:48].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Cutout scale:** Bottom-corner or center overlay (~30–45% frame height) [00:43, 01:01].
  * **Backgrounds:** Product landing page UI [01:01], high-texture macro B-roll (grapefruit drops, carbonated bubbles) from Storyblocks [01:12], static low-res location photo [01:33].
  * **Duration:** 1.5–3.0 seconds for initial hook stage.

### 2. Dynamic B-Roll & Angle Variation [01:48 - 03:39]
* **What it is:** Interspersing talking-head shots with varied stock or creator-shot secondary footage from diverse perspectives [02:02, 03:13 - 03:29].
* **WHEN to use it:** Use throughout the body of UGC and founder ads to avoid visual fatigue and maintain viewer hold rate [02:08, 03:32 - 03:38].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Shot types:** Far wide shot [03:24], extreme close-up (macro eye/texture) [03:26], high-angle selfie [03:27], top-down / overhead [03:28], low-angle (down-up) [03:29].
  * **Source:** Native creator footage or 4K/HD stock footage (Storyblocks) [02:16, 02:34].

### 3. Rapid Micro-Pacing (<0.5s Cuts) [03:40 - 04:20]
* **What it is:** Trimming mundane or routine actions down to hyper-fast, sub-second sequential cuts [04:03 - 04:19].
* **WHEN to use it:** Use during product application, morning routines, unboxing, or step-by-step demonstrations to turn boring clips into high-energy sequences [04:06 - 04:19].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Clip duration:** $\le 0.5$ seconds per clip (12–15 frames at 30 fps) [04:16].
  * **Cut type:** Hard cuts on physical action beats.

### 4. Split Screen & Multi-Grid Layouts [04:20 - 04:41]
* **What it is:** Dividing the 9:16 vertical frame horizontally or vertically into 2 or 9 distinct media panels [04:23, 04:37].
* **WHEN to use it:** Use for direct Before vs. After comparisons, pairing creator commentary with product close-ups, or showcasing product variety [04:24 - 04:30, 04:37].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Vertical 2-Split:** 50/50 vertical division with text headers ("FULL" vs. "EMPTY" or "Before" vs. "After") [04:23].
  * **Grid Layout:** 3x3 photo/video matrix with keyframe zooms into single tiles [04:37 - 04:40].

### 5. J-Cuts and L-Cuts [04:41 - 06:02]
* **What it is:** Split-editing techniques where audio and video transition at staggered timestamps [05:00, 05:26].
  * **J-Cut:** Next scene's audio starts *before* the visual cut (audio leads visual) [05:00].
  * **L-Cut:** Current scene's audio continues *after* the visual cuts to the next clip (visual leads audio transition) [05:26].
* **WHEN to use it:**
  * **J-Cut:** Use at scene transitions to create intrigue, generate urgency, or imply a scene is being cut short [05:43].
  * **L-Cut:** Use during multi-character dialogue or founder-to-B-roll transitions for natural conversational flow [05:43].
* **Timeline appearance:** Audio track protrudes left (J-Cut) [05:09] or right (L-Cut) [05:24].

### 6. Native Social Platform Fonts [06:02 - 06:31]
* **What it is:** Replacing polished corporate brand fonts with UI fonts native to TikTok and Instagram [06:06 - 06:20].
* **WHEN to use it:** Mandatory for all UGC-style ad creative to prevent the video from looking like an ad [06:08 - 06:14].
* **Parameters:**
  * **TikTok Font:** `Proxima Nova Semibold` (white text inside rounded rectangular grey background box) [06:22 - 06:24].
  * **Instagram Stories Font:** `San Francisco` (iOS native style, clean sans-serif) [06:25 - 06:28].

### 7. Audio-First Design & Strategic SFX [06:31 - 06:58]
* **What it is:** Designing the edit specifically for sound-on engagement using punctuation sound effects [06:34 - 06:54].
* **WHEN to use it:** Always. 70% of Instagram Reels users view content with sound ON [06:45 - 06:49].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Key SFX library:** Pop, Whoosh, Wrong Buzzer, Emergency Siren, Sad Violin [06:56].
  * **Placement:** Align exactly with text overlays, graphics pop-ins, or visual cuts.

### 8. Hard Cut vs. Slow Push Zoom-Ins [06:58 - 07:21]
* **What it is:** Applying focal shifts via instant scale jumps or continuous gradual scale increases [07:08 - 07:14].
* **WHEN to use it:** Hard cuts for key takeaway punchlines; slow push-ins to build tension during founder storytelling [07:08 - 07:14].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Hard Cut Zoom:** Instant jump from 100% scale to ~115–125% scale on frame cut [07:08].
  * **Slow Push Zoom:** Linear/ease-in keyframed scale from 100% to 110% over 2.0–4.0 seconds [07:12 - 07:14].

### 9. Dynamic Native Overlays [07:21 - 08:00]
* **What it is:** Superimposing UI elements, press clippings, product reference photos, or native stickers over primary video [07:32 - 07:43].
* **WHEN to use it:** Use in the first 0–3 seconds to establish proof, answer customer comments, or add social context [07:27, 07:32].
* **Parameters:**
  * **TikTok Comment Bubble:** Top-left placed response card with original user handle [07:32].
  * **Press Clipping:** Forbes article screenshot cropped with red highlight callouts [07:38 - 07:39].
  * **Stickers/Emojis:** Native mobile emojis overlaid on focal points [07:41 - 07:43].

### 10. Double Person Effect (Split-Screen Clone) [08:01 - 08:36]
* **What it is:** A single creator acting out a conversation with themselves in the same room using a stationary split shot [08:10 - 08:14].
* **WHEN to use it:** Use as an attention-grabbing hook or skit-style problem-solution narrative [08:13 - 08:27].
* **Parameters:**
  * **Production Requirement:** Locked tripod, static lighting, zero camera movement [08:08 - 08:10].
  * **Edit Technique:** Linear edge mask down the center seam to composite Left Actor and Right Actor clips.

## Captions & on-screen text
* **Font selection:** `Proxima Nova Semibold` (TikTok style) or `San Francisco` (IG Story style) [06:22 - 06:28].
* **Formatting:** White text on dark translucent rounded pills or direct text with subtle drop shadows [06:22, 06:27].
* **Line count:** Maximum 1–3 short lines per screen card [04:28, 06:22].
* **Rule:** **NEVER** use corporate brand style guide fonts on UGC video ads—it immediately alerts the viewer that it is an ad and lowers performance [06:06 - 06:14].

## Sound design
* **Sound-on status:** 70% of Reels users watch with audio enabled [06:47 - 06:49].
* **SFX Types & Placement:**
  * `Whoosh` for visual screen transitions and pop-up overlays [06:56].
  * `Pop` for sticker/emoji and caption additions [06:56].
  * `Wrong Buzzer` / `Emergency Siren` for pain-point highlights [06:56].
  * `Sad Violin` for dramatic problem statements [06:56].
* **Audio ducking:** Voiceover must sit clearly at the top of the mix, with background music ducked down 12–18 dB under spoken dialogue.

## Pacing & structure
* **Cut rhythm:** Hyper-fast pacing; individual action shots cut down to $\le 0.5$ seconds [04:16].
* **B-roll ratio:** High B-roll density. Combine creator A-roll with stock assets (Storyblocks) and macro textures [01:12, 02:02, 02:27].
* **Pattern interrupts:** Alternate every 1.5–2.5 seconds between A-roll, B-roll, green-screen overlays, hard-zoom focal cuts, and sound effect accents [03:13 - 03:29, 07:08].

## Motion graphics / animations
* **Native Pop-ins:** Text and stickers enter with single-frame scale snap (100% $\to$ 108% $\to$ 100%) or standard linear pop [07:41].
* **Highlight callouts:** Red animated/static circles drawn around press logos or specific image details [07:37 - 07:39].
* **Timeline Zoom:** Continuous linear scale push-in (100% to 110%) over talking head clips [07:11 - 07:14].

## Workflow & tools

* **Stock Media:** Storyblocks (4K/HD video, royalty-free audio, SFX, templates) [02:16, 02:34].

## Mistakes / warnings
* **Corporate fonts in UGC:** Do not force brand guidelines or polished brand typography onto UGC ads; it destroys authenticity [06:06 - 06:14].
* **Editing for sound-off:** Do not ignore audio design. Designing exclusively for sound-off ignores the 70% of users viewing Reels with audio on [06:38 - 06:49].
* **Static B-roll:** Avoid monotonous, single-angle B-roll. Always demand or source dynamic B-roll from multiple camera angles [03:13 - 03:29].

### 1. Execute Sub-Second Micro-Cut Sequence (0.5s clips)
```bash
# Trim raw clips into 0.5s (15 frames at 30fps) high-pacing beats
ffmpeg -i aura_outfit_01.mp4 -ss 00:00:01.000 -t 00:00:00.500 -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset fast clip1.mp4
ffmpeg -i aura_outfit_02.mp4 -ss 00:00:02.300 -t 00:00:00.500 -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset fast clip2.mp4
ffmpeg -i aura_outfit_03.mp4 -ss 00:00:00.800 -t 00:00:00.500 -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset fast clip3.mp4

# Concatenate micro-cuts
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i micro_cuts.txt -c copy aura_rapid_pacing.mp4
```

### 2. Generate 50/50 Split Screen (Before/After Outfit)
```bash
# Combine two 9:16 vertical videos side-by-side into a single 9:16 frame
ffmpeg -i aura_streetwear_left.mp4 -i aura_streetwear_right.mp4 -filter_complex \
"[0:v]scale=540:1920,crop=540:1920[left]; \
 [1:v]scale=540:1920,crop=540:1920[right]; \
 [left][right]hstack=inputs=2[v]" \
-map "[v]" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 aura_split_screen.mp4
```

### 3. Programmatic J-Cut (Audio leads Video by 0.75s)
```python
# Python Script using MoviePy to create a J-Cut for an Aura Miami ad transition
from moviepy.editor import VideoFileClip, concatenate_videoclips

clip1 = VideoFileClip("aura_founder_talk.mp4")
clip2 = VideoFileClip("aura_broll_fabric.mp4")

j_cut_lead = 0.75

# Extend audio of clip2 backwards over the end of clip1
clip1_visual = clip1.subclip(0, clip1.duration)
clip1_audio = clip1.audio.subclip(0, clip1.duration - j_cut_lead)

# Audio transition start
audio_mix = concatenate_videoclips([clip1_visual]).set_audio(
    concatenate_videoclips([clip1_audio, clip2.audio])
)
```
