## One-line thesis
High-converting video and static ads succeed by leveraging four core psychological principles—**Attention, Curiosity, Emotion, and Connection**—derived from deep, manual audience research rather than generic AI prompts or technical ad account settings.

## Editing techniques

### 1. Kinetic Text Overlay (Hook & Teasers)
* **What it is:** Bold, punchy text overlays placed on-screen word-by-word or line-by-line to lock in scrolling attention.
* **WHEN to use it:** During the first 0–3 seconds of the video, during key transition slides, and on thesis callouts.
* **Exact Parameters:**
  * **Duration:** 0.3s to 0.8s per word group.
  * **Font & Weight:** Heavy Sans-Serif (e.g., Montserrat/Proxima Nova Bold/Black).
  * **Colors:** White (`#FFFFFF`) primary, Bright Yellow (`#FFD700`) highlight for emotional/key emphasis words ("Feel good?", "GOOD ADS", "IN THIS VIDEO...").
  * **Positioning:** Center frame or lower-third center, staying within 16:9 / 9:16 safe margins.

### 2. Rapid Multi-Brand B-Roll Montage (Proof Stacking)
* **What it is:** Fast-cut showcase of recognized brands/ads (AG1, YoungLA, Skims, LMNT, GRTA Golf) to visually reinforce scale and authority.
* **WHEN to use it:** Right after the hook [00:05–00:13] to validate expertise before diving into teaching content.
* **Exact Parameters:**
  * **Cut Frequency:** 0.5s to 1.2s per shot (8 cuts in ~8 seconds).
  * **Scale / Framing:** Center-cropped vertical ad previews over dark background.

### 3. Circular Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Overlay
* **What it is:** Circular face-cam video inset of the founder talking over a live screen-recorded whiteboard (Miro).
* **WHEN to use it:** During technical breakdowns, slide presentations, and whiteboard drawing segments [00:43–00:50, 01:21–03:29].
* **Exact Parameters:**
  * **Size:** ~18–22% of total screen height.
  * **Position:** Bottom-left corner [00:43, 01:21] or top-right corner [02:27, 03:32] to avoid obstructing main text.
  * **Mask Shape:** Circle with subtle border stroke (`#FFFFFF`, 2px).

### 4. Flash / Light-Leak Transition Teaser Cut
* **What it is:** High-contrast white light flash with fast zoom punch and low-pitched hit sound.
* **WHEN to use it:** Rapid forward-looking teaser sequence ("IN THIS VIDEO...") [00:34–00:42].
* **Exact Parameters:**
  * **Duration:** 6–10 frames opacity ramp (0% → 100% → 0%).
  * **Zoom:** 100% to 108% scale punch on cut.

## Hook construction (first 0–3s)

* **Visual:** Close-up frame of founder talking into a broadcast microphone in a modern glass office, winking at the camera.
* **Text:** On-screen kinetic text overlay in two lines:
  * Line 1 (White): `Why do some ads just...`
  * Line 2 (Yellow): `Feel good?`
* **Sound:** Clean voiceover track ("Why do some ads just feel good?") paired with subtle background music starting immediately at the first frame.

---

## Captions & on-screen text

* **Style:** Clean, bold, high-contrast block lettering with selective script accent fonts for background graphics.
* **Words per line:** 1 to 4 words max.
* **Position:** Center screen for hook/teasers; lower-third for lower-third captions; upper/lower margin callouts on whiteboard slides.
* **Color Rules:**
  * **Primary:** White (`#FFFFFF`)
  * **Secondary/Accent:** Yellow (`#FFFF00` / `#FFD700`)
  * **Annotations:** Digital red (`#FF0000`), orange (`#FF9900`), green (`#00FF00`) pen strokes added manually during explanation.

---

## Sound design

* **Voice Track:** Direct-cardioid dynamic mic (SM7B style), processed with compression, EQ, and de-essing to maintain a clear, upfront conversational tone.
* **Music:** Low-fi / modern electronic beat running underneath the commentary.
* **Music Level & Ducking:** 
  * Background music level: ~ -18 dB to -22 dB under dialogue.
  * Ducking depth: -3 dB to -5 dB during active speech; swells to -12 dB during B-roll montages [00:05–00:13, 00:53–01:05].
* **SFX Placement:** Subtle camera shutter / impact hits synced to light-leak cuts at [00:34, 00:38, 00:40].

## Mistakes / warnings

* **Don't rely exclusively on AI (ChatGPT/Midjourney):** AI won't generate niche-specific, culturally resonant humor or high-converting copy angles without manual research.
* **Don't list technical product specs:** Specs (fabric GSM, exact ingredient percentages) don't sell; emotional angles, curiosity, and relatable pain points do.
* **Don't deliver generic visual templates:** Ads must match the exact aesthetic and subculture of the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

## Reproduction recipe with ffmpeg/scripts

Below is a best-effort FFmpeg workflow extrapolation to recreate the **PiP circular overlay on screen recording** and **kinetic text hook**:

### 1. Circular PiP Mask & Composite on Screen Recording

```bash
# 1. Create a circular masked overlay of founder talking head over screen recording
ffmpeg -i screen_recording.mp4 -i founder_talking_head.mp4 -filter_complex \
"[1:v]scale=384:384:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=384:384,format=argb,\
geq=r='r(X,Y)':g='g(X,Y)':b='b(X,Y)':a='if(lte(hypot(X-192,Y-192),190),255,0)'[pip]; \
[0:v][pip]overlay=x=40:y=main_h-overlay_h-40[outv]" \
-map "[outv]" -map 0:a -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -c:a copy output_pip.mp4
```

### 2. Burn-in Hook Kinetic Text Overlay

```bash
# 2. Add bold white/yellow kinetic hook text overlay
ffmpeg -i output_pip.mp4 -vf \
"drawtext=fontfile=/path/to/Montserrat-Black.ttf:text='Why do some ads just...':x=(w-text_w)/2:y=(h-text_h)/2-40:fontsize=64:fontcolor=white:enable='between(t,0,1.5)', \
 drawtext=fontfile=/path/to/Montserrat-Black.ttf:text='Feel good?':x=(w-text_w)/2:y=(h-text_h)/2+40:fontsize=72:fontcolor=yellow:enable='between(t,1.2,3.0)'" \
-c:v libx264 -crf 18 -c:a copy hook_final.mp4
```

## Top 7 takeaways for editing Aura Miami founder and UGC ads

1. **Hit the 0.5s Hook Window:** Drop the audience right into an intriguing question, pattern interrupt, or provocative statement. Use 0.5-second visual cuts right at frame 0.
2. **Apply the 4 Core Pillars:** Every ad cut must serve at least one of these: **Attention** (visual hook), **Curiosity** (unresolved gap), **Emotion** (flex/identity/relatability for streetwear), or **Connection** (niche culture references).
3. **Use Meme & Culture-First Copy Framing:** For streetwear like Aura Miami, avoid boring product feature lists. Frame copy around subculture humor, lifestyle flexing, and peer-to-peer shareability.
4. **Manual Research-Driven Edits:** Incorporate direct customer quotes, Reddit threads, and real ad comments into on-screen graphics to match actual buyer language.
5. **Keep Dynamic Audio Levels:** Maintain voice track dominance while ducking background hip-hop/electronic beats by -18 dB to -22 dB under dialogue, letting music swell during visual transition montages.
6. **Deploy Real-Time Visual Annotations:** Use handwritten circles, underlines, and arrow overlays to force viewer eyes onto product details or key copy callouts.
7. **Maintain Creative Diversity:** Produce multiple creative formats (Curiosity + Emotion for top-of-funnel; Attention + Connection for retargeting) rather than repeating a single ad layout.
