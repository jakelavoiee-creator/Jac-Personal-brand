### Three recurring series
1. **`DAY N/100` (daily, the backbone).** Keep the counter, change what's inside it. Each day is one honest update, observation or small moment from building, not a quote. **The goal is consistency itself: post every day for 100 days** (Reel 2). Every post is a check-in on whether you're still showing up. *Purpose: community (a reason to come back).* Model: [Ryan Trahan](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/ryan-trahan/)'s numbered challenge series.
2. **`nobody posts this part` (2× a week).** The unglamorous moments: a day where nothing happened, the post that flopped, the awkward family question. *Purpose: reach (people share these because they recognize themselves).*
3. **`honest sunday` (weekly).** What I did this week, what worked, what didn't, what's next, with real numbers when you're comfortable. *Purpose: trust (your proof that you're documenting, not performing).*

### Posting cadence (a hypothesis to test)
You've committed to daily posts publicly, and breaking that streak would cost trust. To make daily posting sustainable:
- **3 anchor reels a week:** scripted, to camera, 30–60s, about 45 minutes each including filming. Film all three in one batch on Sunday.
- **4 light reels a week:** a voiceover over footage from your day, 15–25s, about 20 minutes each. Collect the footage during the week (5–10 short clips a day).
- **The safety valve:** on a bad day, post a light reel in 15 minutes. The streak matters more than polish.
- **Review at day 30:** if the light reels match the anchors on shares and follows per 1,000 reach, lean toward the cheaper format.

### The first 10 reels to produce
1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 10 → 5 → 20 → 23 → 9 → 12

**Why this order:** reels 1–2 reset the story (why it changed, and what the 100 days are for), so every later reel has context. Reels 3, 4, 10 and 5 test the three most shareable feelings: the quiet day, feeling behind, and building at night. Reel 20 establishes the trust ritual. Reel 23 is the first opinion, made once you have some credibility.

## Hypotheses we're testing (first 30 days)
- **H1:** Honest journey reels (D/B) get more **shares per 1,000 reach** than your quote reels did. *Needs Insights for your existing quote reels as the baseline.*
- **H2:** To-camera anchor reels convert better than voiceover reels (**follows per 1,000 reach**), because people follow a face.
- **H3:** Voiceover reels reach about as many non-followers as to-camera reels at half the effort.
- **H4:** Making the consistency goal explicit (with day-1/day-100 follower numbers) increases return viewing (rising **follower share of views** on Day N reels).
- **H5:** "Nobody posts this part" reels get the highest share rate of any series.

## Stage gates (defined by evidence, not follower counts)
| Stage | Move on when… |
|---|---|
| 1. Positioning | 3+ formats tested at least 4 times each; voice feels natural on camera; bio test done |
| 2. Content-market fit | One format or series beats **your own median** shares per 1,000 reach by 2× on 3+ posts |
| 3. Scale | That format is repeatable weekly without quality dropping; follows per 1,000 reach holding steady |
| 4. Community and collaborations | Regular comments from repeat names; DMs with people's own stories; collaborations with aligned creators at a similar size |
| 5. Brand | People use your series names back to you; growth isn't dependent on single viral posts |
