## 🌿 Body and nature series

- **Hook:** `when did you last touch the ground?`
1. What is grounding or earthing?
2. Does walking barefoot reduce inflammation?
3. What did grounding studies find about sleep and stress?

### 5. Hugging trees / forest bathing
- **Hook:** `trees are medicine.`
1. What is forest bathing in Japan?
2. Does being in a forest lower stress hormones?
3. What are phytoncides released by trees?
4. Do trees boost your immune system? → natural killer cells (Li, 2007–2010)
- **Ending:** `we pay for supplements. trees give it away.`

### 6. Raw milk ⭐ (most polarizing)
- **Hook:** `why is milk illegal?`
1. Why is raw milk illegal in some states?
2. Is raw milk healthier than pasteurized milk?
3. Does raw milk help with lactose intolerance? → Stanford found no
4. Was bird flu found in raw milk? → yes, 2024
- **Ending:** `natural or dangerous? you decide.`

### 7. Blue light ⭐ (wear your red glasses)
- **Hook:** `your phone is lying to your brain.`
1. What does blue light do to melatonin?
2. Why does looking at your phone at night keep you awake?
3. Do blue light glasses actually work? → Cochrane 2023: probably not for eye strain
4. Why do red lights help you sleep?
- **Ending:** take off the red glasses. `the sun sets for a reason.`

### 8. Music frequencies (440 vs 432 Hz)
- **Hook:** `they changed the frequency.`

2. When did 440 Hz become the standard? → 1939
3. What is 432 Hz music?
4. Is there scientific proof 432 Hz is better? *(honest check: small pilot studies)*
- **Ending:** play 2s at 440, then 2s at 432. `can you feel the difference?`

### 9. Old vs modern architecture

### 10. Jesus ✝ ⭐

## 🧴 Series: "what's touching your skin?" (4 parts)
Same opening each time, `what's touching your skin? part X`; same ending, `you decide. part X+1 tomorrow.`
**Order:** tattoos → perfume → clothes → shoes

### 11. Part 1: Tattoos (keep it curious, not judgmental)
- **Hook:** `where does the ink go?`
1. Does tattoo ink stay in your skin?
2. Does tattoo ink travel to your lymph nodes?
3. Did a 2024 study link tattoos to lymphoma? → +21% risk (an association, not proof)
4. Why did Europe ban some tattoo ink colors? → 2022
- Bonus: Did the FDA find bacteria in tattoo ink?
- **Ending:** `art on the outside. ink on the inside.`

### 12. Part 2: Perfume
- **Hook:** `"fragrance" is a secret.`
1. What does fragrance mean on an ingredient label?
2. Why don't perfume companies have to list their ingredients?
3. What are phthalates in perfume?
4. Are phthalates endocrine disruptors?
- **Ending:** `you spray it on your neck every morning.`

### 13. Part 3: Clothes
- **Hook:** `read your label.`
1. Are there chemicals in new clothes?
2. Why do new clothes have formaldehyde?
3. Were PFAS forever chemicals found in clothing?
4. Do synthetic clothes shed microplastics when you wear them?
- Bonus: Did tests find lead in Shein clothing? (CBC, 2021)
- **Ending:** `cotton was enough for 5,000 years.`

### 14. Part 4: Shoes and socks
- **Hook:** `your shoes are a wall.`
1. Do rubber soles block the body's connection to the ground?
2. Do modern shoes weaken your feet?
3. Does walking in minimalist shoes make your feet stronger? → Ridge et al., 2019
4. How long did humans walk barefoot before shoes?
- **Visual:** shoes off, socks off, step onto grass
- **Ending:** `we covered the one thing touching the earth.`
