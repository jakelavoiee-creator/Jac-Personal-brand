# Task 6: Operating system

## After each post: send me this 48–72 hours after it goes up
Screenshots are fine. Open the reel, tap **View insights**, and send:

1. **Overview:** views, reach (accounts reached), % followers vs non-followers
2. **Engagement:** likes, comments, **shares**, **saves**, **follows** from this reel, profile visits
3. **Retention:** average watch time and the retention graph or skip rate (if Instagram shows it)
4. **Context (one line each):** what you actually posted (the hook line), how long it took to make, and how you felt about it before posting

Without numbers, I can only judge the craft, not the results.

## Weekly (Sunday, before filming)
- Account Insights for the last 7 days: accounts reached, profile visits, follower change
- Any comments or DMs that stood out (screenshots). They become content (concepts 15 and 27).
- What actually happened this week with Aura Miami and your life. That's the raw material for next week's scripts and Honest Sunday.

**What I send back:** a review covering what worked, what didn't, what the evidence suggests, what's still uncertain and what to test next, plus **three concrete actions** and next week's scripts.

## The metrics, with denominators
Everything is calculated per post from **Insights data you send**. Rates are only calculated when the inputs exist.

| Metric | Formula | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|
| Shares per 1K reach | shares ÷ reach × 1,000 | **Shareability:** the main growth signal |
| Saves per 1K reach | saves ÷ reach × 1,000 | Value: worth coming back to |
| Follows per 1K reach | follows ÷ reach × 1,000 | **Conversion:** did the reel make people want more of you |
| Non-follower % | from Insights | Is the reel reaching new people |
| Completion proxy | avg watch time ÷ reel length | Did the content hold attention |
| Profile → follow | follows ÷ profile visits | Is the profile doing its job |

**Baseline:** your median across the first 10 posts. Every post is compared to **your own median**, not industry averages. One viral post proves nothing; a pattern repeated across 3+ posts does.
