## Strengths

2. **A visual system.** The cover template is consistent and readable at thumbnail size.
3. **A healthy follower/following ratio** (1,044 / 131). The audience doesn't look like follow-for-follow, though I can't verify its quality without Insights.

## Weaknesses (judged against the new brand rules you wrote)
1. **The quote reels break your own rule #3.** "You can do it. You will do it. You deserve it." is exactly the "empty motivational quote" format your brief says to avoid. "Start making memories" is the same. "Be who you were meant to be, not told to be" is the strongest of the three, but it's still a quote, not a view into your journey.
2. **The profile describes a different brand.** The bio says *faith · discipline · becoming*. The new identity is *entrepreneurship in the making*. Someone arriving from a reel about building a business would land on a bio about faith and becoming.
3. **The name field is spent on a job title.** "CREATIVE DIRECTOR" tells a visitor what you do for work, not why to follow you. The name field is also searchable, and nobody searches "creative director" looking for encouragement.
4. **There's no face or voice yet.** Text-on-background reels give nobody a reason to feel they know you. Your brief depends on "he hasn't figured it out either, but he's still trying," and that needs you on camera or at least your voice.
5. **The email is cut off** and takes a bio line. Move it to the Contact button.
6. **No highlights or pinned post.** A new visitor has no "start here."

## Missed opportunities
- **The 100-day challenge has no stakes or storyline.** It counts days, but nobody knows what the 100 days are *for*. Give it a goal and it becomes your Journey-in-Public series (Pillar D).
- **Aura Miami barely appears.** Building it is your most real, specific, non-guru material.

## A decision only you can make

My recommendation: keep ✝ as part of who you are (in the name field) and let faith come up naturally when it's true to the story. Don't make it the headline, because the new audience is defined by the entrepreneurial struggle. Tell me if you see it differently, since this changes the scripts.
