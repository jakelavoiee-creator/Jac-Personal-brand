## Script 1: Why I'm changing the challenge
**Concept:** a public reset. You call out your own quote reels and turn the 100 days into a real documentary. This honesty is the brand.
**Audience emotion:** tired of motivational content that feels fake.
**Hook options:**
- A. "For [N] days I posted quotes I'd probably scroll past." ← **recommended:** self-aware, specific, slightly uncomfortable
- B. "I've posted [N] days in a row, and I think I've been doing it wrong."
- C. "I broke my own rule [N] days in a row."

**Script (to camera):**
> For [N] days, I posted quotes I'd probably scroll past.
> *(beat)*
> "You can do it. You deserve it."
> And look, they're not wrong. They're just… not really me.
> I started this because I'm trying to build something. [REAL, one line: e.g. "I'm building a brand called Aura Miami."]
> And honestly? I haven't figured it out yet.
> So the rest of these hundred days are going to be different.
> No more quotes. Just what it actually looks like, the good days and the ones that go nowhere.
> If you're building something too, and you feel like you're the only one who hasn't figured it out… you're not.
> Day [N+1]. Let's actually start.

**Visuals:** to camera, eye level, window light. On "You can do it. You deserve it," cut for 1.5s to a screen recording of your grid of quote reels.
**Editing:** cut every pause; burned-in captions; no music, or a very low ambient bed. End card `DAY [N+1]/100`.
**Ending:** "Let's actually start." It turns the confession into a beginning.
**Caption:** `the first [N] days were practice. the rest is the real thing. what are you building right now?`
**CTA:** the caption question only (Conversation).
**Research inspiration:** GaryVee's "document, don't create"; Trahan-style numbered series ([02-competitor-research.md](02-competitor-research.md)).
**Test hypothesis:** comments and shares per 1,000 reach beat your quote-reel baseline (H1).
**Truth check:** you did post quote reels, and you are building Aura Miami. ✓

## Script 2: What the 100 days are for (pin this)
**The goal (confirmed by Jac):** post every day for 100 days. The challenge is **consistency itself**, so every `DAY N/100` is a check-in on one question: *is he still showing up?*
**Optional stake (recommended, only if you're comfortable):** show your real follower count on day 1 and promise to show it again on day 100, *whatever it is*. That isn't a promise of growth. It's a promise to be honest about the result, and it gives people a reason to come back.
**Audience emotion:** "I've quit things too. I want to see if he makes it."
**Hook options:**
- A. "I'm trying to post every day for 100 days. Here's why that's harder than it sounds." ← **recommended:** clear stakes, no claim of success
- B. "I'm putting this in public so I can't quietly quit."
- C. "Most people quit by day [X]. I'm on day [N]."

**Script (to camera):**
> I'm trying to post every single day for 100 days.
> Not because I think I'll go viral. Because I want to see if I can actually stay consistent at something.
> [REAL, one line, only if true: e.g. "I've started a lot of things and quietly stopped." / "Every time it's been just me holding me accountable, I've let it slide."]
> So I'm doing it in public. If I miss a day, you'll see it.
> Right now I've got [REAL: follower count] followers. On day 100, I'll show you the number again, whatever it is.
> Some of these posts will be good. Some won't. That's kind of the point.
> Day [N] of 100. Come back tomorrow.

**Visuals:** to camera. Write `100` on paper and cross off each day. That paper becomes a recurring object you show briefly in every Honest Sunday.
**Ending:** "Come back tomorrow." Simple, and it's the whole premise.
**Caption:** `100 days. every day. pinning this so I can't pretend I never said it. what's something you've been trying to stay consistent with?`
**Test hypothesis:** this becomes your best follows-per-1,000-reach post. Pinning it lifts profile visit → follow rate (H4).
**Truth check:** the goal is real ✓. The bracketed lines only go in if true.

## Script 3: Nobody posts this part #1
**Concept:** the day where nothing happened. **Only post this on a day that was really like that.**
**Audience emotion:** quiet guilt about unproductive days.
**Hook options:**
- A. "Nobody posts this part." ← **recommended:** short, works as a series title, creates curiosity
- B. "Today I worked on my business for [X] hours, and nothing happened."
- C. "This is what most days actually look like."

**Script (voiceover, slow and close to the mic):**
> Nobody posts this part.
> The day you sit down to work on your thing… and nothing happens.
> No sale. No reply. Nothing you can point to.
> Today was one of those. [REAL, one line: what you actually did, e.g. "I rewrote the same email four times."]
> I used to think days like this meant I was failing.
> I'm starting to think they're just… the part nobody films.
> If today was one of those for you too, it still counts.
> Day [N].

**Visuals:** 6–8 real clips from that day, 2–3 seconds each: laptop screen, the coffee going cold, looking out the window, a walk, the empty inbox. Darken the footage to match your look.
**Editing:** cut on the end of each spoken line, captions in the middle third, quiet piano or room tone. The last clip holds for 2 seconds of silence before `DAY N/100`.
**Ending:** "It still counts." It gives permission without preaching.
**Caption:** `the highlight reel is like 5% of it. the rest looks like this.`
**Test hypothesis:** the highest share rate of the first week (H5). Watch shares per 1,000 reach.

## Script 4: Feeling behind
**Concept:** the comparison wound, told with **your real specifics**.
**Audience emotion:** "everyone my age is ahead of me."
**Hook options:**
- A. "Some people my age are [REAL]. I'm [REAL]." ← **recommended:** the contrast is the hook, and it's yours
- B. "The hardest part of building something isn't the work. It's the comparison."
- C. "If you feel behind, watch this before you open Instagram again."

**Script (to camera):**
> Some people my age are [REAL: e.g. "buying houses"].
> I'm [REAL: e.g. "building a brand that hasn't made money yet"].
> And some days that hits harder than I let on.
> Something I'm starting to realize: I'm comparing my middle to their highlights.
> I don't see their bad days. They don't see mine.
> And "behind" only makes sense if we're all running the same race. We're not.
> I don't believe that every single day. But I'm trying to.
> If you feel behind today, you might just be on a different road.
> Day [N].

**Visuals:** to camera, a tight frame, a quieter setting than usual.
**Editing:** captions on; a hard cut after "We're not," then the next line lands softer. No music for the first 5 seconds.
**Ending:** "a different road." It reframes without promising anything.
**Caption:** `what's the thing that makes you feel behind? for me it's [REAL].`
**Test hypothesis:** highest reach to non-followers in week 1 (it's the most universal feeling).
**Why it isn't generic:** the two bracketed lines are your real life. Without them, don't post it.

## Script 5: For whoever's working on their thing tonight
**Concept:** a reminder for the night shift of ambition. It addresses the viewer, so it doesn't claim anything about you unless you add the optional line.
**Audience emotion:** tired and unseen.
**Hook options:**
- A. "This one's for whoever's working on their thing tonight." ← **recommended:** speaks to one person, and people send it to that person
- B. "It's [TIME] and I'm still working. Maybe you are too." (only if true)
- C. "Nobody claps for this part."

**Script (voiceover):**
> This one's for whoever's working on their thing tonight.
> After the shift. After class. After everyone else logged off.
> Nobody's clapping for this part. Most people don't even know you're doing it.
> [OPTIONAL, REAL: "Right now that's me too. It's [TIME] and I'm still on [TASK]."]
> But I think this is where it actually gets built. Not in the announcement. In the nights nobody sees.
> If you're tired, that's allowed. Close the laptop if you need to.
> Just come back tomorrow.
> Day [N].

**Visuals:** real night footage: a lit screen in a dark room, the city at night, typing hands, the light switch going off at the end.
**Editing:** slow cuts, about 3 seconds each; captions; the final clip is the light switching off, then black, then `DAY N/100`.
**Ending:** "Just come back tomorrow." It's gentle, not hustle culture, and it doesn't tell anyone to grind.
**Caption:** `send this to the person you know is up working on something right now.`
**CTA:** a share prompt, which fits here because the post is literally *for* someone else.
**Test hypothesis:** shares per 1,000 reach for a voiceover reel vs a to-camera reel (H3).
