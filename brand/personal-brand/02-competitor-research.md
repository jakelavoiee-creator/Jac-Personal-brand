## Patterns worth adapting [interpretation]

**1. Ryan Trahan: a challenge with stakes** (confidence: medium-high; documentary series reported by [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/ryan-trahan/))
- **Mechanism:** a numbered series with a clear goal and stakes, so every episode answers "did he make it today?" Viewers come back to see the outcome, not the advice.
- **For you:** the 100 days already has the numbering. It's missing the **goal** and the **stakes**. "Day 23/100" means nothing until people know what the 100 days are for.
- **Avoid:** made-up stunts. The stakes have to be real (for example, a real Aura Miami milestone).

**2. Matt D'Avella: personal experiments** (medium)
- **Mechanism:** "I tried X for N days." The curiosity comes from wanting to see the result, and the honest result, good or bad, is the payoff.
- **For you:** small, real experiments (waking at 5am, no phone until noon, cold-pitching 10 people), reported honestly even when they fail.

**3. Gary Vaynerchuk: "document, don't create"** (high; his public philosophy for years)
- **Mechanism:** recording what's actually happening is easier to keep up and more believable than producing advice.
- **For you:** this is the core of your brief. Take the documenting and leave the volume and the yelling.

**4. Alex Hormozi: one idea per reel, delivered fast** (high; documented in [OpusClip's breakdown](https://opus.pro/blog/create-videos-in-alex-hormozi-style-the-ultimate-guide))
- **Mechanism:** the first sentence is the whole claim, there's no intro, captions are burned in for silent viewing, and he says "how I" rather than "how to."
- **For you:** one idea per reel, the point in the first line, captions always on. Swap his certainty for honest observation: "something I'm starting to notice…" rather than "here's how you…"
- **Avoid:** the authority stance. You haven't earned "here's how," and your brief forbids pretending.

**5. Mel Robbins: reassurance plus one tool** (medium-high)
- **Mechanism:** she names a feeling the viewer has, gives permission ("you're not broken"), then offers one small concrete thing to try. People share it because it says what they can't.
- **For you:** Pillar E. Every reminder needs a specific observation or one small action, never only a quote.

**6. Josh Zilberberg: self-deprecating specifics** (medium; described by [The CJN](https://thecjn.ca/?p=1528) / [HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/instagram/josh.zilberberg/))
- **Mechanism:** the laugh comes from a humiliating, specific detail the viewer recognizes in themselves.
- **For you:** the funny side of building something, like refreshing a dashboard 30 times or telling family "it's going good." Only real moments.

**7. Codie Sanchez: one recurring thesis** (medium)
- **Mechanism:** every post supports one idea (boring businesses), so the name means something.
- **For you:** your thesis is **"the middle part nobody posts."**

**8. Tori Dunlap: name the community** (medium; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tori_Dunlap))
- **Mechanism:** a shared identity ("financial feminists") gives people something to belong to, not just someone to follow.
- **For you:** test a name for your audience after about 30 days. Don't force one on day 1.

**9. Dan Koe and Jay Shetty: voiceover over footage** (medium)
- **Mechanism:** a reflective voice over moving footage. It looks cinematic and you don't need to be on camera.
- **For you:** your easiest format to produce. Use your own footage and your own voice.
- **Avoid:** the polished, all-knowing narrator.

**10. Daily Stoic: daily output** (~13 posts/week, [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/dailystoic/))
- **Mechanism:** a quote works because there's interpretation next to it.
- **For you:** if you post a line on screen, the voiceover or caption has to say why it matters to *you today*.

## What not to copy
- **Iman Gadzhi and Dan Martell:** the luxury and wealth signaling. It directly breaks your rule against faking success.
- **Hormozi and GaryVee:** speaking from the finish line. Every "here's how I made $X" is a guru claim you can't make yet, and shouldn't.
- **Jay Shetty and Yung Pueblo:** the wise-sage persona. You're the person still in the middle of it, not the one on the mountain.
- **Any creator's exact lines.** Adapt the mechanism, never the script.
