# Awakening carousels: competitor research and creative brief

Personal brand page (separate from Aura Miami). Format: Instagram carousels that guide people through the soul awakening process, mapped **year by year**.

> Research date: Oct 7, 2026. Follower counts marked *(approx.)* come from memory, not a live source. Check them in the app before you rely on them. There was no logged-in Instagram access for this pass.

---

## 1. North star

**"The map I wish I had."** A calm, grounded, quietly authoritative guide who has already walked the road and is turning back to light the path.

- Tone: warm, still, honest. No hype and no "high-vibe" glitter.
- Feeling on the last slide: *"I'm not crazy, I'm on schedule."*
- Positioning gap: everyone posts *stages* of awakening. Almost no one posts a **timeline**, meaning what Year 1, Year 3 and Year 7 actually feel like. That timeline is the moat.

## 3. What the winners have in common (the format to adopt)

- **Slide 1 is a promise or a mirror**: "Signs you're in Year 2 of your awakening", "Nobody tells you this about the dark night".
- **One idea per slide**, at most 25 words.
- **7–10 slides.** The second-to-last slide is the emotional payoff, and the last slide is a save or share CTA.
- **Consistent visual identity**: two or three colors and one serif with one sans.
- **Second-person voice** ("you'll notice…") with first-person proof ("in my year 3, I…").

## 4. Content architecture: the Awakening Timeline

Every post belongs to one **Year**, which gives the page a series you can binge.

| Year | Chapter name | What people feel | Example carousel hooks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 0 | **The Crack** | Restless, a sense that "something's off", a trigger event | "7 signs your awakening already started" |
| Year 1 | **The Opening** | Synchronicities, a honeymoon high, consuming everything | "Year 1 of awakening: what's normal" |
| Year 2–3 | **The Dark Night** | Loss of identity and friendships, isolation, purging | "Why your friends feel like strangers now" |
| Year 3–4 | **The Shedding** | Boundaries, leaving jobs or relationships, grief | "What you'll lose in year 3 (and why it's mercy)" |
| Year 4–6 | **Integration** | Grounding, a calm body, spirituality that feels practical | "Awakening isn't a high. It's a nervous system." |
| Year 7+ | **Embodiment / Service** | Quiet knowing, purpose, guiding others | "What awakening looks like 7 years in" |

The source framing is common across the niche: an initial awakening, then the dark night (often **6 months to 3 years**), then integration (often **1–3 years**) ([source](https://mysticryst.com/blogs/the-mystic-journal/how-long-dark-night-soul-last)). Your edge is telling it as **lived years**, with your own story as proof.

**Recurring series:**
1. **"Year X: what's normal"**: the flagship, one per chapter.
2. **"Signs you're in…"**: shareable mirror posts.
3. **"Nobody tells you…"**: myth-busting.
4. **"Practice for this year"**: one exercise, made to be saved.
5. **"My Year X"**: a personal story that builds trust.

## 5. Visual system (draft, to be locked after round 1)

- **Canvas:** 1080×1350 (4:5) with 120px safe margins.
- **Palette:** bone `#F2EDE4` background, ink `#1E1B18` text, and one accent that shifts per Year (for example, dusk violet `#5B4B8A` for the Dark Night and sage `#7A8B6F` for Integration). The Year becomes recognizable by color.
- **Type:** an editorial serif for headlines (Cormorant or Playfair) and a clean sans for body text (Inter). Headlines run 72–96pt and body 36–40pt.
- **Layout:** the headline sits in the top 40%, the body in the middle 40% and the Year tag and handle in the bottom 20%. Slide 1 carries a large Year marker such as "YEAR 2", like a book chapter.
- **Imagery:** minimal. Use textured paper, a single line illustration or grainy film photos of you. Use one image style only.
