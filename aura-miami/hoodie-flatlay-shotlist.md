# Aura Miami: Hoodie Flat Lay Shot List (TikTok Carousel)

**North star:** dark luxury streetwear. Quiet, heavy, expensive. Gold on black, cold cement, one hard window light.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080x1920). Keep the product inside the middle 70% so TikTok's caption and buttons don't cover it.

## Locked product spec (paste into every prompt, never change)

- Jet-black 450gsm heavyweight brushed-fleece hoodie, boxy oversized fit, dropped shoulders, double-layer hood, kangaroo pocket, thick ribbed cuffs and hem
- Flat black drawcords, matte gold metal aglets
- All branding in muted metallic gold `#C9A84C`
- Wordmark reads exactly **AURA MIAMI**, in tall condensed sans-serif capitals (Bebas Neue style)
- Emblem: a fine-line circular sacred-geometry mark (concentric circles + flower of life)
- Tagline: *Presence Over Performance.*
- Surface: raw grey poured concrete with hairline cracks, pores and faint trowel marks
- Light: single hard window light from the upper left, long soft shadow to the lower right

## Workflow

1. Generate **Slide 1 (front)** first. Re-roll until the embroidery spells AURA MIAMI cleanly.
2. Upload the approved front as a **reference image** for slides 2 to 5, so the hoodie stays identical.
3. Lock each approved frame before moving to the next. Change one variable per re-roll.
