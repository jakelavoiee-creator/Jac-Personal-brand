# DON CALMA: Character Bible v1

> **North Star:** *The calmest man in Miami.* A dignified 72-year-old Cuban-Miami gentleman who answers the city's chaos with one slow, perfect cha-cha step and doesn't spill a drop of his cafecito.
> Tone: **deadpan, warm, aspirational.** Viewers should laugh first, then want his peace.

**240,310 followers on only 11 posts**, with no bio and no link.

**What worked: the formula to steal**
1. **The silhouette reads in 0.5 seconds:** blond bob, curled moustache, brown houndstooth suit.
2. **Locked identity:** the same outfit and hair in every clip.
3. **One signature move:** deadpan shadowboxing, a dapper look paired with an aggressive action.
4. **Only the location changes:** Paris landmark after Paris landmark.
5. **Silent:** a trending song over the clip (the breakout was Nelly's "Dilemma"), so it works in every language.
6. **A catchphrase sign-off:** "Oui Madame."
7. **The "is he real?" debate** drove comments.

**Where he's leaking: our opening**
- **Engagement fell 97%** from the first post to the latest. The format has **no story arc**: it's one joke on repeat, and the novelty wore off within about two weeks.
- **The trust was burned:** the $JEANPHIL meme coin rose and then lost 99% in a day, and press coverage called it a possible scam. Hiding that he's AI plus a coin left the audience feeling played.
- **No world:** he has no friends, no rivals, no lore, and nothing to buy that people love.

**Our edge:** we keep the 7 viral mechanics and add **openly disclosed AI, a story arc, a recurring cast, and a real brand (Aura Miami) instead of a coin.**

## 3. Signature move and catchphrase

- **The Move: "El Paso Calma."** A slow, crisp cha-cha step (*one-two, cha-cha-cha*), then a half-turn and a sip of cafecito on the final beat. The coffee never spills.
- **The contrast engine:** the world around him is chaos (a Lambo revving, a spring-break crowd, a hurricane-warning sky, a Brickell traffic jam), and he stays absolute calm.
- **Catchphrase (caption sign-off on every post):** **"Con calma."**
  Caption format: `<4–7 word observation>. Con calma.` For example, "Rent went up again. Con calma."
