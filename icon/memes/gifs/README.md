## Get them into Instagram's GIF search
1. Create a GIPHY account as **thebiggiorgi** (giphy.com → Join). Use the same profile picture and link the IG.
2. Upload all 10 (Upload → choose files). Add **5+ tags each** (table above) and always include `thebiggiorgi` and `giorgi`.
3. Apply for a **Brand or Artist channel** (giphy.com/apply). Only GIFs from approved channels show up in Instagram's comment and Story search. Approval isn't guaranteed and can take days to weeks. Uploading 10+ GIFs and showing an active IG account helps.
4. Until then: post the GIFs as Story posts and send them in DMs (both work without approval).

## Using them in comments (once searchable)
- Reply under big meme pages with the GIF that **fits the post**. A few a day at most, and never the same one copy-pasted. Spam behavior gets accounts limited.
- Reply to comments on your own posts with them. It boosts engagement and teaches followers the GIFs exist.
