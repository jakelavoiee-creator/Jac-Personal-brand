# Jac — Brand North Star

## Mission (one line)
Find the awakened souls and bring them together, first as followers, then as a community that meets in real life.

## Why it exists
Since 2020, people have been splitting into two groups: those still asleep, and a small group (Jac's estimate: 4–5%) who are waking up and feel alone in it. This page is the signal fire for that second group. It shows them: you're not crazy, you're not the only one, and there's a whole crew of us.

What we're taking back: **our time, our freedom, our peace.**
What we're spreading: **love and awareness.** We never shame anyone who's asleep.

## Who we're talking to
The person who started questioning things in the last few years. They connect the dots. They're tired of the noise. They feel like the odd one out at the dinner table. When a video lands, they think "that's me" and hit follow, because a video they *felt* tells them more about the page than one they only watched.

## Jac's story arc (every video is a piece of it)
**From:** stuck, confused, cut off from the light, unhealthy.
**To:** eyes open on real health and real purpose, cutting the noise every day to focus on what matters: relationships, love, purpose, and the knowledge the soul needs to grow.

## Content pillars
1. **The awakening:** what it felt like and what it cost, from 2020 to now.
2. **Connecting the dots:** everyday moments where the system shows itself (food, health, work, media, time). Mentioned and noticed, never lectured.
3. **Cutting the noise:** what Jac dropped and what came back in its place.
4. **Real health:** the body as the soul's home.
5. **Love and relationships:** finding your people and finding love once you've woken up.
6. **The gathering:** calls to the others, and eventually real-life meetups.

## Visual identity: talk to the soul, not the person
**The soul is a light being in a vast universe.** The body is the vessel; the light is who you truly are. Every b-roll shows the soul, never the everyday world it lives in.

- **Show:** light leaving the body, a glowing figure, energy fields, stars and nebulae, cosmic scale, a small light inside vast dark space, auras, light reaching other lights, galaxies forming.
- **Never as b-roll:** literal props of daily life (clocks, traffic, cars, coffee, windows, dinner tables, grocery aisles). The script can mention them; the picture always answers with the soul.
- **Feeling:** waking up as the soul stepping out of the body and seeing itself as light. From that understanding comes higher consciousness and the ease of creating in this world once you know how energy works.
- **Palette:** deep space black and indigo, warm gold and white light, accent red #9B2226 only in the type.
- **B-roll placement:** Jac sends the b-roll. Claude places it by the vibe of each line: the felt line gets the strongest image, and the soul-leaving-the-body shots go on the awakening moments.

## The signature: the same every reel
Only the **script** and the **b-roll** change. Fonts, caption look, sound effects, intro, outro and transitions are one locked system, so people recognise a Jac reel (and Jac's face) in one frame. See `SIGNATURE.md`.

## Tone tokens
- **Feel:** cinematic, intimate, warm. Like a movie scene you feel more than you hear.
- **Words:** first person, concrete moments, honest questions. Deep conversation, never "matrix" slang.
- **Look:** warm grade, film grain, accent red #9B2226. Black-and-white b-roll for heavy or abstract ideas, color for release and love.
- **Sound:** a cinematic score that builds (Interstellar-style organs, slow strings). The edit is cut to the music.
- **Pace:** 15–30 s, a visual or sound spike at least every 2 s, plus one breath before the felt line.

## The test for every video
1. Would an awakened soul feel seen in the first 2 seconds?
2. Is there one concrete moment, not a vague claim?
3. Is there one line the b-roll and the music carry as pure feeling?
4. Does it invite them in without begging?
5. Could only Jac say this?
