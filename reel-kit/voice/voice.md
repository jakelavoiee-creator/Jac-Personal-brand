## Who's speaking

## Rhythm
- Natural speech runs long: chains of "and... and... right?". For reels, cut that into short spoken lines (5–12 words) and keep one longer run as the emotional build.
- He checks in with the listener: **"right?"**, **"okay?"**, **"you know?"**
- He builds in threes: "our time, our freedom, and our peace."
- He saves the swear for one punch at the peak: "our fucking peace." Use at most one per video, and only on the felt line.
