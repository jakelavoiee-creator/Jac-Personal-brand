## Voice (for any copy you write)

- Talking to his bro. Calm, direct, curious, intelligent, occasionally funny.
- First-person discovery framing: "What I realized…", "What changed for me…", "After years of consuming this stuff…"
- Elevated vocabulary, plain meaning. One idea per sentence.
- Hooks challenge an assumption with precision ("You don't realize how much of your personality was installed.").
- Write toward the reader's "Wait… that's actually true… I've never looked at it like that."

## Hard guardrails

- **Authenticity:** never invent stories, trauma, achievements, or before/after narratives for Jac. If a personal detail is needed and not in the spec, ask.
- **Credibility:** label frameworks as frameworks ("a lens I find useful"). Present a scientific claim as fact only when it is actually supported. 3D→4D→5D is a perspective, not a measurement.
- **Spirituality:** exploratory, never dogmatic.
- **Positioning:** perspective-sharer in the process, never a guru at the finish line. Content gives value first; the product is a natural extension, not the point of every post.
- **Influencers** (Sinek, Howes, Greene, Dispenza, Tolle, Ghiyam, Shi Heng Yi, Huberman, Brecka): synthesize into Jac's perspective; never reproduce their content.

## Building the product

- Business spec, not tech spec: choose framework, DB, auth, payments, hosting freely — favor the simplest stack that ships a premium, mobile-first experience.
- It is a *personal operating system for an identity reset*, not a course platform. The user always knows where they are, what to do now, why, what to reflect on, and what's next.
- Design language: cinematic, minimal, dark, editorial. Black canvas, white type, blue as the single accent. Generous negative space.
- Optimize for **Attention + Trust + Transformation**, and measure transformation (completion, monthly progression, testimonials) alongside revenue.
